The **Musaned** platform transformed domestic worker recruitment in Saudi Arabia from a paper track through offices to a unified electronic system under Ministry of Human Resources supervision. This guide covers the conditions, fees, and rights as applied in 2026.

## What Musaned Is and How It Works

[Musaned](https://musaned.com.sa) is a platform under the [Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa), managing the full lifecycle of a domestic worker: from recruitment to contract to renewal to transfer to exit.

Musaned differs from Qiwa (which manages private-sector labor contracts). Musaned is dedicated **exclusively to domestic labor** in the types listed in the Saudi Domestic Labor Regulation.

---

## Covered Domestic Worker Types

The Domestic Labor Regulation covers nine official specializations:

| Occupation | Description |
|---|---|
| **Housemaid** | General cleaning and household tasks |
| **Cook** | Preparing family meals |
| **Nanny** | Care of children under 6 |
| **Private driver** | Driving the family car |
| **Patient companion** | Care of the sick or elderly |
| **Gardener** | Garden care |
| **House guard** | Night-time home guarding |
| **Private farmer** | Cultivating private land (rare) |
| **Private livestock herder** | Raising private livestock |

Most requested: housemaid, private driver, nanny.

---

## Employer (Sponsor) Conditions

The system requires several conditions in the sponsor:

- **Age**: 21 or older
- **Fixed monthly income**: at least 7,000 SAR (proven via bank statement or employer letter)
- **Adequate housing**: requirements vary by number of workers and housing type
- **Nationality**: Saudis without additional condition; residents must have at least two years of iqama history
- **Record**: no active violations recorded against a previous recruitment

**Maximum domestic workers count**: depends on family composition (3 for a single person, 4 for a small family, 6 for a large family), and may reach 8 for special cases (patients, people with disabilities).

## Fees and Costs

Total cost breaks down into several items:

| Item | Approximate value |
|---|---|
| **Musaned platform fee** | 100 SAR |
| **Recruitment office fee** | 3,000–8,000 SAR (by nationality) |
| **Medical exam in source country** | Borne by source-country office |
| **Airfare** | 1,000–3,000 SAR by destination |
| **Health insurance** | Legally required, 700–1,200 SAR annually |
| **Visa fees** | 200 SAR |
| **Approximate total** | **12,000–25,000 SAR** |

Most are paid via Musaned through SADAD, except the recruitment office fee paid directly to the office.

## Musaned Recruitment Steps

Full electronic procedure:

1. **Register on Musaned** with a Nafath account
2. **Eligibility check**: the system automatically verifies your income, age, and record
3. **Choose occupation and nationality** requested
4. **Choose recruitment office**: from an approved list on Musaned
5. **Complete the contract**: standard bilateral contract (electronically signed)
6. **Pay fees** via SADAD
7. **Office follow-up**: file passes medical exam, visa, then travel
8. **Arrival**: typically 45–90 days from application date

## Rights and Obligations — Standard Contract

The Saudi Domestic Labor Regulation mandates a standard contract between the sponsor and the worker, including:

### Worker's Rights

- **Monthly wage** paid on time via bank transfer (Domestic Wage Protection System)
- **Weekly rest day** at least once
- **Working hours** not exceeding 10 hours per day with breaks
- **Annual leave** 30 days after two continuous years
- **Return ticket** at contract end
- **Full healthcare** at the sponsor's expense
- **Work-accident insurance** mandatory

### Worker's Obligations

- Perform agreed work honestly and diligently
- Preserve the sponsor's property
- Adhere to appropriate conduct
- Not work for anyone other than the sponsor without official approval

### Contract Termination Cases

- Natural end of contract term (1, 2, or 3 years)
- Mutual consent before end
- Grave breach by either party (per a statutory list)
- Worker transfer to another employer with current employer's approval

## Domestic Wage Protection System

Since 2021, Saudi Arabia has extended the Wage Protection System to domestic workers:

- **Wage transferred monthly** to a bank account in the worker's name
- **Regularity monitored** via Musaned
- **3 consecutive months of delay** opens transfer without sponsor consent
- **Bank account** opened for every worker via an approved bank before contract start

This protects the worker from illegal wage withholding and protects the sponsor from false non-payment claims.

## Additional Questions

**Can I recruit a driver without a car?**
Yes. The regulation does not require vehicle ownership, but the sponsor remains bound by wage and rights. Owning a car logically justifies recruiting a driver.

**How do I recruit a specific worker I know personally?**
Via "Named Recruitment", enter the worker's data (name, nationality, passport number) in Musaned, coordinated with a recruitment office in their country. Fees are similar to regular recruitment.

**What if a worker is dismissed for a legitimate reason after arrival?**
If you prove a statutory reason (theft, damage, inability to work), the recruitment office is obligated to refund part of the fees or replace the worker within a warranty period (usually 90 days from arrival) per the office contract.

**Can a violating worker be deported automatically?**
No. Even workers who commit violations are not deported automatically, but via a formal complaint in Musaned, then transferred to the competent authorities to review and decide.
