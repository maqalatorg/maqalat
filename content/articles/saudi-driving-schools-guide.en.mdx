## Complete guide to Saudi driving schools

Choosing a driving school is an important decision — it affects **cost, time, quality of training**, and sometimes your ability to pass the exam first try. This guide covers what you need to know before enrolling.

## Best-known driving schools

### National networks

**Dallini**
- The largest and most widespread in Saudi Arabia.
- Branches in Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, and many other cities.
- Training for cars, motorcycles, and heavy transport.
- Women's sections at every branch.

**Saei**
- Focus on Riyadh and Dammam.
- Competitive pricing.
- Flexible schedules.

### University-affiliated schools

**Princess Nourah University Driving School (Riyadh)**
- The most well-known for women since 2018.
- Attached to the university, fully women-only environment.
- Spacious training yards.
- High demand — book early.

**King Abdulaziz University Driving School (Jeddah)**
- Strong option in the Western Region.
- University teaching environment.
- Men and women (separate schedules).

**IAU (Imam Abdulrahman) Driving School (Dammam)**
- The best-known in the Eastern Region.
- Modern facilities.

### Regional schools

- **Saudi Driving School (Jeddah)**: one of the oldest in the Western Region.
- **Eastern Driving School (Dammam)**: convenient for Aramco and industrial area workers.
- **Qassim (Buraidah)**, **Taif**, **Aseer (Abha)**, and others in each region.

The full official up-to-date list is on [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/) → Traffic → Driving Schools.

## Real prices — what ads don't tell you

Advertised prices are typically **lower than actual cost**. Why?

| Component | Approximate cost |
|---|---|
| Base registration fee | 500-1,000 SAR |
| Theory-training hours | 200-500 SAR |
| Practical hours (20-30 for a beginner) | 1,500-2,500 SAR |
| Traffic exam fees | 400-600 SAR |
| Medical exam | 100-200 SAR |
| Licence issuance fee (5 or 10 years) | 400-600 SAR |
| **Approximate total** | **3,100-5,400 SAR** |

**Factors that raise cost**:
- Need for extra hours if you fail the exam.
- Choosing a premium school with newer equipment.
- Training in evening or weekend slots (pricier).

**Factors that lower cost**:
- Prior experience → fewer hours needed.
- Foreign licence → shortened path.
- Seasonal offers (typically at the start of the school semester).

## How to choose your school

### 1) Proximity to home

Every training hour you commute — proximity saves effort and time. **Prioritise schools within 5-10 km**.

### 2) All-in pricing (not partial)

Request a **detailed invoice** before enrolling. Some schools advertise 2,000 SAR then add fees on everything later. An all-in invoice protects from surprises.

### 3) Vehicle quality

**Modern cars** make training easier (light steering, advanced automatic transmission). An old car may make you fear driving.

### 4) Instructors

**Women often prefer women instructors**. Ask about the instructor mix, specialties, and years of experience. A good instructor beats a luxury car.

### 5) School reputation

- **Ask past trainees**: real experiences beat ads.
- **Google and X reviews**: give an indicator.
- **Pass rate on traffic exams**: some schools publish it — best to ask.

### 6) Schedule flexibility

- **Morning, evening, or weekend?**
- **Class reschedule policy**: some schools are strict (miss = forfeit), some flexible.

### 7) Offers and packages

- **Full package upfront**: usually cheaper.
- **Instalment payment**: some schools allow it.
- **Student/employee discounts**: ask.

## Complete training stages

### Stage 1: Registration and medical exam

- Register at the school with your ID.
- Initial medical exam (detailed in our [medical exam article](/en/driving-license-medical-exam-guide)).
- Receive the theory-training booklet.

### Stage 2: Theory training

- 5-10 hours of lectures.
- Content: traffic rules, signs, safety, driving etiquette.
- **Theory exam** at the end of this stage (multiple choice).
- **Passing it is required to move to practical**.

### Stage 3: In-yard practical training

- 5-10 hours inside the school yard.
- Learn: moving off, parallel parking, angle parking, reversing.
- Safe environment, no streets.

### Stage 4: Street practical training

- 10-20 hours on real streets.
- Start with light streets, then busy.
- Learn: turns, traffic lights, highways, night conditions.

### Stage 5: Official traffic exam

- The exam at approved traffic centres.
- The examiner is from Traffic (not your school).
- Route is preset.
- Pass = your licence.

## Mistakes that waste your budget

- **Refusing extra lessons when needed**: better than failing the exam (you pay the exam fee again).
- **Fully relying on the school**: get extra lessons with a relative/friend (after trainee permit — varies by region).
- **Ignoring theory training**: passing is required, and failure delays your path.
- **Blindly picking the cheapest**: a bad school costs more (re-exams, extra hours).

## Tips for the practical exam

- **Sleep well the night before**.
- **Arrive early**: stress is the worst enemy.
- **Breathe slowly before entering**.
- **Follow the examiner's instructions literally**: don't try to show off.
- **Stop fully at red lights** (common mistake).
- **Buckle up and adjust the mirror before moving off**.
- **Don't speed** even on empty roads.
