King Abdulaziz University in Jeddah is **the largest by enrollment in the kingdom**: 117,000 students, more than double King Saud University, and over ten times KFUPM. But size is not KAU's only distinction، it is **the only Saudi university** offering marine science, meteorology, and astronomy ([Wikipedia، KAU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdulaziz_University)).

Globally, KAU has moved from #149 (2025) to #163 (2026) to #200 (2027) in QS. A visible decline attributed to shifting ranking methodology and intensifying global competition، but **it remains among the top 200 universities worldwide** ([SPA، 22 Saudi Universities in QS 2027](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2618524)).

## Colleges and Programs

KAU has **24 colleges** (15 on the main campus + 9 off-campus / Rabigh branch):

### Health Sector
- Medicine، one of the most established medical faculties in the region
- Dentistry
- Pharmacy
- Applied Medical Sciences
- Nursing
- Health Sciences

### Engineering Sector
The College of Engineering (**ABET accredited**) includes:
- Electrical Engineering
- Computer Engineering
- Biomedical Engineering
- Nuclear Engineering، rare in the region
- Aeronautical Engineering
- Chemical and Materials Engineering
- Civil Engineering
- Industrial Engineering
- Mining Engineering
- Production and Mechanical Design Engineering
- Thermal Engineering and Desalination Technologies، unique in KSA

### Science Sector (Some Unique in KSA)
- Sciences، physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics
- **Marine Sciences**، **only in Saudi Arabia**
- **Meteorology and Environment**، **only in Saudi Arabia**
- **Astronomy**، **only in Saudi Arabia**

### Business and Law Sector
- **Economics and Administration** (1969، first faculty at the university, **AACSB accredited**)، accounting, management, marketing, finance, economics
- Law (2012)

### Humanities and Design Sector
- Arts and Humanities، languages, history, geography, Islamic studies
- Communication and Media
- Computing and Information Technology
- Design
- Architecture and Planning
- Tourism
- **Maritime Studies**، unique in Saudi Arabia at this depth (navigation, marine engineering, port studies)

### Rabigh Branch
Effectively an independent university within the same system, includes:
- Engineering
- Business
- Sciences
- Arts

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's + PhD for most programs.

## Where Graduates Work

KAU graduates distribute heavily across the Saudi west (Jeddah, Makkah, Taif, Yanbu) with national reach:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Strong secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Medicine** | King Abdulaziz University Hospital + major Jeddah hospitals | KFSH Jeddah, National Guard hospitals, private sector |
| **Dentistry** | Private sector | Jeddah public hospitals, Ministry of Health |
| **Pharmacy** | Private pharmacy chains | Pharmaceutical companies, hospitals, SFDA |
| **Nuclear Engineering** | King Abdulaziz City for Science and Technology (KACST) | Atomic Energy Authority, Aramco |
| **Chemical and Thermal Engineering** | Aramco (Jeddah refinery), SABIC, desalination companies | Red Sea projects, Yanbu Industrial City |
| **Aeronautical Engineering** | Saudia Airlines, SAMI, GACA | Airport maintenance companies |
| **Marine Science / Maritime Studies** | Red Sea Authority, Jeddah Islamic Port | Shipping companies, Aramco marine branch, environmental research |
| **Meteorology** | General Authority of Meteorology and Environmental Protection | GACA, Aramco (risk analysis) |
| **Astronomy** | International Astronomical Center, KACST | Academic teaching, Islamic astronomical endowments |
| **Business and Finance** | Saudi banks | Listed Jeddah companies, Capital Market Authority, consulting |
| **Computer Science** | STC, Jeddah tech companies | Mega-projects, banks, startups |
| **Media and Communication** | MBC, Rotana, Saudi channels | Advertising agencies, digital (SRMG, Thmanyah) |

**Strategic note**: A KAU graduate specialising in marine science, meteorology, or astronomy faces **less competition** thanks to the scarcity of comparable Saudi programs, with growing demand from Vision projects (Red Sea, AMAALA, Qiddiya) and the needs of aviation and astronomy authorities.

## What Makes KAU Different

1. **Exclusive programs**، marine science, meteorology, astronomy, maritime studies, thermal desalination engineering. No Saudi alternatives.
2. **Largest size**، 117,000 students = broader options, wider social exposure, massive alumni network.
3. **Early international accreditation**، ABET (Engineering) since 2003, AACSB (Business) since 2015. Verifiable quality signal.
4. **Jeddah location**، gateway to the two holy cities, near Red Sea projects, AMAALA, NEOM (reasonable distance).
5. **Rabigh branch**، practically a second university within the same institution, offering geographic flexibility.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**KAU fits you if:**
- You want a rare specialty (marine science, meteorology, astronomy, nuclear or thermal engineering)
- You prefer the Jeddah / western Saudi environment over Riyadh or the Eastern Province
- You want a comprehensive university with many options for switching disciplines
- Medicine at a large teaching hospital is your goal

**KAU may not fit you if:**
- Global ranking is your priority (KFUPM #63 or KSU #107 rank higher)
- You want a small university (KAU is very large، you may feel like a number)
- Your discipline is one where another Saudi university is specifically stronger (e.g., KFUPM for petroleum and computing)

## Strong Alternatives

- **KFUPM**، for engineering, computing, and petroleum (higher ranked)
- **KSU**، comprehensive alternative in Riyadh with higher rank
- **KAUST**، graduate-only research in English on the Red Sea (north of Jeddah)
- **Umm Al-Qura University**، for Sharia, humanities, and sciences in Makkah
- **Taif University**، regional alternative in Taif
