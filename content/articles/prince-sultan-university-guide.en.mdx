Prince Sultan University (PSU) is the **first accredited private university** in Saudi Arabia ([PSU، About](https://www.psu.edu.sa/en/about-us)). Founded in 1999 as a private college and elevated to university status in 2003. Based in Riyadh, English-medium, and holds both **AACSB** (Business) and **ABET** (Engineering) accreditations، a rare combination among Saudi universities.

The campus is gender-segregated (male and female in adjacent buildings), yet academic standards and accreditations are unified. PSU is genuinely a **market-oriented university**: most programs are designed in consultation with industry, and many faculty members bring executive experience.

**Notable achievement**: PSU's Business College was among the first three business schools in Saudi Arabia to earn AACSB accreditation (KSU, Alfaisal, PSU)، a standard held by only ≤6% of business schools globally.

## Colleges and Programs

### 1. College of Business Administration

- BBA، tracks in Finance, Marketing, Accounting, General Management, Banking
- BS Economics
- **MBA**، the flagship graduate program
- MS Forensic Accounting، regionally rare
- Accreditation: AACSB

### 2. College of Engineering

- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Civil & Environmental Engineering
- Industrial & Systems Engineering
- Accreditation: ABET

### 3. College of Computer & Information Sciences (CCIS)

- Computer Science
- Software Engineering
- Information Systems
- Cybersecurity
- **MS Data Science & AI**، high market demand
- Accreditation: ABET

### 4. College of Law

- LLB
- **LLM in International Commercial Law**، first private LLM program in the Kingdom
- Among the first private law colleges in Saudi Arabia, with focus on commercial and international law

### 5. College of Architecture & Design

- Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)
- Interior Design
- Graphic Design

### 6. College of Humanities & Sciences

- Translation, Applied Linguistics
- Psychology
- Mathematics, Physics (service courses for Engineering and Computing)

## Career Outcomes، Where Alumni Work

PSU graduates are **built for the Saudi private sector**:

| Field | Typical employers |
|---|---|
| **Business** | Saudi banks (Al Rajhi, SNB, Riyad Bank, Bank AlBilad), insurers, Big 4 consulting, large family businesses, PIF, Aramco (finance and strategy divisions) |
| **Engineering** | Aramco, SABIC, major contractors (Bin Laden, Saudi Bin Laden, Al Rajhi Investment), Ministry of Municipal Affairs |
| **Computing** | STC, Mobily, Zain, tech startups (Salla, Ather, Tamara), cybersecurity firms, bank tech divisions |

**Notable point**: Global consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG, Bain) accept PSU applications for direct campus recruiting، a channel available at only a handful of Saudi universities.

## What Makes PSU Different

1. **Historical primacy**، the first private university in Saudi Arabia, with a reputation built over a quarter century.
2. **Early AACSB accreditation**، the Business College has held accreditation for years, with ongoing renewals (indicating a stable, verified standard).
3. **Strong industry ties**، many Business and Engineering faculty come from executive roles, and college advisory boards include senior executives from major companies.
4. **Riyadh location**، proximity to bank and corporate headquarters enables meaningful summer internships without relocation.
5. **Medium size**، larger than Alfaisal, smaller than public universities, giving reasonable faculty-to-student ratios and interactive classes.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**PSU suits you if:**
- Your ambition is business, banking, consulting, or tech within the Saudi market
- You want internationally recognized accreditation (AACSB/ABET) that transfers well to graduate study abroad
- Your English is solid (foundation year is available to elevate proficiency)
- You prefer a market-oriented academic environment over pure academic research
- Your family can accommodate private-university tuition

**It may not suit you if:**
- You want to study Medicine or Dentistry (not offered at PSU)
- You prefer a research-heavy theoretical academic environment (KAUST or KFUPM are closer)
- You want a coeducational campus (PSU is segregated)
- Tuition is a significant financial constraint

## Strong Alternatives

- **Alfaisal University**، private, Riyadh, offers Medicine, Engineering, Business, coeducational
- **Alyamamah University**، private, Riyadh, typically lower tuition, strong in Business and Law
- **King Saud University**، public, Riyadh, tuition-free for Saudis
- **KFUPM (King Fahd Petroleum & Minerals)**، public, Dhahran, strongest in Engineering and Computing
