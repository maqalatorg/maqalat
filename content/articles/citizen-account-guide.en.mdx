The Citizen Account (Hisab Almowaten) is a direct cash-support programme for Saudi middle and low-income households, launched in 2017 alongside the fuel-price reform to protect purchasing power. It runs under the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) through the official portal at [ca.gov.sa](https://ca.gov.sa/).

## Who qualifies?

Eligibility is evaluated on three joint criteria:

| Criterion | Detail |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Saudi citizen (or those in the same category, such as certain children of Saudi mothers, per specific rules) |
| Residency | Inside the Kingdom, without exceeding the allowed monthly time abroad |
| Income & assets | Total household income and assets within the officially published caps |

**Divorced women, widows, and adult women living independently** are treated as heads of their own household and can register in their own name without a guardian.

**Dependents** counted in the household: spouse, children under 18, children over 18 if they are students or have disabilities, and in some cases dependent parents.

## How the benefit is calculated

The general formula sums three components:

1. **Base entitlement for the head of household** — fixed amount.
2. **Per adult dependent** — a fixed amount, lower than the head's.
3. **Per child** — a lower amount, capped by a maximum number of children.

The provisional total is then compared to the **independence threshold**, the income level at which a household is considered economically independent. When actual income exceeds this threshold, the benefit tapers on a gradual curve, reaching zero at the "target point." This design gives more support to lower-income households, decreasing as income rises.

> **Note**: Actual amounts are updated annually by decision of the Ministry of Finance. This year's figures may differ from last year's, so check ca.gov.sa before calculating — the official estimator on the portal is always up to date.

## Registration steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [ca.gov.sa](https://ca.gov.sa/) and choose "New Registration" |
| 2 | Enter your ID number, Hijri date of birth, and contact details |
| 3 | Activate with the OTP sent to your Absher-registered mobile |
| 4 | Fill in head-of-household and dependent details (many fields pull from Absher automatically) |
| 5 | Attach income proof: last 3 months' bank statement, salary certificate, commercial registration if any |
| 6 | Add housing status (owner, tenant, family home) |
| 7 | Review the summary and submit |

The eligibility decision usually takes 7 to 30 working days. The result arrives on the portal and by SMS.

## Monthly payment schedule

Payment is officially made on the **10th of each Gregorian month**. If the 10th falls on a Friday or public holiday, payment is brought forward to the **previous working day**. Funds are deposited into the applicant's registered bank account under the same name — third-party accounts are not accepted.

## Common mistakes that delay approval

- **Bank account in a different name**: must match the applicant's full four-part name.
- **Declared income doesn't match bank**: the system compares your declaration to automatically pulled bank statements.
- **Dependents not updated in Absher**: if you have a newborn or change in marital status, update Absher first, then the Citizen Account.
- **Expired national address**: verify your address is current at [Saudi Post (SPL Online)](https://splonline.com.sa/).

## Appealing a rejection

If your application is rejected, the reason appears on your portal dashboard. You can appeal within **60 days** via the "Objection" service in the portal, uploading supporting documents. The appeal decision is typically issued within 30 working days.

## How Citizen Account relates to other support programmes

- **Sanad (Developed Social Security)**: fully separate — one does not require the other, but they are assessed on different criteria.
- **Electricity subsidy for low-income households**: was linked to the Citizen Account previously, then separated under the 2024 reforms.
- **Sakani (housing)**: fully independent — eligibility via [sakani.sa](https://sakani.sa/).

## History of the Citizen Account Program

The Citizen Account was formally launched in **December 2017** after the government announced fuel and electricity price increases as part of broader fiscal reforms. The core idea: instead of untargeted subsidies flowing to every consumer regardless of income, redirect support to families that actually need it based on their income. Official source: [Ministry of Finance budget documents](https://www.mof.gov.sa/) and the [founding Council of Ministers decree](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/).

Since launch, the program has undergone several major revisions:

| Year | Update |
|---|---|
| 2017 | Initial launch with high allocations to offset fuel price rises |
| 2019 | Introduction of the "self-sufficiency threshold" tied to actual income |
| 2020 | COVID adjustments — accelerated disbursement and temporary benefit increases |
| 2023 | Integration of Social Insurance data for income verification |
| 2024 | Electricity subsidy separated from Citizen Account into a standalone program |

## Who Receives the Highest Benefit?

Benefits aren't fixed across families — the program is designed to give **more to those who need more**. Factors that raise your monthly amount:

1. **Number of children under guardianship**: each child adds a specific amount up to a ceiling.
2. **Single-provider household**: a widow or divorced mother supporting children — receives preferential treatment.
3. **Monthly income less than half the self-sufficiency threshold**: full benefits without deduction.
4. **Presence of disabled individuals in the family**: additional benefit if officially registered with the [King Salman Center for Disability Research](https://www.moh.gov.sa/) or an approved center.

Factors that reduce your amount:
1. Monthly income close to the target-point threshold.
2. Assets (multiple properties, luxury vehicles).
3. A family member employed at a high salary.

The official estimation calculator is at [ca.gov.sa](https://ca.gov.sa/) and shows an estimate of your benefit before final registration, non-committally.

## Citizen Account and Your Financial Plan

Depending on the program monthly? Consider these points before building your budget:

- **The benefit is not fixed**: reviewed periodically based on income updates. Don't treat it as steady income.
- **The registered bank matters**: transfer delays sometimes occur due to bank-side issues. Choose a large trusted bank.
- **Update your data as soon as it changes**: birth, divorce, new job, leaving a job. Concealment = retroactive disqualification.
- **The right to appeal a rejection is statutory**: don't lose hope from the first attempt — many appeals succeed with complete documentation.
