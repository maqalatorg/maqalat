The Saudi passport is one of the strongest in the Arab world, but major Western countries (Europe, US, UK, Canada, Australia) still require a prior visa. This guide covers each of their requirements for Saudi applicants, with best-practice tips for first-time approval.

**Before starting**: every visa requires a **passport valid for more than 6 months** from date of entry, and a photo per ICAO specs (white background, no smile, specific size).

## Schengen visa (29 European countries)

**Countries covered**: Germany, France, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Greece, Portugal, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Poland, Czechia, and others. **Not Schengen**: UK, Ireland, Russia, Romania (joins 2026), Bulgaria (joins 2026).

**Basic rule**: apply to the embassy of the country you will:
- **enter first**, or
- **stay longest in**.

**Required documents**:

| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| Completed application form | From embassy site or VFS |
| Passport | + old passports if any |
| Two photos | ICAO, white background |
| Confirmed flight booking | Round trip (not a final ticket, an option-hold booking) |
| Hotel booking covering all trip nights | Refundable hold |
| Bank statement 3-6 months | Should show a reasonable balance (~500 SAR × number of travel days minimum) |
| Employer letter | Stating salary and leave dates |
| Travel insurance covering min 30,000 EUR | Mandatory for Schengen |
| Commercial registration | For business owners |
| Ownership proof | Property deed or car (strengthens the application) |

**Fee**: 90 EUR for adults + ~50-100 SAR VFS center fee.

**Processing time**: 15 working days officially, up to 45 days in peak season.

**Golden tip**: attach a **cover letter** explaining who you are, why you're travelling, where you'll stay, and how you'll return. A one-page rational letter meaningfully raises approval odds.

## US visa (B1/B2)

**Most common type**: B1 (business visit) / B2 (tourism or medical), typically issued together as one visa.

**Duration**: 10 years (multiple-entry) for Saudis — longer than 90% of world nationalities.

**Steps**:

1. **Fill DS-160** on [ceac.state.gov](https://ceac.state.gov/) — takes 1-2 hours.
2. **Pay visa fee**: ~185 USD (~700 SAR). Payment method varies by bank — check embassy instructions.
3. **Book interview**: at Riyadh embassy or Jeddah / Dhahran consulate. Appointments vary — may be 2-6 months out.
4. **Biometrics**: before or on the interview day.
5. **The interview**: short questions (2-5 minutes). The officer decides on the spot.
6. **Visa issuance**: 5-10 working days after approval.

**Documents at the interview**:
- Passport.
- DS-160 confirmation page.
- Photo (per US specs).
- Fee payment receipt.
- Proof of Saudi ties: employment contract, property deed, family record.

**Common refusal reasons**: failing to prove intent to return (young applicant with no job or family), inconsistent info, weak travel history.

## UK visa (Standard Visitor)

**Duration**: 6 months typically, or 2, 5, 10 years for multi-entry visitor visas.

**Steps**:

1. **Apply online** at [gov.uk/apply-uk-visa](https://www.gov.uk/apply-uk-visa).
2. **Pay fees**: ~115 GBP (~560 SAR) for a short visitor.
3. **Pay Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS)**: ~300 GBP annually if you'll stay more than 6 months (not required for short visits).
4. **Book VFS appointment** in Riyadh or Jeddah for biometrics.
5. **Upload documents** online before the appointment.

**Documents**:
- Passport.
- Bank statement 6 months.
- Employer letter.
- Travel plans (hotel, flight).
- Proof of Saudi ties.

**Processing time**: 3-4 weeks.

## Canada visa (Visitor Visa TRV)

**Duration**: usually 10 years multi-entry (bounded by passport validity).

**Steps**:

1. Create an account at [ircc.canada.ca](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html).
2. Complete form IMM 5257 and upload documents.
3. Pay fees: 100 CAD (~275 SAR) + 85 CAD biometrics.
4. Book a VFS biometrics appointment.
5. Wait for the decision.

**Processing time**: 1-2 months typically.

## Australia visa (Visitor 600)

**Duration**: 3-12 months per subclass, usually valid one year.

**Steps**:
1. Apply online at [immi.homeaffairs.gov.au](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/).
2. Fees: 190 AUD (~460 SAR).
3. No interview typically — decision on documents.
4. Processing: 2-6 weeks.

## General tips for Saudi applicants

- **Start at least two months before travel** — especially for the US and UK.
- **Never lie on the visa form**: any false detail is caught in later checks.
- **Bank balance matters**: normal account activity without sudden inflows in the week before applying.
- **Don't book a non-refundable flight before visa issuance**: especially for the US and UK.
- **If rejected, don't reapply immediately**: understand the reason, address it, then reapply.
- **Countries that rejected you appear in other countries' applications**: be honest about any prior refusal.
