Noor is the official administrative platform of Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education, managing the entire student lifecycle from kindergarten through high school: registration, admissions, attendance, results, transfers, and the permanent academic file. Access at [noor.moe.gov.sa](https://noor.moe.gov.sa/). Every guardian in Saudi Arabia needs a Noor account — you cannot register your child in primary school without one.

## Account types on Noor

| Account | Privileges |
|---|---|
| Guardian | Enrol children, view results, request transfer, absence alerts |
| Student (middle & high school) | View grades, schedule, academic record |
| Teacher | Enter grades, log absence, follow up on students |
| Principal | Manage all school data, teachers, and students |
| Education supervisor | Follow multiple schools within their supervision area |

## New guardian registration

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [noor.moe.gov.sa](https://noor.moe.gov.sa/) |
| 2 | On the home page choose "New Guardian Registration" |
| 3 | Enter ID number, Hijri date of birth, and relationship |
| 4 | Enter your Absher-registered mobile (system verifies) |
| 5 | Receive username and activation code by SMS |
| 6 | Sign in, change password, and update your email |

**Note**: don't use someone else's username or password — the system tracks sessions. Mistakes are logged on the guardian's file and affect later transfer requests.

## Enrolling children in first grade primary

This is the most-searched Noor query each year. Registration opens seasonally (typically May-July) — outside that window you won't see the option.

**Age criteria**: accepted from age 6 years 3 months at start of academic year, or 5 years 10 months if the child completed kindergarten. Younger ages need special permission.

**Required documents** (uploaded as images in Noor):
- Copy of father's or mother's national ID.
- Vaccination certificate from Sehhaty (usually pulled automatically).
- Title deed or Ejar-documented rental contract — to confirm geographic zone.
- Kindergarten-completion certificate (optional, but eases admission).

**School choice**: the system shows schools in your residential zone (typically 2-3 km). You rank them first to third. The first school is tried first; if rejected for lack of seats, it moves to the second.

## Tracking results and alerts

From the guardian's account, for each child you see:

- **Weekly schedule** per term.
- **Daily absence** (SMS arrives the same day).
- **Term grades**: appear once approved by the principal.
- **Year-end result**: published after certification in Noor.
- **School certificate**: printable with an approved electronic seal.

## Requesting a student transfer

Two types:

**Internal transfer (within the same city)**:
- From guardian's account: Services → Internal Transfer Request.
- Choose the new school (must have an open seat).
- Current school approves, then new school, then education office.
- Duration: typically 5-10 working days.

**External transfer (between regions)**:
- Needs a documented justification: guardian's job transfer, permanent housing move, medical circumstances.
- Documents: transfer letter from employer or new housing contract.
- Duration: 15-30 working days.

## Common issues and fixes

- **"No data found"**: child's data not added in Absher — update dependents in Absher first.
- **Rejection for a specific school**: geographic zone or seats full. Pick an alternative.
- **Forgotten password**: on the sign-in page, choose "Forgot Password," you'll receive an SMS to Absher's mobile.
- **Account locked after 5 attempts**: auto-unlocks after 30 minutes.

## Noor vs Madrasati

| Criterion | Noor | Madrasati |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Administrative system | Learning platform |
| Daily user | Guardian and admin | Student and teacher |
| Manages | Enrolment, transfer, results | Classes, homework, exams |
| After COVID | Continued | Partially merged with in-person |

## Noor and the Full Educational Ladder K-to-PhD

Noor covers **general education** only. The full Saudi student journey passes through four sequential digital environments, each responsible for a stage:

| Stage | Age | System | What it manages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kindergarten | 3-5 years | [Noor](https://noor.moe.gov.sa/) | Registration, records |
| Primary/Middle/High School | 6-18 years | [Noor](https://noor.moe.gov.sa/) + [Madrasati](/en/madrasati-platform-guide) | Administration + daily learning |
| University | 18+ | [Unified Admission Portal](/en/saudi-universities-unified-admission) + each university's system | Application + study |
| Graduate school | 22+ | Each university's system + [Scholarship Portal](https://scholarships.moe.gov.sa/) | Master's and PhD |

The transition between systems is **manual** — Noor's role ends with high school, and the university unified-admission portal takes over. Don't expect your Noor high-school grades to transfer automatically — but they are electronically verifiable by universities through the [Musaddaq platform](https://www.moe.gov.sa/).

## Noor and National Exams

Results from the **national exams** required by universities (General Aptitude Test, Achievement Test, STEP for English) don't come from Noor — they come from the [Education and Training Evaluation Commission (Qiyas/ETEC)](https://etec.gov.sa/). However, the **exam schedule** and location appear in Noor under "Student Services" → "Central Exams." This integration eliminates the need for students to monitor two sites — but it doesn't auto-transfer scores.

## Guardian Rights Through Noor

Noor isn't just an informational tool — it's an executive arm for guardian rights enshrined in the **Saudi Education System**:

1. **Right to immediate access** to your child's attendance, grades, and behavior reports.
2. **Right to file a complaint** against the school or teacher via "Submit Complaint" in Noor, escalated to the regional Education Department within 10 business days.
3. **Right to transfer your child** for regulated reasons (guardian's job transfer, health circumstance, social circumstance).
4. **Right to choose secondary track** (Sharia, Sciences, Literary, Administrative) at the end of first-year secondary.
5. **Right to appeal a grade**: via "Appeal a Grade" within two weeks of results announcement.

To access official texts of these rights: the [Saudi Education System on laws.boe.gov.sa](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/) and its executive regulations on the [Bureau of Experts portal](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/).
