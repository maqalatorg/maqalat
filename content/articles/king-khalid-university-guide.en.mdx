King Khalid University differs from Saudi Arabia's flagship universities in that it is **essentially a regional institution** — founded in 1998 by merging the southern branches of Imam University and King Saud University, becoming the primary educational destination for the Asir region and Saudi Arabia's south ([Wikipedia — King Khalid University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Khalid_University)).

Today it hosts **26 colleges** and **9 research centers** across **27 campuses** in Abha and neighboring governorates, serving **61,000+ students**. In QS World University Rankings 2027, it holds **#514 globally**.

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Abha (main campus: University City in Al-Fara'a) + 27 campuses across Asir |
| **Founded** | 1998 (by royal decree — merging Imam and King Saud southern branches) |
| **Character** | Regional comprehensive university — the largest in the south |
| **Student body** | 61,708 (current official estimate) |
| **Type** | Public — free for Saudis with monthly stipend |
| **Regional advantage** | Serves an entire region (Asir) with no local competitor of its size |

## Colleges and Programs

Colleges are organized into **four academic clusters**:

### Health Sciences Cluster
- **Human Medicine** — with a full teaching hospital
- **Dentistry**
- **Pharmacy**
- **Applied Medical Sciences**
- **Nursing**
- **Public Health and Health Informatics**

### Sciences Cluster
- **Sciences** — physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, statistics
- **Engineering** — civil, electrical, mechanical, industrial
- **Computer and Information** — computer science, software engineering, networks
- **Architecture and Planning**

### Applied Sciences Cluster
- **Business Administration** — accounting, management, marketing, finance
- **Law**
- **Administrative and Financial Sciences**

### Humanities Cluster
- **Sharia and Fundamentals of Religion**
- **Arabic Language**
- **Languages and Translation**
- **Social Sciences**
- **Education**
- **Arts and Humanities**
- **Applied Arts**
- **Earth Sciences**

### Regional Community Colleges
- Community colleges in Abha, Bisha, Tihama, Al-Wa'sha, Tarib, Sarat Abidah, Khamis Mushait, and Muhayil Asir

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's + PhD for most primary disciplines.

## Where Graduates Work

KKU graduates primarily serve the southern region, with national pathways for high-demand disciplines:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Human Medicine** | University Hospital in Abha + Ministry of Health hospitals in Asir | Southern region hospitals (Jazan, Najran, Al-Bahah), local private sector |
| **Dentistry and Pharmacy** | Private sector in Abha and Khamis Mushait | Public hospitals, pharmacy chains (Nahdi, Al-Dawaa) |
| **Engineering (all disciplines)** | Asir development projects (Sadeer-Abha corridor, tourism projects) | Aramco and major contractors (southern branches), government agencies |
| **Computer Science** | STC, telecom companies, digital government transformation | Banks, startups, academic teaching |
| **Business and Finance** | Saudi banks (southern branches) | Local companies, Zakat and Tax Authority |
| **Sharia** | Sharia judiciary in Asir courts | Awqaf, preaching, Senior Scholars Council |
| **Education** | Public education in Asir region | Private schools, educational administration |
| **Tourism and Hospitality** (via specialised business programs) | Asir tourism development projects — a rapidly growing sector | Luxury resorts, Tourism Authority |

**Strategic note**: **Asir's development as a strategic Saudi tourism destination** (Sadeer-Abha corridor, Rijal Almaa, Al-Wa'sha) creates new job opportunities each year — and a local graduate in engineering, administration, and tourism has an advantage over someone transferred from Riyadh or Jeddah.

## What Makes KKU Different

1. **Regional presence without peer** — the only comprehensive university of this scale in Asir and southern Saudi Arabia.
2. **The University City in Al-Fara'a** — a modern purpose-built campus meeting international standards, opened in recent years.
3. **Regional medicine** — the Medical College and University Hospital serve over two million residents of the south, providing intense clinical training for graduates.
4. **Wide geographic reach** — 27 campuses across governorates = genuine educational access without students needing to relocate far.
5. **Benefit of Abha's climate** — the only Saudi university with moderate weather in both summer and winter, a comfortable study environment.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**KKU fits you if:**
- You are from Asir or the Saudi south generally (nearest comprehensive public university)
- Your specialty is medicine, business, or Sharia and you want to stay in the region
- You prefer Abha's climate (especially over Riyadh's hot summers)
- You want a large university with multiple specialty options

**KKU may not fit you if:**
- Your specialty requires advanced research (KAUST/KFUPM go much deeper)
- You want the highest global ranking (KFUPM #63 or KSU #107 rank higher)
- You want a career track at major companies concentrated in Riyadh and the Eastern Province (universities there are geographically closer with larger networks)

## Strong Alternatives

- **King Saud University** — comprehensive programs in Riyadh
- **King Abdulaziz University** — for Jeddah and the west
- **Najran University** — southern alternative closer to the Yemen border
- **Jazan University** — southwestern alternative
- **Al-Baha University** — alternative close to Asir
