The Saudi passport opens 90+ countries with no prior visa or via visa-on-arrival, per the [Henley Passport Index](https://www.henleyglobal.com/passport-index/) and our official sources. This guide breaks countries down by continent and access type — with a standing warning: **rules change**, so before booking a ticket, always verify on the destination country's embassy site or the Saudi Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

## Access types

| Type | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Full visa-free** | Enter directly with your passport, no prior procedure |
| **Visa-on-arrival (VoA)** | Visa issued at the arrival airport, usually for a fee, in minutes |
| **e-Visa** | Apply online before travel; issued in hours or days |
| **Prior visa (required)** | Traditional embassy route, not included in this list |

## Key travel tips

- **Always verify with the destination embassy before booking**: this list may update overnight.
- **Passport must be valid for more than 6 months** from date of entry.
- **Some countries require return tickets** even under "visa-free."

## Saudi Passport Global Ranking — Where Are We?

According to the [Henley Passport Index](https://www.henleyglobal.com/passport-index/) — the world's most widely-referenced passport ranking — the Saudi passport has ranked between the **60th and 65th globally** in recent years, out of nearly 200 passports. Numbers aren't fixed, but the general trend: **noticeable improvement** since 2019 driven by several new agreements.

Other indices give slightly different numbers because of differing methodologies:

| Index | What it measures |
|---|---|
| **[Henley Passport Index](https://www.henleyglobal.com/passport-index/)** | Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access (IATA data) |
| **Arton Passport Index** | Multi-dimensional access weighted by unique destinations |
| **Nomad Passport Index** | Combines travel rights, tax, and culture |

For a Saudi, the practical impact: your passport grants free access to over 90 countries, but remains weaker than comparable Gulf passports (an Emirati passport ranks around 9th globally).

## New Agreements Opening Doors

The Kingdom signed bilateral agreements easing entry for its citizens to several countries in recent years, most notably:

- **Japan** (2024): full 90-day exemption — a major leap; Japan previously required a prior visa.
- **South Korea** (K-ETA): eased entry with a quick electronic application instead of the paper visa.
- **European Union (upcoming ETIAS)**: an electronic travel authorization similar to UK ETA becomes mandatory in 2026 for some nationalities including Saudis to enter Schengen — **not a visa**, but a fast prior registration.
- **United Kingdom (ETA)**: since 2024, Saudis need an **Electronic Travel Authorization** instead of the traditional visa to enter the UK as tourists for up to 6 months — much simpler than the previous visa.
- **China** (2024 onwards): expanded free-transit system to 240 hours in select cities, provided arrival and departure are from different countries.

These changes mean: **your list from 3 years ago differs from today's list**. Check for updates before every trip.

## Types of Saudi Passports Beyond the Ordinary One

The passport in your hand is likely the **Ordinary Passport**. The Kingdom also issues:

- **Special Passport**: for senior state employees during official missions — opens additional countries.
- **Diplomatic Passport**: for the diplomatic corps and members of the ruling family — highest access level.
- **Hajj Passport**: temporary, for pilgrims from countries without full Saudi consular representation.

Each type has different exemption lists. The list above covers only the ordinary passport. For official details: [General Directorate of Passports — gdp.gov.sa](https://www.gdp.gov.sa/).
