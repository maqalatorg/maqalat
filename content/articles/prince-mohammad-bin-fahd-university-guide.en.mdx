Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University (PMU) is the primary private option for the **Eastern Province**. Founded in Al Khobar in 2006 ([PMU، About](https://www.pmu.edu.sa/about)) in academic partnership with the **Texas International Education Consortium** (TIEC), which shaped the initial US-model curriculum.

Today PMU is an **outcome-based education** (OBE) university, English-medium, feeding directly into the Eastern industrial cluster (Aramco, SABIC, oil-service firms) thanks to its location.

**Distinction**: PMU is the **first private university in the Eastern Province** to fully adopt the OBE (Outcome-Based Education) model, and it feeds directly into the industrial and oil sector fabric of Dhahran/Dammam/Al Khobar.

## Colleges and Programs

### 1. College of Business Administration

- BS Business، tracks in Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Banking
- **MBA**، executive and general tracks
- MS Finance

### 2. College of Engineering

- Civil Engineering
- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Industrial Engineering
- Accreditation: ABET

### 3. College of Computer Engineering & Sciences (CCSE)

- Computer Science
- Computer Engineering
- Information Technology Engineering
- Accreditation: ABET

### 4. College of Law

- LLB، established to fill the private-law-education gap in the Eastern Province
- Focus on commercial law and labor law

### 5. College of Architecture & Design

- Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)
- Interior Design
- Graphic Design

### 6. College of Sciences & Humanities

- Mathematics, Physics (service courses for Engineering)
- English Language, Translation

## Career Outcomes، Where Alumni Work

PMU graduates are **embedded in the Eastern industrial fabric**:

| Field | Typical employers |
|---|---|
| **Engineering** | Aramco (Eastern operations), SABIC, Ma'aden, Gulf contractors, oilfield services (Schlumberger, Halliburton, Baker Hughes) |
| **Computer Engineering** | Aramco (IT division), STC, tech companies in Al Khobar and Dammam, banks (Eastern branches) |
| **Business** | Saudi banks (Eastern branches), insurers, major family businesses in Al Khobar (Zamil, Olayan) |
| **Law** | Law firms in Al Khobar and Dammam, legal departments in oil-sector companies, Ministry of Justice |
| **Architecture & Design** | Engineering consultancies, real-estate developers in the Eastern Province |

**Notable point**: PMU is roughly 20 km from Aramco, offering direct internship and research-collaboration channels. Many Engineering graduates are hired into Aramco immediately after graduation.

## What Makes PMU Different

1. **Strategic Eastern Province location**، close to Aramco, SABIC, and major energy firms. No relocation needed to work in the Kingdom's industrial heartland.
2. **US-style OBE model**، project-based and team-based assessment, not just final exams. Prepares graduates for modern workplace expectations.
3. **Early ABET accreditation**، held by most Engineering and Computing programs.
4. **Medium size**، around 5,000 students, providing a personal academic environment rather than mass classrooms.
5. **Industry partnerships**، advisory boards for colleges include executives from Aramco and SABIC.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**PMU suits you if:**
- Your family or career ambition is in the Eastern Province
- You want to work at Aramco, SABIC, or Eastern industrial firms
- You prefer a US-style teaching model (projects, teamwork, continuous assessment)
- Your English is solid, or you're prepared to complete the foundation year
- Private-university tuition is not a hardship

**It may not suit you if:**
- You want a Riyadh or Jeddah setting (KFUPM is your Eastern option if you want tuition-free)
- You want a medical or pharmacy program (not offered at PMU)
- You prefer a coeducational campus (PMU is segregated)

## Strong Alternatives in the Eastern Province

- **KFUPM (King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals)**، public, Dhahran, top-ranked engineering in the Arab world, tuition-free for Saudis
- **Imam Abdulrahman Bin Faisal University (IAU)**، public, Dammam, the Eastern Province's largest public university
- **Alfaisal University**، private, Riyadh, strongest in Medicine and internationally accredited
- **Prince Sultan University (PSU)**، private, Riyadh, strongest in Business and Law
