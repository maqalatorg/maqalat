The Saudi Labor Law, issued by Royal Decree M/51 of 1426 AH and its subsequent amendments, governs the worker–employer relationship. This guide covers core rights directly with references to the governing article, so it serves as a practical reference when asked about a specific right.

## Wage and Time

### Wage — How It Is Paid

- **In the official currency** (Saudi Riyal) and via a bank in the **Wage Protection System**
- **Cycle**: monthly for monthly-paid employees (Article 90), weekly or bi-monthly for daily workers
- **Due date**: within the first week of the month following the pay cycle (Article 90)
- **Delay compensation**: the employee may claim compensation for delay via the Labor Office

### Working Hours

| Item | Detail | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| **Weekly cap** | 48 hours (8 × 6 days) | Art. 98 |
| **Ramadan (Muslim)** | 36 hours (6 daily) | Art. 98(2) |
| **Overtime** | Paid at 150% of the regular hourly wage | Art. 107 |
| **Working Friday/Holiday** | Compensatory rest day or 150% pay | Art. 105 |

**Overtime cap**: 300 hours per year with Ministry approval. Exceeding this requires special authorization.

## Leaves

### Annual Leave (Article 109)

- **21 days** minimum in the first year
- **30 days** after 5 consecutive years with the same employer
- **Cannot be waived** in writing, and cannot be replaced by cash except at end of service
- **Carry-over**: leave may be carried to the next year by written agreement

### Sick Leave (Article 117)

Within one year the worker is entitled to:

| Period | Wage percentage |
|---|---|
| 30 days | Full wage |
| 60 days | Three-quarters wage |
| 30 days | Unpaid |
| **Total** | **120 days** |

Requires a medical report from a certified provider (government hospital or insured provider).

### Occasion Leaves

- **Eid al-Fitr**: 4 days (Article 112)
- **Eid al-Adha**: 4 days (Article 112)
- **National Day**: 1 day
- **Marriage leave**: 5 days (Article 113)
- **Paternity leave**: 3 days (Article 113 after M/46 amendment)
- **Bereavement (spouse/parent/child)**: 5 days (Article 113)
- **Widow's mourning leave**: 130 days (Article 160)

### Maternity Leave (Article 151)

- **10 weeks** at full pay, split between pre- and post-birth per the worker's choice
- **4 additional weeks** at full pay if the newborn requires special care
- **Daily nursing right**: one hour per day until the child reaches 6 months (Article 154)

## Termination and Notice

### Termination Types

| Type | Party | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Legitimate termination (Art. 74)** | Employer | Full gratuity |
| **Grave dismissal (Art. 80)** | Employer | No gratuity |
| **Resignation (Art. 85)** | Employee | Tiered gratuity |
| **Unfair dismissal** | Employer | Additional compensation + full gratuity |
| **Termination for worker's disability** | Health condition | Full gratuity |

### Notice Period (Article 75)

- **60 days in writing** for monthly-paid employees
- **30 days** for others

The employer may accelerate termination by paying a notice allowance (the remaining period's wage).

### Unfair Dismissal — Compensation

Article 77: if the dismissal is unfair on an open-ended contract, the compensation is:

- **15 days' wage per year of service**, based on the last wage
- **With a minimum** of two months' wage
- **In addition to** the full end-of-service gratuity

By ruling of the Labor Court.

## Protection and Safety

### Job Security

- **No dismissal during annual or sick leave** (Article 77)
- **No dismissal for union activity or formal complaint** (Art. 77)
- **Protection of pregnant and nursing workers** — the contract cannot be terminated due to pregnancy (Article 151)

### Wage Protection

The Wage Protection System obliges the employer to pay wages via approved Saudi banks, and the Ministry of Human Resources monitors establishment compliance monthly. Non-compliance suspends the establishment's government services.

## Formal Complaint Process

### First: Amicable Settlement

- Via Absher → "Foreign Workforce" → "Labor Complaints"
- Reviewed within 21 days
- 65% of complaints are resolved at this stage

### Second: Labor Court

If amicable settlement fails, it is referred to:

- Specialized Labor Courts (Article 224)
- Litigation is free for the worker
- First-instance ruling within 6–12 months on average
- Appeal available within 30 days of the ruling
