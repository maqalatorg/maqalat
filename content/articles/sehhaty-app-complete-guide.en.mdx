Sehhaty is the official digital gateway to the Saudi Ministry of Health, connecting citizens and residents to the full health system: from primary-care centre to hospital, from prescription to pharmacy. Official URL: [sehhaty.sa](https://sehhaty.sa/) or the "Sehhaty" mobile app on iOS/Android.

Sehhaty was launched in **August 2019**, and in **January 2022** the Ministry of Health merged the standalone "Mawid" (appointments) and "Tabaud" (COVID tracing) apps into Sehhaty, making it the unified gateway for digital health services.

## Main Sehhaty services

| Service | Description |
|---|---|
| **Primary-care centre registration** | Links you to a centre near your address; becomes your default destination |
| **Appointment booking** | Centres, hospitals (post-referral), vaccination appointments |
| **E-prescriptions** | Every government prescription appears digitally |
| **Medication dispensing** | Government pharmacy or partner pharmacy |
| **Vaccination certificates** | For your children and for travellers, with official seal |
| **Unified medical record** | Medical history, tests, imaging, diagnoses |
| **Insurance verification** | If insured through employment, shows your cover and providers |
| **Health isolation** | Follow-up if you're in quarantine or as a contact |
| **Sehhaty digital card** | The digital health ID, replacing old paper cards |

## Registration and use steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Download "Sehhaty" from App Store or Google Play, or open sehhaty.sa |
| 2 | Open and choose "Sign In" |
| 3 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher account |
| 4 | Activate with the OTP sent to your Absher mobile |
| 5 | Review your data and set mobile + email for alerts |
| 6 | "Select Care Centre" — pick one from the list near your national address |
| 7 | Sehhaty is ready — browse the home screen |

## Booking a health-centre appointment

Rest assured you no longer walk in for a number:

1. From Home, choose "Appointments" → "New Booking."
2. Select your registered centre (or search another).
3. Choose the specialty (general, family, paediatrics, dentistry, etc.).
4. Available days and times appear — pick what suits you.
5. Confirm — booking number and QR code appear in the app.
6. Go at appointment time and show the QR at reception.

## E-prescription and medication

After your doctor's visit, the prescription is written digitally and delivered in the app:

- **Dispense from a government pharmacy**: free for citizens. Go to any government pharmacy or major centre and show the prescription code.
- **Dispense from a partner private pharmacy**: per your insurance cover. As long as insurance includes the medication, it's dispensed without payment.
- **Rare medications**: may need scheduled pickup or referral to a specialty hospital.

**Note**: an electronic Wasfaty prescription is valid for **7 days** from issuance ([source — Wasfaty patient FAQ](https://wasfaty.sa/faq-patients/)). If expired, you need a new visit.

## Vaccination certificates

A common service before travel or child school enrolment:

- **Routine child vaccinations**: appear automatically after each dose.
- **Official travel certificate**: printable PDF with e-seal, recognised in most countries.
- **Yellow fever vaccination**: for travellers to Africa and South America — issued with the international certificate.
- **Hajj vaccination**: mandatory for pilgrims (Meningitis, Influenza).

## Unified medical record

Every visit, test, scan, and diagnosis at any government facility is recorded in your file:

- Medical history.
- Current and past medications.
- Allergies (critical in emergencies).
- Chronic conditions.
- Latest test results.

This record is shared with the doctor at every visit — no need to re-explain.

## Health insurance and private sector

- If you're **insured through employment**: the app shows your insurer, approved providers, and cover limits.
- **Private hospital care**: needs **pre-approval** for some cases; visible in the app.
- **Emergency**: usually dispensed without prior approval.

For your insurance company details: the "Insurance" section in the app.

## Common mistakes

- **Care centre far from your home**: complicates appointments. Update it to the nearest in Sehhaty settings.
- **Notifications off**: you miss appointments. Enable in phone settings.
- **Old mobile number**: blocks OTP for prescriptions. Update in Absher first, then Sehhaty.
- **Walk-in without a booking number**: may not be served during full appointment slots.
- **Repeated no-shows**: repeated absence may temporarily restrict your ability to book, per the health centre's policy.

## How Sehhaty relates to other Ministry of Health apps

In January 2022, the Ministry of Health merged several standalone apps into Sehhaty:

| Former app | Function | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| **Mawid** | Government hospital appointment booking | Merged into Sehhaty |
| **Tabaud** | COVID tracing and contact | Merged into Sehhaty |
| **Wasfaty** | Electronic prescriptions | Runs at [wasfaty.sa](https://wasfaty.sa/) and surfaces inside Sehhaty |

Other apps that remain separate:

- **Seha Virtual** ([seha.sa](https://www.seha.sa/)): remote medical consultations.
- **Tawakkalna**: digital identity and general public services.
