The Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF, known as "Hadaf") is a Saudi government body founded in 2000, now one of the most important arms of employment and training support in the Kingdom. It operates under the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development. Services accessible at [hrdf.org.sa](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/) or [hrsd.gov.sa](https://hrsd.gov.sa/) for related services.

Hadaf isn't just a training platform — it's a network supporting the Saudi's entire labour-market journey: from first job search, through vocational training, to wage-uplift support.

## Three platforms under the Hadaf umbrella

| Platform | Function | URL |
|---|---|---|
| **Hadaf (parent)** | Managing support and major programmes | hrdf.org.sa |
| **Taqat** | Job matching between seekers and establishments | taqat.sa |
| **Doroob** | Free e-training | doroob.sa |

The account is unified across all three — register once with Absher, benefit everywhere.

## Wage-support programmes for Saudis

These are the most searched Hadaf arms:

### Employment support

Hadaf grants private establishments a portion of a new Saudi employee's salary for a set period (1-24 months). Practical effect: the establishment prefers hiring a Saudi because effective cost is lower.

### Wage-uplift support

For Saudis whose salary crosses a specific threshold, Hadaf may support an additional raise as an incentive to stay in the job. Programme terms change annually.

### On-the-job training support

Hadaf funds the full training cost of a Saudi for a number of months inside an establishment, and pays the Saudi a monthly stipend during training. The programme is known as **Tamheer**.

## Vocational training programmes

### Tamheer

- For recently graduated Saudis (6 months of training inside an establishment).
- Monthly stipend for the trainee.
- Certified upon completion.
- Access at [tamheer.hrdf.org.sa](https://tamheer.hrdf.org.sa/).

### Doroob

- A free e-training platform.
- Courses in freelancing, programming, marketing, languages, accounting.
- Digital completion certificates.
- Detailed in our [Doroob article](/en/doroob-free-courses-guide).

### Professional certification support

Hadaf subsidises part of the cost of international certifications (PMP, CFA, Cisco…) for Saudis employed in specific fields, under the "Professional Certifications" programme.

## Taqat — the employment brokerage

Taqat is the official employment portal for Saudis in the private sector:

| User | Benefits |
|---|---|
| Job seeker | CV registration, job matching, direct nomination |
| Establishment | Post jobs, search Saudi talent base, filter by city and specialty |
| Career adviser | Free professional guidance for registered Saudis |

**Registration**:
- Go to [taqat.sa](https://www.taqat.sa/)
- Sign in with Absher (name and ID pulled automatically)
- Complete your CV: certificates, experience, skills, languages
- Set preferred specialty and city
- Enable alerts for new matching jobs

## Registering with Hadaf as a job seeker

This step precedes benefiting from any support programme:

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [hrdf.org.sa](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/) |
| 2 | Choose "Individual Account" → "New Registration" |
| 3 | Sign in with National Access or Absher |
| 4 | Select "Job Seeker" as primary status |
| 5 | Complete education and experience info (if any) |
| 6 | Upload your CV (optional) |
| 7 | Enable alerts for programmes matching you |

## Support eligibility conditions

- Saudi nationality.
- Age band and educational level differ across programmes.
- Applicant must not already benefit from a **conflicting programme** (can't combine Tamheer with standard employment support).
- Serious Taqat registration (non-empty CV).

## Common mistakes that hurt your chances

- **CV without detailed experience**: Taqat won't nominate you.
- **Refusing two consecutive interviews**: may lower your ranking for 60 days.
- **Outdated email and mobile**: you miss alerts.
- **Programme conflict**: don't register for Tamheer while in another supported job.

## HRDF and Saudization (Nitaqat)

HRDF doesn't work in a vacuum — it's tightly coupled with the [Nitaqat programme](https://qiwa.sa/), managed by the Ministry of Human Resources, which classifies private-sector establishments by their Saudi-employment ratio into **Platinum**, **Green** (three tiers), or **Red** bands. Establishments in higher bands receive:

- **Priority in work-permit processing** for their non-Saudi employees
- **Larger HRDF subsidies** when hiring Saudis
- **Advantages in government tenders** with Saudization preference weighting

Practically: an employer hires a Saudi for two complementary reasons — lowering their cost through HRDF support, and raising their Nitaqat classification for further government benefits. This double incentive made Saudization more commercially viable for employers, not just an administrative obligation.

## Does HRDF Support Women's Employment Specifically?

Yes — HRDF has dedicated arms for hiring Saudi women:

| Programme | Target audience | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Women's employment support** | Employers | Higher subsidy rate when hiring female Saudis |
| **Remote work** | Women working from home | Formal employment path via [Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/) with proper contract |
| **Business incubators** | Female entrepreneurs | Small-project funding through HRDF partnerships |
| **Women's Tamheer tracks** | Recent female graduates | On-job training with a monthly stipend |

To maximise benefits, women employers or job seekers should complete the **women's account** within Taqat so they receive automatic nomination for these tracks.

## HRDF by the Numbers

HRDF publishes annual impact reports available on [its official site](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/en/publications/). These reports show beneficiary numbers, spending volume, and the retention rate one year later. The most important metric for the Saudi applicant: **retention rate** per programme — the higher it is, the more successful the programme is at keeping the Saudi in their job, not just placing them.
