Naif Arab University for Security Sciences is not a Saudi university in the institutional sense — it is **an Arab League-affiliated university** ([UNODC — NAUSS](https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/commissions/CCPCJ/PNI/institutes-NAUSS.html)), headquartered in Riyadh. Founded in 1978 as the first Arab university specialised in security sciences at the graduate level.

The university is **accredited by the United Nations** as part of the UN Programme Network on Crime Prevention and Criminal Justice (UN-PNI), serving security agency personnel across **22 Arab countries** — an exceptional academic environment without Arab peer.

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Riyadh, Naif District (northeast Riyadh) |
| **Founded** | 1978 (by resolution of the Arab League) |
| **Character** | Pan-Arab international university specialised in security sciences — graduate only |
| **Governing body** | **Arab League** (not Saudi Ministry of Education) |
| **Degrees available** | Higher diploma + Master's + PhD — **no bachelor's** |
| **Target audience** | Officers and staff of security agencies across 22 Arab countries + researchers + academics |
| **International recognition** | UN-PNI institute, partnerships with Interpol and UNODC |

## Ranking and Recognition

The university **does not appear in standard QS/THE rankings** because its unusual specialised nature (graduate-only in a narrow field, most students being full-time employees) falls outside ranking criteria. **International recognition comes through other channels**:

- **UN affiliated institute** (UN-PNI) — the highest recognition in the field
- **Permanent partner with Interpol** — joint training and programmes
- **Partner with UNODC** — UN Office on Drugs and Crime
- **First in the Arab world** with no institutional peer combining academia and security training

## Colleges and Programs

The university organizes its programmes into **five colleges and centers** ([NAUSS Official Website](https://nauss.edu.sa)):

### 1. College of Criminal Justice
- **Department of Criminal Law** — crimes, punishments, modern criminal policy
- **Department of Police Sciences** — investigation methodologies, crisis management, crowd control
- **Department of Security Management** — security planning, risk management, personal protection
- **Department of Forensic Sciences** — fingerprint science, DNA analysis, crime scene

### 2. College of Forensic Sciences
- **Forensic Medicine** — forensic diagnosis, toxicology, suspicious deaths
- **Criminology** — patterns, criminal statistics, criminal sociology
- **Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics** — digital investigation, critical infrastructure protection

### 3. Training College
- Short and medium training programmes for security agency officers
- Specialised training in counter-terrorism, drug control, border security

### 4. College of Language Studies
- **Arabic for Non-Native Speakers in Security**
- **Specialised English for Security Personnel** — the language of international documents and cross-border cooperation

### 5. Deanship of Graduate Studies
- Supervises all master's and doctoral programmes across specialties

**Research centers**:
- **Research and Studies Center** — applied research in security and crime
- **Computer and Information Center** — regional criminal databases, digital investigation support

## Where Graduates Work

The nature of the university means **most students are employees before enrollment**. Graduates return to their employer with higher qualifications and promotion. Main destinations:

| Track | Destinations |
|---|---|
| **Saudi security agencies** | Ministry of Interior, Public Security, General Investigation, General Directorate of Prisons, Passports, Border Guard, Civil Defence, General Intelligence |
| **Arab security agencies** | Interior and Defence ministries across all 22 Arab countries (each dispatches its officers) |
| **Security academia** | Police and security colleges and institutes across Arab countries, security research centers |
| **International organisations** | Interpol, UNODC, regional counter-terrorism organisations, UN peacekeeping missions |
| **Specialised private sector** | Global private security companies (especially post-2020 growth in Saudi Arabia), cybersecurity firms |
| **Security consulting** | Advisory to government agencies, security auditing, risk assessment |
| **Judiciary and Public Prosecution** | Specialisation in terrorism, drugs, cybersecurity, fraud cases |
| **Prison administration and rehabilitation** | General Directorate of Prisons, rehabilitation centers, penal policy research |

**Strategic note**: With **Vision 2030** and growing demand for specialties like **cybersecurity** (National Cybersecurity Authority, banks, telecom authority) and **security for mega-events** (NEOM, Qiddiya, Riyadh Season, 2034 World Cup), demand for NAUSS graduates is rising rapidly.

## What Makes NAUSS Different

1. **The only Arab institution with this depth** — alternatives are Western institutions (FBI Academy, Bramshill, ENSP France) with much higher costs and a very different environment.
2. **The only Arab university UN-accredited** in security sciences.
3. **Alumni network including security leadership in 22 Arab countries** — exceptional professional network value.
4. **Arab environment** — Arabic-first instruction, suitable for the Arab graduate without a language barrier.
5. **Active research center in security-sensitive topics** — terrorism, cybersecurity, offender rehabilitation, security of mega-events.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**NAUSS fits you if:**
- You are an officer or employee in a security agency and want specialised graduate studies
- Your bachelor's is in law, police sciences, sociology, or computing, and you want to specialise in security
- You work or aspire to work in counter-terrorism, cybersecurity, forensic medicine, or prison administration
- You want a broad Arab professional network in the security sector

**NAUSS may not fit you if:**
- You are seeking a bachelor's degree (the university offers no undergraduate)
- Your specialty is outside security and criminal justice
- You want a high global ranking by traditional measures (QS/THE)

## Strong Alternatives

- **King Fahd Security College** — for a bachelor's in security sciences inside Saudi Arabia
- **King Khalid Military College** — for a military track
- **King Saud University, College of Law** — for criminal law separately from a security specialisation
- **Specialised international universities** — Bramshill (UK), ENSP (France), FBI National Academy (USA) — require scholarship and special conditions
