Umm Al-Qura University is not merely a regional university — it is **Makkah's university**, with all that carries in scholarly and religious responsibility. The first college (Sharia) was founded in 1949, making it one of **Saudi Arabia's oldest higher education institutions**. Today it serves over 100,000 students across Makkah, Taif, and Al-Qunfudhah through **14 colleges** spanning Sharia, medicine, and Islamic engineering ([Wikipedia — Umm al-Qura University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umm_al-Qura_University)).

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Makkah (main campus) + branches in Al-Lith, Al-Qunfudhah, Al-Jumoom |
| **Founded** | 1949 (College of Sharia) → 1981 (independent university) |
| **Character** | Comprehensive university with Sharia origins, strong presence in medicine and engineering |
| **Student body** | 100,000+ undergraduate + 10,000+ graduate |
| **Type** | Public — free for Saudis with monthly stipend |
| **Distinctive role** | Serves the Two Holy Mosques institutionally (Hajj and Umrah research, Islamic architecture) |

**Direct read**: The global ranking is modest by QS standards — Sharia and Islamic scholarship does not map to Western research metrics. But **UQU's Islamic standing** and role as Makkah's university make it the natural destination for Sharia students across the Muslim world.

## Colleges and Programs

Fourteen colleges built historically from a Sharia nucleus into a comprehensive system:

### Sharia and Humanities (Historical Core)
- **Sharia and Islamic Studies** (1949 — oldest) — fiqh, usul, Sharia policy, Islamic studies
- **Da'wah and Fundamentals of Religion** (1981) — creed, hadith, tafsir
- **Arabic Language** (1981)
- **Social Sciences** (1984)
- **Education** (1981)

### Health Sector (Strong Growth Post-1990)
- **Medicine** (1995)
- **Dental Medicine** (2007)
- **Pharmacy** (2006)
- **Nursing**
- **Public Health and Health Informatics** (2011)
- **Applied Medical Sciences**

### Engineering and Sciences
- **Engineering and Islamic Architecture** (1982) — **unique in the region**
- **Applied Sciences** (1990)
- **Computers and Information Systems**

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's + PhD for most programs.

## Where Graduates Work

UQU graduates — particularly in Sharia and Islamic Architecture — have a **distinct labor market** thanks to proximity to the Two Holy Mosques and their related projects:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Sharia and Fiqh** | General Presidency for the Affairs of the Two Holy Mosques | Sharia judiciary, Awqaf, Senior Scholars Council |
| **Da'wah and Fundamentals of Religion** | Ministry of Islamic Affairs (preaching offices at the Holy Mosques) | Imams and Khateebs, Qur'an radio |
| **Engineering and Islamic Architecture** | **Holy Mosques expansion projects** (General Presidency, Ministry of Hajj) | Historical mosque consultancies, Ministry of Islamic Affairs, Makkah Development Authority, Madinah Development Authority |
| **Medicine** | King Abdullah Hospital (Makkah), Al-Noor Specialist Hospital | Ministry of Health hospitals in Taif and Jeddah, private sector |
| **Dentistry and Pharmacy** | Private sector in Makkah and Taif | Public hospitals, pharmaceutical companies |
| **Computing and Information Systems** | STC, Makkah tech companies | Vision projects (NEOM, Red Sea — relatively nearby), banks, digital transformation |
| **Public Health** | Ministry of Health (Hajj and Umrah units) — **a rare specialty with strategic importance** | WHO (Hajj seasons), epidemic research bodies |

**Strategic note**: A UQU **Public Health** graduate — trained on one of the world's most complex health challenges (the Hajj season) — carries a rare profile in demand at global health organisations and across the Islamic world.

## What Makes UQU Different

1. **Makkah's university** — no substitute. A student of Sharia, Islamic Architecture, or Public Health lives near the Haram every day — an academic-spiritual experience without equal.
2. **Islamic Architecture** — a unique program in Saudi Arabia and the Islamic world. Holy Mosques expansion projects alone can absorb its graduates for decades.
3. **Public Health in the Hajj context** — a unique applied specialty; graduates are internationally in demand.
4. **Deep historical foundation** — Saudi Arabia's first formal Sharia college, giving UQU an academic legacy and scholar/graduate network without parallel.
5. **Regional branches** — Al-Lith, Al-Qunfudhah, Al-Jumoom = expanded educational service to the southwest region.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**UQU fits you if:**
- You want a Sharia path with Makkah's institutional authority (high symbolic value)
- You dream of working on Holy Mosques projects (Islamic Architecture, Sharia, Public Health)
- Your discipline is medicine or health, and you want a distinctive work environment (Hajj season is an exceptional training opportunity)
- You prefer the Makkah / western Saudi environment

**UQU may not fit you if:**
- Global ranking is your priority (KFUPM / KSU / KAU rank higher)
- Your discipline is deep applied scientific research (KAUST or KFUPM more suitable)
- You want a quiet campus without seasonal congestion (Makkah is seasonal)
