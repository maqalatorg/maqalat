The Saudi driver's license has seen its digital services expand markedly since 2017, and both issuance and renewal are now among the easiest Absher procedures. This guide covers the core paths for citizens, residents, and foreign converters, plus the exceptional cases.

## Types of Saudi Driver's Licenses

The Traffic system issues several license types by category:

| Type | Use | Minimum age |
|---|---|---|
| **Motorcycle** | Powered two-wheelers | 16 |
| **Private (Class 3)** | Personal cars up to 9 passengers | 18 |
| **Light transport** | Light trucks and small buses | 20 |
| **Heavy transport** | Large trucks | 21 |
| **Public transport** | Taxis and buses | 21 |
| **Heavy equipment** | Cranes, bulldozers | 22 |

The most-requested is the private license, and this guide focuses on it.

## New License Issuance, Full Steps

### For Citizens and Residents Who Have Not Driven Before

The full path passes through 4 stations:

1. **Medical exam**: at any certified medical center (vision, hearing, general health). Many providers now push the result electronically to Traffic directly.
2. **Enroll in a driving school**: certified schools operate in all major cities. Fees vary between 2,500–4,500 SAR depending on the school and program length.
3. **Tests**:
   - **Theory test**: traffic-rule and safety questions
   - **Practical test**: vehicle control, parking, reversing, turning
4. **Electronic issuance**: after passing, the license is issued automatically on Absher within 24 hours.

**Typical course length**: 20–40 training hours at the school, spread over 2–6 weeks by program.

### For Saudi Women

Since June 2018 women have been permitted to drive. The path mirrors men's exactly: medical exam, school, test, issuance. Women's driving schools operate in major cities, some attached to major universities.

## Converting a Foreign License

Residents holding a driver's license from their country have two paths:

### Path One: Direct Conversion (Recognized Nationalities)

Traffic allows direct conversion without a new practical test for holders of licenses issued by countries covered by bilateral agreements or otherwise recognized. Reported examples: GCC states, the United States, the United Kingdom, and several European countries. **The list changes periodically — check it live on Absher when starting the service before relying on it.**

**Conditions**:

- Valid license (not expired)
- Official translation (if not in Arabic or English)
- Certified medical exam
- Conversion fee payment

**Steps** via Absher → Traffic → "Convert Foreign Driver's License":

1. Upload the foreign license image
2. Upload the translation (if needed)
3. Pay
4. Book a verification appointment (some cases only)
5. Collect the Saudi license

### Path Two: Short Test Route

For nationalities not covered by direct conversion:

- A brief theory test
- One practical test (no need for the full driving-school program)
- Requires the foreign license to be valid

**Practical tip**: check the direct-conversion nationality list on Absher before enrolling in a full driving school program.

## Absher Renewal

The simplest procedure, since the license is already electronically registered:

1. Absher → Traffic → "Renew Driver's License"
2. Choose the renewal period (5 or 10 years)
3. Pay the statutory fee via SADAD
4. The digital license updates instantly in the Absher app
5. The plastic card arrives via Saudi Post within 10–15 days

**Medical exam at renewal**:

| Category | Required? |
|---|---|
| **Citizen under 65** | No for first two renewals, yes after |
| **Citizen 65+** | Yes every 5 years |
| **Resident** | Yes every renewal |

## Statutory Fees

The Traffic tariff for a private license is **SAR 40 per year**, uniform for citizens and residents:

| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| **Issue – 5 years** | SAR 200 |
| **Issue – 10 years** | SAR 400 |
| **Renew – 5 years** | SAR 200 |
| **Renew – 10 years** | SAR 400 |
| **Foreign license conversion** | Prorated on the chosen period (SAR 200 / 400) |

**Other fees** (lost/damaged replacement, medical exam, re-testing) appear on the Absher screen when the service is opened, and are subject to executive updates.

Driving-school tuition is entirely separate from the Traffic fee and varies by school, city, and program.

## Points System and License

Saudi Arabia's traffic-point regulation counts points on the driver's license (issued 1438 AH, activated 2017). Statutory consequences on accumulation:

- **24 points** in one Hijri year, first occurrence → license cancelled for **3 months**.
- **24 points** in one Hijri year, second occurrence → license cancelled for **6 months**.
- Further recurrences carry tougher penalties.

For full details see the [traffic violations guide](/en/traffic-violations-guide) or the [regulation on the Ministry of Interior site](https://www.moi.gov.sa/wps/portal/Home/sectors/publicsecurity/traffic/).

## Additional Questions

**Can I drive with the digital license only, without the plastic card?**
Yes in Saudi Arabia. The digital license in the Absher app carries the same official force. But for foreign travel, an international driving permit alongside the Saudi one is required.

**How do I get a Saudi international driving permit?**
Via the [Saudi Automobile and Touring Club](https://www.sataclub.com.sa/). Not issued directly by Traffic.

**Can I drive a friend's car without formal registration?**
Yes, provided your license is valid and the vehicle carries comprehensive insurance covering "other drivers" or an "authorization to drive". Formal authorization via Absher is the safest.

**What if I get a violation with an expired license?**
The fine doubles: the original violation fine + a 500 SAR expired-license fine. Points are added as any violation.
