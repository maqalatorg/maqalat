The Saudi government salary scale is the system that determines every Saudi government employee's pay and has seen multiple reforms since its late-1970s creation. This guide covers the scale structure as applied in 2026, and how actual pay is computed (base + allowances).

## Scale Structure

The scale consists of **15 grades** ordered by job level. Each grade has **15 steps**, and the step advances via the annual increment without needing additional approval.

### Example Grades

| Grade | Sample Job |
|---|---|
| **1** | Junior admin assistant, clerk |
| **3** | Technician, specialist assistant |
| **5** | Junior specialist (post-bachelor's) |
| **7** | Mid-specialist, section head |
| **10** | Department manager |
| **13** | Assistant deputy |
| **14** | Deputy Minister |
| **15** | Minister and equivalent |

Examples are indicative — each government entity maps its jobs to grades per its Civil Service-approved classification.

## Actual Salary Components

Actual salary = **base salary** + **allowances** + **increments**, minus insurance and retirement contributions.

### 1. Base Salary

Set by the grade/step intersection. Grade 1 step 1 starts around 4,500 SAR, rising gradually. Grade 15 (minister) reaches about 25,000 SAR base.

**Increment rule**: each year the employee gets an additional step within their grade until reaching step 15. Increment value: 150–350 SAR by grade.

### 2. Transport Allowance

Paid to employees using their personal vehicle for work. Value: 500–1,500 SAR by job nature. Some entities pay it to all; some to leadership only.

### 3. Housing Allowance

Paid annually as a lump sum equal to **3 monthly base salaries**, to those not in government housing. Usually transferred in Muharram or January.

### 4. Work-Nature Allowance

Paid for specific occupations as compensation for special conditions:

| Sector | Type | Approximate value |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | Infection / risk | 15–50% of base |
| **Education** | Teaching | 10–30% of base |
| **Public Security** | Field risk | 20–40% of base |
| **Customs** | Scarcity | 20–30% |
| **Judiciary** | Judicial (raised by M/16) | Substantial |

### 5. Representation Allowance

For senior leadership (deputy, director general). Value varies by position.

### 6. Special Allowances

- Travel allowance during missions
- Training allowance when the employee is dispatched
- Promotion allowance (for some jobs on promotion)
- Furniture-moving allowance on inter-region transfer

## Civil vs Military Scales

| Criterion | Civil Scale | Military Scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Overseeing body** | Civil Service | Ministry of Defense / Guard / Interior |
| **Unit** | Grades/steps | Military ranks (private, corporal, sergeant…) |
| **Promotion** | Internal via increment + external via vacancy | By seniority + course success |
| **Allowances** | Multiple by job | Military work-nature + risk + field |
| **Retirement** | 65 (per 2024 system) | 60 usually + eligible for early retirement |

## Promotion — How It Happens

Promotion to a higher grade requires:

1. **Reaching a threshold in the current grade** (usually step 10 or higher)
2. **A vacancy** in the higher grade at the entity
3. **Meeting promotion conditions**: years of experience, academic degree, performance rating (Excellent or Very Good)
4. **Passing the entity's process**: may include an interview, test, internal review
5. **Issuance of the promotion decision** and Civil Service ratification

**Promotion timing**: usually 3–7 years per grade depending on vacancies and performance.

## Cost-of-Living Increments and Raises

Since the scale's inception, there have been special adjustments (King Abdulaziz allowance, King Abdullah base-raise, 1439 AH adjustment), and values are raised periodically. Not annual, but by special royal orders.

## Additional Questions

**Can an employee view their salary in detail?**
Yes, via the "Mudad" government payroll system, or the monthly salary slip. Every line is shown separately.

**Is there an absolute government salary cap?**
No published official cap, but the highest grade (15) reaches 25,000 base + substantial allowances. Strategic senior positions are outside the scale and set by special royal orders.

**How is end-of-service gratuity computed for government employees?**
Government employees fall under the civil retirement system (not the Labor Law) and receive a retirement pension, not a private-sector-style gratuity. See our [GOSI pension guide](/en/gosi-pension-guide).

**Do government employees earn overtime pay?**
Generally no — government jobs follow the defined work week. Some entities pay an "extra hours" allowance with ministerial or authority approval.
