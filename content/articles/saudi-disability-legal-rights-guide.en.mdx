This guide is a legal and practical reference to the rights guaranteed by Saudi regulations for persons with disabilities (hearing, visual, motor, mental, and intellectual). It relies on the **Care for Persons with Disabilities Law** issued by Royal Decree M/37 of 1421 AH, and its subsequent amendments.

## First: the digital Disability Card

The card is **the key to all rights**. Without it you can't benefit from discounts, exemptions, or support.

### Issuance conditions

- **Saudi citizen or resident** (some services are citizen-only).
- **Certified medical report** from a major government hospital or accredited diagnostic centre documenting disability type and grade.
- **Disability grade documented by percentage**: mild, moderate, severe.

### Issuance steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Gather medical reports from your treating physician |
| 2 | Visit an accredited diagnostic centre (e.g., King Salman Center or a government hospital) |
| 3 | Obtain a comprehensive diagnostic report |
| 4 | Open [Sehhaty](/en/sehhaty-app-complete-guide) → "Disability" → "Card Request" |
| 5 | Upload reports |
| 6 | Application review by the [Authority of Persons with Disabilities (APD)](https://apd.gov.sa/) |
| 7 | Card issued digitally in Tawakkalna and Absher |

Issuance duration: typically **15-30 working days**.

## Second: health rights

### Specialised healthcare

- **Free** at government hospitals for citizens.
- **Medical devices**: wheelchairs, hearing aids, prosthetics — provided by the health sector or associations.
- **Medical and functional rehabilitation**: physical therapy, speech therapy, motor rehab — free at specialised centres.

### Psychological and social support

- **Rehabilitation centres** provide psychological support to the individual and family.
- **Specialised associations** for each disability type (hearing, visual…).

## Third: financial support

### Comprehensive Rehabilitation

- **Who qualifies**: Saudi families with a documented disabled member.
- **Support value**: varies by disability grade, beneficiary age, and family status.
- **URL**: [sbis.hrsd.gov.sa](https://sbis.hrsd.gov.sa/) → Comprehensive Rehabilitation.

### Social welfare stipend

- **Who qualifies**: families supporting a severely disabled person.
- **Value**: added to Social Security.

### Driver and helper stipend

- **Who qualifies**: severely disabled persons who need a driver or home helper.
- **Disbursement**: via Comprehensive Rehabilitation.

### Device and modified vehicle purchase support

- **Right**: support for buying a vehicle equipped with driving devices suited to a motor disability.
- **Procedure**: via the Ministry of HRSD or specialised associations.

## Fourth: education

### Right to education

- **Mandatory**: government schools must admit students with mild and moderate disabilities under **inclusion**.
- **Severe disability**: specialised schools (Al-Amal for deaf, Al-Noor for blind).
- **Student's school**: as close to their residence as possible.

### University education

- **Right to university education** on equal terms.
- **Accommodations**: Braille files, sign-language interpreter, extra time in exams.
- **Scholarships**: available with suitable conditions.

### Special education schools

- **Al-Amal Institutes**: for the deaf and hard-of-hearing.
- **Al-Noor Institutes**: for the blind and low-vision.
- **Intellectual Education Institutes**: for intellectual disabilities.

## Fifth: employment

### Right to work

- **Source**: Saudi Labour Law and the Care for Persons with Disabilities Law.
- **Right**: work in public and private sectors without discrimination.

### Employment quota

- **Requirement**: private establishments with 25+ workers must employ a percentage of persons with disabilities.
- **Currently**: at least 4% of employees.
- **Incentives**: establishments with disabled employees count them as Saudi nationals in Nitaqat.

### Protection from dismissal

- **Prohibited** to dismiss due to disability.
- **Promotion**: on equal criteria to other employees.
- **Workplace accommodations**: providing equipment, adjusting hours if needed.

### Vocational rehabilitation

- **Vocational rehab centres** train on suitable professions.
- **[Doroob](/en/doroob-free-courses-guide)** offers accessible free e-courses.
- **[Hadaf fund](/en/hrdf-hadaf-guide)** supports employing persons with disabilities.

## Sixth: mobility and access

### Parking

- **Designated parking** for persons with disabilities at public facilities.
- **Use**: requires showing the Disability Card or the vehicle sticker.
- **Violating it**: a traffic offence.

### Special licence

- **Right**: obtaining a driving licence for a modified vehicle for motor disabilities.
- **Procedure**: specialised medical exam + training at an equipped centre.

### Public transport

- **Right**: free or subsidised transport for disabled students.
- **Planes and trains**: boarding accommodations, priority seating, discounts (per carrier).

### Building accessibility

- **Requirement**: all new public buildings must be accessible (elevators, ramps, restrooms, signage).
- **Complaint**: an inaccessible facility can be reported to the municipality.

## Seventh: legal protection

### Protection from harm

- **Source**: Protection from Harm Law and Care for Persons with Disabilities Law.
- **Right**: filing a harm complaint via 1919 or protection shelters.
- **Judicial protection**: urgent priority hearing.

### Guardianship and power of attorney

- **Right**: appointing a legal proxy via [Najiz](/en/najiz-services-guide).
- **Guardianship of minors**: follows general provisions with the disabled person's interest in mind.

### Court testimony

- **Right**: giving testimony in one's language (like sign language for the deaf).
- **Interpreter**: provided by the court for free.

## Eighth: exemptions and discounts

### Government exemptions

| Fee | Exemption |
|---|---|
| Traffic and violations fee for a modified vehicle | Specific exemptions |
| Some municipal fees | By case |
| ID and passport issuance fees | Per regulations |
| Special-licence issuance fee | Specific exemptions |

### Special discounts

- **Airlines**: discounts for Saudi persons with disabilities on Saudi carriers.
- **Haramain and Riyadh trains**: discounts.
- **Some museums and recreational facilities**: free or discounted entry.

## Ninth: responsible authorities

| Authority | Role |
|---|---|
| **[Authority of Persons with Disabilities (APD)](https://apd.gov.sa/)** | Official reference for policies and card issuance |
| **Ministry of HRSD** | Comprehensive Rehabilitation and stipends |
| **Ministry of Health** | Medical care and rehabilitation |
| **Ministry of Education** | Education and inclusion |
| **[King Salman Center for Disability Research](https://kscdr.org.sa/)** | Research and diagnosis |
| **Specialised associations** | Qualitative care |

## Tenth: procedures when a right is violated

**Employment discrimination**:
- Complaint via [Qiwa](/en/qiwa-platform-guide).

**Missing accessibility in a public building**:
- Complaint to the municipality or the government Tawasul platform.

**Refusal of treatment or care mistreatment**:
- Complaint to the [Council of Health Insurance (CCHI)](https://www.cchi.gov.sa/) or Ministry of Health.

**Refusal of school enrolment**:
- Escalation to the Ministry of Education.

**Harm or neglect**:
- Call 1919 or protection shelters.
