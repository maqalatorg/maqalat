Sakani is Saudi Arabia's national housing-support portal, run by the Ministry of Municipalities & Housing (MoMRAH) with the Real Estate Development Fund. Its stated target is to raise Saudi home-ownership to 70% under Vision 2030. Official portal: [sakani.sa](https://sakani.sa/).

Discussions about Sakani often blur three distinct cases: **families**, **singles**, and **independent women (divorced or widowed)**. This guide separates each.

## Core programmes inside Sakani

| Programme | What it means | Provider |
|---|---|---|
| Subsidised housing units | Ready or under-construction apartment/villa at a reduced price | MoMRAH + developers |
| Subsidised housing finance | Bank loan with soft instalments plus a subsidised capital grant | REDF + banks |
| Free land plots | A free plot for priority families | MoMRAH |
| Self-construction | Support to build on land you already own | REDF |
| Rental subsidy | Temporary rent support until a unit is available | MoMRAH (temporary) |

Each programme has different conditions, but registering on Sakani opens all options — you are not forced to choose from the start.

## General eligibility criteria

Before we detail each case, these are the shared criteria checked for every applicant:

1. **Nationality**: Saudi citizen (non-Saudi wives of Saudi husbands have a special track with different criteria).
2. **Age**: 25+ (limited exceptions for dependents under this age).
3. **No prior home ownership**: no title deed under the applicant or spouse.
4. **Housing independence**: currently in independent housing (documented rental or family unit that belongs to you) — not co-residing with parents.
5. **Financeable income**: for families that will take financing, not required for fully subsidised units.

## Sakani for families (the default case)

Saudi families (spouse + children) are the primary target group. Priority within this group depends on:

- **Family size**: families with children before families without.
- **Income**: middle and lower income before high income.
- **Registration year**: earliest first (within each income bracket).
- **Region**: distribution constrained by unit availability in the requested area.

**Maximum financing** per family is set by monthly income and head of household's age. The official calculator on sakani.sa gives an estimate as soon as you enter income.

## Sakani for singles

"Single" here means: **an unmarried man or woman, 25+, employed and living independently**. This case is often misunderstood:

**Yes, singles can register and benefit**, but with **two extra requirements**:
- **Stable income from employment** — a single without income is not accepted.
- **Currently independent housing** — a rental documented in Ejar under the applicant's name.

**Programmes available to singles**:
- Subsidised housing finance (with slightly different terms than families).
- Some units in small-apartment projects (studio, 1-bedroom).
- Free plots: **generally not available for singles**.

**Priority is lower than families** in all cases. A single who registered 5 years ago may not receive a unit before a family that registered last year.

## Sakani for divorced and widowed women

Divorced or widowed women are legally treated as **heads of an independent household**, not as singles. This is a large priority gap:

**Conditions**:
- Divorce documented in the Personal Status Court or via Absher (customary divorce not accepted).
- For widows, husband's death proven by an official certificate.
- Independent income (job, pension, Social Security, documented family support).
- Currently in independent housing (rental or family home).
- Custody of children raises priority.

**Programmes available**:
- All programmes are open, same as families.
- **Free land plot**: available with elevated priority for divorced and widowed women with children.
- **Subsidised units**: priority within the family queue — above high-income couples.
- **Temporary rental subsidy**: subsidised if the divorce is recent and until a unit is available.

## Sakani registration steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [sakani.sa](https://sakani.sa/) and choose "Individuals" |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher |
| 3 | Review personal data and complete any missing fields |
| 4 | Specify marital status accurately (married / divorced / widowed / single) |
| 5 | Attach proof of housing independence (rental contract or title deed for family home) |
| 6 | Enter income data (verified automatically against GOSI and bank) |
| 7 | Choose preferred region and maximum monthly instalment |
| 8 | Submit and keep your reference number |

Eligibility decision is typically issued within **15 working days**; queue enrolment begins immediately on approval.

## Stages after approval

1. **Queue placement**: you receive a clear priority number.
2. **Unit offers**: available units appear as options on your dashboard.
3. **Choosing a unit or financing**: accept an offer or wait for an alternative.
4. **Financing process**: with a bank or lender (credit file reviewed).
5. **Contract signing**: final documentation via Ejar or notary.
6. **Support disbursement**: paid directly to developer or bank — never to the beneficiary in cash.

## Common mistakes that hurt your priority

- **Co-residing with family recorded in national address**: the system marks you as not independent.
- **Rental not documented in Ejar**: a paper contract is not counted.
- **Delayed marital-status update**: an unrecorded marriage or divorce slows progression.
- **Rejecting 3 consecutive offers**: may lower your queue position for a full cycle.
- **Undeclared income**: the system compares your declaration to bank statements — mismatch is a problem.
