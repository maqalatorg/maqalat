This guide brings everything a Saudi job seeker is entitled to in one place: **cash support, training, employment platforms, and health rights**. The goal: no entitlement should escape you unknowingly.

## First: who is officially a "job seeker"?

Before any talk of rights, you must be **officially registered as a job seeker**. Registration is via:

- **[Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/)**: the primary portal. Sign up with Absher, upload a CV, and set specialty and city.
- **Without registration**: you cannot benefit from any support programme.

**A complete CV** is the foundation of everything. Account with empty CV = nominated for zero jobs.

## Cash support — four tracks

### 1) Hafiz

- **Who qualifies**: Saudi seeker with no prior work experience or limited experience.
- **Value**: fixed monthly amount, currently around 2,000 SAR (updates annually).
- **Duration**: 12 months, non-extendable except in exceptions.
- **Condition**: active search (interviews, courses, no offer refusal).
- **[Full Hafiz article](/en/hafiz-job-seeker-support-guide)**.

### 2) SANED (unemployment insurance)

- **Who qualifies**: those previously in private sector who lost work involuntarily.
- **Main condition**: 12 months of SANED contribution in the last 36 months.
- **Value**: percentage of your last salary (45-60%).
- **Duration**: up to 12 months over a lifetime.
- **[Full SANED article](/en/saned-unemployment-insurance-guide)**.

### 3) Tamheer

- **Who qualifies**: recent Saudi graduate (within 6 years of graduation).
- **Value**: ~3,000 SAR monthly + work experience + certified completion.
- **Duration**: 6 months inside a private or public establishment.
- **[Full Tamheer article](/en/tamheer-programme-guide)**.

### 4) Developed Social Security (for low-income families)

- **Who qualifies**: Saudi families whose income falls below the minimum living threshold.
- **Value**: gap between family income and the threshold.
- **[Full Social Security article](/en/saned-social-security-guide)**.

## Second: training and qualification

### Doroob

- Free e-training platform.
- Courses in: freelancing, programming, marketing, languages, accounting.
- Hadaf-certified.
- **Unlimited** — take as much as you want.
- **[Full Doroob article](/en/doroob-free-courses-guide)**.

### Hadaf specialised courses

- Offered via Taqat as recommendations based on your specialty.
- Direct courses at accredited training centres.
- Monthly stipend during attendance in some tracks.

### International professional certification support

- Hadaf subsidises part of fees for certifications: PMP, CFA, Cisco, Google Certified, and more.
- For employed Saudis in specific fields.

## Third: employment platforms

### Public sector

- **[MCS (Employment Competitions Platform)](https://www.mcs.gov.sa/)**: general government jobs.
- **Jadarah**: ministry competitions.
- **Scholarships and special benefits**: via ministry sites directly.

### Private sector

- **[Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/)**: the official portal.
- **LinkedIn**: most famous globally and in Saudi Arabia.
- **Bayt.com** and **Naukri Gulf**: Gulf-India crossover.
- **Wuzzuf** and **Mbeet**: Egypt and Saudi Arabia.
- **Major companies directly**: Aramco, SABIC, STC, banks.

### Freelance work

- **Khamsat, Mostaql, Bahr**: Arabic.
- **Fiverr, Upwork, Freelancer**: international.
- Some require IBAN for earnings transfer.

### Entrepreneurship

- **[Monshaat](https://www.monshaat.gov.sa/)**: official SME support authority.
- Financing, training, business incubators.
- "Kafalah" programmes for financing projects on preferential terms.

## Fourth: health rights

A Saudi citizen (job seeker or not) has:

- **Free healthcare** at Ministry of Health centres and government hospitals.
- **[Sehhaty](/en/sehhaty-app-complete-guide)** for appointment booking.
- **Medications** from government pharmacies at no charge.
- **Major surgery** by referral from primary care.
- **Emergency** at any hospital free.

An unemployed resident needs **private health insurance** to receive care.

## Fifth: other rights

### Transport

- **Some programmes** subsidise transport for interviews.
- **Haramain and Riyadh trains** at subsidised prices for citizens.

### Housing

- If independent (single over 25) you can register with [Sakani](/en/sakani-housing-guide) at lower priority than families.
- **Housing subsidy** is part of Social Security in specific cases.

### Education

- **Government scholarships**: via Ministry of Education for demand fields.
- **University scholarships**: free for Saudi students at government universities.

### Justice

- **Court fees** are mostly nominal.
- **[Free legal consultation](https://www.moj.gov.sa/)** via Ministry of Justice for some cases.
- **Royal Court**: for humanitarian cases after exhausting authorities. See our article on [Royal Court petitions](/en/royal-court-petition-guide).

## A practical 30-day job seeker plan

**Week 1**:
- Register on Taqat and upload a strong CV.
- Register for Hafiz or SANED per your case.
- Create a LinkedIn profile.

**Week 2**:
- Start 2-3 Doroob courses.
- Apply to 10-15 jobs in your specialty.
- Reach out to your network — many opportunities come from there.

**Week 3**:
- Follow up interview dates.
- Practise common interview questions.
- Check **Tamheer** if you're a recent graduate.

**Week 4**:
- Evaluate what worked and what didn't.
- Broaden the search (adjacent specialties, other cities).
- Consider **freelance** as a parallel option.

## Mistakes that waste your chances

- **Relying on Hafiz alone**: support is temporary, not permanent.
- **Refusing "below your level" offers**: work beats unemployment.
- **Neglecting professional network**: 70% of jobs are filled via connections.
- **Searching in one specialty only**: expand to adjacent fields.
- **CV without details**: nominated to zero jobs.
- **Not updating skills**: the market changes — move with it.
