## What Absher Is and Who Runs It

Absher is a platform operated by the **Saudi Ministry of Interior** that links digital services across sectors including **Passports, Traffic, Civil Affairs, and Civil Defense**. Launched officially in **2010** and expanded gradually since, it now delivers hundreds of services connected with government and private entities via [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa) or its official app.

The identity and mobile data registered in Absher form the reference that **Nafath** (National Unified Access) uses to authenticate you when signing in to any other government platform — Qiwa, Sakani, Citizen Account, Najiz, Sehhaty, Tawakkalna, and others.

## Registration Step by Step

| Step | What you do |
|---|---|
| **1. Visit the site** | Open [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa) and choose "Create Account" |
| **2. Basic data** | Enter your ID (citizen) or iqama (resident) and date of birth |
| **3. Mobile number** | Must be officially registered in your name with a Saudi telecom (STC, Mobily, Zain, and others) |
| **4. SMS verification** | The system sends a code — enter it to confirm |
| **5. Username and password** | Choose a username and a password with letters, numbers, and symbols, min 8 characters |
| **6. Activation** | Mandatory step via any Saudi bank ATM or bank app — the account is confirmed instantly |

**Why banking activation is required**: it prevents creating Absher accounts with stolen ID data. Linking your account to your real bank account closes the identity-spoofing loophole.

## Common Problems and Fixes

- **"Mobile number doesn't match"**: the number entered isn't registered in your name with your telecom. Check your operator app (myStc, My Mobily, Zain) and update ownership data with your ID.
- **"My account is locked from failed attempts"**: the account locks after consecutive failed attempts and unlocks automatically after a cooldown, or use "Forgot Password" to reset.
- **"The SMS code doesn't arrive"**: could be forwarding or reception. Move to better coverage, or use "Resend Code" after the wait period shown on screen.
- **"Can't activate via bank app"**: most Saudi banks (Al Rajhi, SNB/AlAhli, Riyad Bank, and others) offer "Absher Activation" inside the app under government services. If your bank doesn't, use an ATM.
- **"ID not recognized"**: check the ID expiry. Expired IDs cannot create accounts.

## Account Security — Non-Negotiables

- **Never share your username or password** with anyone, even someone claiming to be from the Ministry. The Ministry never calls asking for these.
- **Enable two-factor verification** in account settings — an SMS code will be required on every sign-in.
- **Never open Absher links from SMS or WhatsApp** — type the URL directly in your browser.
- **Review the sign-in log** in account settings periodically to spot unfamiliar logins.

Fraud against Absher accounts is common, mainly via phishing links that mimic the real site. Genuine Absher is always on `absher.sa` or `my.absher.sa` — no other domain.

## Common Registration Pitfalls

Failures at the intersection of ministry systems are the usual culprits:

1. **National Address mismatch**: your address in [SPL](https://splonline.com.sa/) must be current. A missing or expired address blocks registration.
2. **Phone number registered under someone else**: if your Saudi SIM is registered under another person's ID (common with pre-owned SIMs), Absher won't accept it — update ownership with your telecom first.
3. **Fingerprint or photo data missing**: some accounts require an in-person visit to a Civil Affairs branch to capture or refresh biometric data.
4. **Expired ID or iqama**: expired documents cannot create or reactivate an account.

## Absher Business vs Individuals — Know the Difference

| Feature | Absher Individuals | Absher Business |
|---|---|---|
| **Who uses it** | Citizens and residents personally | Company representatives and delegates |
| **Main services** | Personal ID, traffic, passports, health | Employee sponsorship, visas, iqamas |
| **Login** | Personal Nafath | Delegate Nafath tied to the CR |
| **Access** | Everyone with a Saudi ID/iqama | Only authorized company representatives |
| **URL** | [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/) | Business section inside [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/portal/landing.html) |

For most expatriates, both matter: your personal Absher for your own services, and your employer's Business Absher for actions requiring employer approval.
