The tests administered by the Education and Training Evaluation Commission (ETEC) are a core gateway to university admission and government employment in Saudi Arabia. Confusing the three (Qiyas, Tahsili, and the Cognitive Ability Test) is common — each has a distinct purpose, audience, and set of rules. This guide clarifies the differences, dates, test locations, and how many retakes are allowed.

## Quick comparison table of the three tests

| Criterion | Qiyas (Qudurat / GAT) | Tahsili (SAAT) | Cognitive Ability Test |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Measures general analytical skills | Measures academic achievement in science-track high school | Measures cognitive ability for employment |
| **Target audience** | High-school students for university admission | Science-track students (medicine/engineering/sciences) | Job seekers, especially government roles |
| **Test content** | Verbal + Quantitative | Physics + Chemistry + Biology + Math | Verbal + Quantitative + Deductive + Spatial |
| **Duration** | ~3 hours (120 questions) | ~3 hours (160 questions) | ~2.5 hours (~100 questions) |
| **Score** | 100 | 100 | 100 |
| **Score validity** | 5 years | 5 years | 5 years |
| **Retake limit** | 5 times max | 5 times max | Per the employer's rules |
| **Fees** | SAR 100–150 | SAR 100–150 | SAR 100–150 |
| **Organizing body** | ETEC | ETEC | ETEC |

## 1. Qiyas (General Aptitude Test)

### Purpose

Measures a student's analytical and reasoning ability — **independent of any specific school subject**. It tests the thinking skills a student has built up over years of education, not memorized content.

### Main sections

**1. Verbal section (60 questions)**:
- Reading comprehension
- Verbal analogies (word-to-word relationships)
- Sentence completion
- Contextual error detection

**2. Quantitative section (60 questions)**:
- Arithmetic
- Geometry
- Basic algebra
- Data analysis

### Who is it for?

**General high-school students** — anyone planning to apply to Saudi public or private universities. Most universities require a minimum Qiyas score (varying by university and major).

### The weighted score

Most universities do not rely on Qiyas alone; they calculate a **weighted score** that combines:
- High-school GPA
- Qiyas score
- Tahsili score (for science majors)

Weighting varies by university and major. Check each university's admissions guide.

---

## 2. The Tahsili (Standardized Achievement Test)

### Purpose

Measures how well **a high-school student has absorbed the science subjects** they actually studied — unlike Qiyas, which tests general aptitude. Required for majors including:

- Medicine, dentistry, and pharmacy
- All engineering disciplines
- Sciences (physics, chemistry, biology)
- Some computer science tracks

### Question distribution

| Subject | Approximate share of questions |
|---|---|
| Math | 20% |
| Physics | 20% |
| Chemistry | 20% |
| Biology | 20% |
| Others (earth science / English) | 20% |

**Note**: The exact distribution differs between the traditional science track and the developed track.

### How it differs from Qiyas

A student who never seriously studied a subject can still pass Qiyas because it tests thinking ability. Tahsili, on the other hand, **will fail anyone who did not actually absorb the curriculum** — it tests curriculum content directly.

---

## 3. The Cognitive Ability Test

### Purpose

**Measures cognitive ability for employment** — mainly for government jobs. ETEC launched it to standardize evaluation criteria in employment competitions.

### Sections

- Verbal comprehension and linguistic reasoning
- Quantitative reasoning
- Logical / deductive reasoning
- Spatial and pattern reasoning

### Who is it for?

- Applicants to government jobs through the Jadarat platform or public-sector competitions
- Some large private-sector employers require it
- New graduates strengthening their employment profile

### Does it replace Qiyas?

**No.** Qiyas is for universities; the Cognitive Ability Test is for employment. Passing one does not substitute for the other if the other is required.

## When are the tests held?

All tests run in **windows** across the year:

### Qiyas and Tahsili

- **First window**: autumn/winter (typically October – December)
- **Second window**: spring/summer (typically March – June)

Each window spans several weeks, and the student picks a specific date and center at registration.

### Cognitive Ability Test

Available almost continuously throughout the year, with new sittings opened based on demand.

**For exact dates**: always check the official Qiyas platform at [etec.gov.sa](https://www.etec.gov.sa), since the schedule is updated annually.

---

## Where are the tests held?

Test centers are spread across **every region of Saudi Arabia**. Major cities host multiple centers:

- Riyadh, Jeddah, Makkah, Madinah, Dammam, Dhahran, Khobar
- Taif, Hail, Tabuk, Buraidah, Arar, Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jazan, Najran
- Mid-sized cities each host one or more centers

### Booking a center

When registering on the Qiyas platform, you choose:
1. The **city** you want to test in
2. The **specific center** (with available seats)
3. **Gender** (male and female centers are separate)
4. The **date and time** available

**Tip**: Registering early gives you a wider choice of centers and dates. Latecomers may have to travel to a different city.

## How many retakes are allowed?

### Qiyas and Tahsili

**Maximum: 5 attempts** within the 5-year score validity. The rules:

- Every attempt counts, whether you show up or not (once registered and paid).
- The **highest score** you achieved is the one used for admission.
- Once the 5 attempts are exhausted, you cannot retake until the score validity (5 years) expires.

### Cognitive Ability Test

Retakes are handled more flexibly, but the rules vary by employer. Check the specific job or competition requirements.

---

## Which first: Qiyas or Tahsili?

A common question among high-school students. The recommended order:

1. **Qiyas first**, at the end of grade 11 or start of grade 12. It requires no specific curriculum prep.
2. **Tahsili at the end of grade 12** — the curriculum is complete and the material is still fresh.

This order leaves you enough time to retake either test if the score falls below expectations.

## Preparation tips

### For Qiyas

- Learn **question-solving techniques**, not content memorization.
- Practice with **past Qiyas prep books** (available in bookstores).
- Apply **time management** — many questions, tight time.

### For Tahsili

- Review the **full science-track high-school curriculum** (physics/chemistry/biology/math).
- Focus on **fundamentals and laws**, not complex derivations.
- Use **past Tahsili papers** — the style repeats.

### For the Cognitive Ability Test

- Practice **logical reasoning** (series/analogies).
- Build **mental arithmetic speed**.
- Review **basic Arabic grammar**.
