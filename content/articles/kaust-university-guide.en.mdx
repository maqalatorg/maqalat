KAUST is not a Saudi university in the conventional sense. Founded in 2009 with a **$20 billion endowment** ([Wikipedia — KAUST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdullah_University_of_Science_and_Technology)), it teaches exclusively in English, is **coeducational from day one** (the first coed campus in Saudi Arabia), and offers no undergraduate degrees — **only master's and PhD** in STEM disciplines.

In QS Subject Rankings 2026, KAUST holds **eight subjects in the global top 100** ([KAUST Official Rankings](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/news/kaust-world-top-100-eight-subjects-saudi-arabia-leads-four)) and leads Saudi Arabia in four: Materials Science, Chemistry, Natural Sciences, and Electrical Engineering.

## Academic Divisions and Programs

KAUST is organized into **three academic divisions** containing 17 programs ([KAUST Study Programs](https://www.kaust.edu.sa/en/study/division-programs)):

### 1. Biological and Environmental Science and Engineering (BESE)
- Bioscience — genomics, molecular and cellular biology
- Bioengineering
- Environmental Science and Engineering
- Marine Science — applied Red Sea research
- Plant Science — desert agriculture and food security

### 2. Computer, Electrical, and Mathematical Sciences and Engineering (CEMSE)
- Computer Science
- Electrical and Computer Engineering
- Applied Mathematics and Computational Science
- Statistics

### 3. Physical Science and Engineering (PSE)
- Chemistry
- Chemical Engineering
- Materials Science and Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Applied Physics
- Earth Systems Science and Engineering

### Cross-Disciplinary
- Technology Innovation and Entrepreneurship

**Each program offers**: MS (thesis-based) + PhD. Typical duration: MS in two years, PhD in 4–5 years.

## Where Graduates Work

KAUST graduates **are not on the traditional Saudi career track**. Given the university's global research nature:

| Career Path | Typical Destinations |
|---|---|
| **Academia (~50%)** | Assistant professor roles at US, European, and Asian universities; researchers at KAUST itself; major Saudi universities |
| **Global Industry (~25%)** | Google Research, Microsoft, Meta, NVIDIA, global pharma, ExxonMobil, Shell, Saudi Aramco (research arm) |
| **Saudi Industry (~15%)** | Aramco (research), SABIC (research), Ma'aden, KACST, upcoming NEOM projects (STC AI Labs, Zain Digital) |
| **Entrepreneurship (~10%)** | Startups spinning out of KAUST — over 100 startups have originated from KAUST students/researchers since 2009, some acquired by global companies |

**Important note**: KAUST is **not a direct-employment feeder into the traditional Saudi market**. Graduates leave with an international research profile, and their career outcomes look more like MIT or Stanford graduates than typical Saudi public university graduates.

## What Makes KAUST Different

1. **Coeducational from day one** — first fully coed university campus in Saudi Arabia. Working environment close to Western universities.
2. **Massive endowment ($20B)** — abundant research funding, top-tier equipment, competitive faculty and researcher compensation, full fellowships.
3. **International character** — students and faculty from 100+ nationalities. Saudi citizenship is not an advantage.
4. **Fully integrated campus** — international schools for children, housing, beach, recreation → best suited to married researchers.
5. **Graduate-only focus** — no undergraduate distraction, full attention on advanced research.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**KAUST fits you if:**
- Your bachelor's is in STEM with a strong GPA (3.5+), and you want advanced research
- Your English is excellent (not merely "good")
- Your ambition is international academic/research, not Saudi government employment
- You want a coeducational, integrated campus resembling Western universities
- You're comfortable living in a semi-isolated campus (Thuwal is not a large city)

**KAUST may not fit you if:**
- You want an undergraduate degree (KAUST offers none)
- Your career goal is a traditional Saudi ministry or company (KFUPM or KSU are closer)
- You want a traditional single-gender Saudi environment
- Medicine is your specialty (KAUST does not offer medicine)

## Strong Alternatives

- **KFUPM** — English-language graduate engineering with undergraduate options
- **King Saud bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences** — for graduate health specialties
- **Alfaisal University** — private, English-language, research-oriented, both undergraduate and graduate
- **US and European universities** — through scholarship or self-funded programs
