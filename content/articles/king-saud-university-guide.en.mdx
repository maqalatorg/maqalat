King Saud University is not Saudi Arabia's top-ranked university، but it is **unmatched in history, size, and breadth**. Founded in 1957 as the first university in the kingdom, KSU today holds **21 academic colleges** covering medicine, engineering, sciences, business, humanities, and Sharia، a range no other Saudi university approaches.

In the QS World University Rankings 2027, KSU jumped from #143 to **#107 globally** ([SPA، 22 Saudi Universities in QS 2027](https://www.spa.gov.sa/en/N2618524))، a 36-place leap in one year, second only to KFUPM among Saudi institutions.

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Main campus in Riyadh + branches in Abha, Qassim, Al-Jouf |
| **Founded** | 1957، first university in the kingdom |
| **Original name** | University of Riyadh (until 1982) |
| **Character** | Comprehensive university covering nearly every discipline |
| **Language** | Arabic for humanities and Sharia; English for medicine, engineering, sciences |
| **Type** | Public، free for Saudis with monthly stipend |
| **Student body** | ~40,000 total, 7% international |
| **Main campus size** | 900 hectares (2,224 acres)، one of the largest in the Middle East |

The 2027 jump (143 → 107) is among the largest single-year improvements in the university's history, credited largely to expanded research publication output and rising citation rates.

## Colleges and Programs

Twenty-one colleges across four sectors ([Wikipedia، KSU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Saud_University)):

### Health Sector
- **Medicine** (1969)، Saudi Arabia's oldest medical college
- **Dentistry** (1976)، ranked #48 globally
- **Pharmacy** (1959)، ranked #48 globally
- **Applied Medical Sciences** (1976)
- **Nursing** (2003)
- **Prince Sultan College for Emergency Medical Services**، rare specialty in Saudi Arabia

### Science and Engineering Sector
- **Engineering** (1962)، civil, electrical, mechanical, industrial, petroleum, computer
- **Sciences** (1958)
- **Computer and Information Sciences** (1983)
- **Architecture and Planning** (1983)
- **Food and Agriculture** (1965)

### Business and Law Sector
- **Business Administration** (1959)
- **Law and Political Science** (2006)

### Humanities Sector
- Arts, Education, Languages and Translation (English, French, German, Chinese, Spanish, Japanese, Turkish), Tourism & Archaeology, Physical Education & Sports

### Community and Institutes
- **Applied Studies and Community Service**
- **Riyadh Community College**
- **15 specialised research institutes**

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's (project-based and thesis-based) + PhD for most disciplines.

## Where Graduates Work

Given its scale and breadth, KSU graduates spread across every sector of the Saudi economy:

| Sector / Discipline | Primary destination | Secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Medicine** | Ministry of Health hospitals + KFSH&RC + Specialist hospitals | Private sector (Saudi German Hospital, Dallah, Mouwasat) |
| **Dentistry** | Private clinics | Public hospitals, academic teaching |
| **Pharmacy** | Retail pharmacy chains (Nahdi, Al-Dawaa) | Pharmaceutical companies (SPIMACO, Tabuk Pharmaceuticals), hospitals |
| **Engineering (all disciplines)** | Aramco, SABIC, Saudi Electricity | Contractors, giga-projects (NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea), government agencies |
| **Computer Science** | STC, MCIT, banks | Google Cloud KSA, AWS KSA, startups |
| **Business and Finance** | Major Saudi banks | Capital Market Authority, listed companies, consulting (McKinsey, BCG) |
| **Law and Political Science** | Board of Grievances, Public Prosecution, Ministry of Justice | Private law firms, Royal Court |
| **Architecture and Planning** | Vision-era mega-projects (NEOM, Qiddiya, Diriyah) | Ministry of Municipal Affairs, consultancies |
| **Humanities and Languages** | Public education, Ministry of Foreign Affairs, media | Translation, publishing, cultural sector |
| **Sharia and Islamic Studies** | Education, Endowments, Sharia judiciary | Academic research |
| **Tourism and Archaeology** | Tourism Authority, Heritage Commission, major hotels | AlUla, Red Sea, Diriyah |

**Strategic note**: KSU graduates in Riyadh benefit from the university's proximity to ministries and major corporate headquarters، a shorter path to internships and direct employment than at any other Saudi university.

## What Makes KSU Different

1. **The broadest range of disciplines**، the only Saudi university offering medicine, engineering, Sharia, law, and tourism under one umbrella.
2. **Strategic location**، heart of Riyadh, near ministries and major corporate headquarters.
3. **University Medical City**، one of the largest academic medical complexes in the Middle East, offering clinical training few Saudi universities can match.
4. **Expanding research output**، 15 specialised research institutes, some with international partnerships.
5. **Dedicated women's campus**، full parallel infrastructure with the same programs available to male students.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**KSU fits you if:**
- You want a broad university with wide choices (you can switch majors within the same institution)
- Your goal is medicine, dentistry, or pharmacy (strong option with regional reputation)
- You prefer the Riyadh environment and its nearby career opportunities
- You aim for an academic / research trajectory (15 research institutes = research assistant opportunities)

**KSU may not fit you if:**
- You want a deeply specialised institution in a single discipline (KFUPM for engineering or KAUST for research are deeper)
- Top global ranking is your absolute priority (KFUPM #63 vs KSU #107)
- You prefer English across all disciplines (at KSU, humanities are in Arabic)

## Strong Alternatives

- **KFUPM**، for focused engineering, computing, and petroleum
- **King Abdulaziz University (KAU)**، comprehensive alternative in Jeddah
- **Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University**، world's largest women's university, also in Riyadh
- **King Saud bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences**، specialised medical-only university
- **Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University**، for Sharia and humanities
