Effat University is **a pioneer in Saudi women's education**. Founded in 1999 at the initiative of **Her Royal Highness Princess Effat Al Thunayan** ([Effat University — About](https://www.effatuniversity.edu.sa/about)), wife of King Faisal bin Abdulaziz, to become the first exclusively women's private university in the Kingdom. Today it operates under the **King Faisal Foundation** (the same foundation that sponsors Alfaisal University), giving it institutional stability and international accreditations.

It is a **research university, English-medium, and designed to produce women leaders in fields historically restricted to women in the region**: architecture, engineering, entrepreneurship, and design. The campus is in Jeddah, with a distinctive architectural design reflecting its mission.

**Distinction**: Effat is among the few women's universities globally to hold **three specialized international accreditations simultaneously**: AACSB for Business, ABET for Engineering, NASAD for Design — a rare combination in the Arab region.

## Colleges and Programs

### 1. College of Engineering

- Electrical & Computer Engineering
- Computer Science
- Information Systems Engineering
- **Accreditation**: ABET
- **Distinction**: Among the first women's engineering colleges in the region with ABET accreditation

### 2. College of Architecture & Design

- **Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)** — 5 years, among the strongest women's architecture programs in the region
- Urban and Architectural Design
- Visual & Digital Production (Graphic Design)
- Product Design
- **Accreditation**: NASAD (National Association of Schools of Art and Design) — US-based

### 3. College of Business

- BS Business — tracks in Entrepreneurship, Marketing, Finance, Economics
- **MBA**
- MS Entrepreneurship — among the first in the region
- **Accreditation**: AACSB

### 4. College of Humanities & Social Sciences

- Psychology — among the strongest Saudi women's psychology programs
- Translation
- Early Childhood Education
- Instructional Design

**Notable point**: Effat alumnae **appear densely across Saudi women's leadership sectors**, particularly in architecture, entrepreneurship, and mid-to-senior management. The university intentionally designs its programs to produce **leaders, not just employees**.

## What Makes Effat Different

1. **Clear founding mission** — Effat was not built to be an ordinary women's college but to open fields historically closed to Saudi women (architecture, engineering). That mission shows in every detail.
2. **Triple international accreditation (AACSB + ABET + NASAD)** — extremely rare globally for a women's university to hold all three.
3. **King Faisal Foundation sponsorship** — the same sponsor as Alfaisal University, meaning strong institutional standards, stable funding, and an influential alumni network.
4. **Campus designed for women** — a safe and comprehensive academic environment (sports, arts, full facilities).
5. **Strong academic psychology department** — among the best Saudi women's psychology programs according to specialists.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**Effat suits you if:**
- You want a strong women's university with English-medium instruction
- Your ambition is architecture, engineering, entrepreneurship, or design
- You prefer a fully women-only environment (no coeducation)
- Your family can accommodate private-university tuition
- You want internationally recognized accreditations for graduate study abroad

**It may not suit you if:**
- You want a coeducational campus (Alfaisal, KAUST, PMU are coeducational)
- Your major is Medicine or Pharmacy (not offered at Effat)
- You prefer a tuition-free public university (Princess Nourah, King Abdulaziz women's sections)
- You want the highest possible international ranking (Alfaisal and KAUST rank higher internationally)

## Strong Alternatives

- **Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University** — public, Riyadh, the world's largest women's university, tuition-free
- **Alfaisal University** — private, Riyadh, coeducational, strongest in Medicine
- **King Abdulaziz University — women's section** — public, Jeddah, wide range of majors, tuition-free
- **Dar Al-Hekma University** — private women's university, Jeddah, close geographic alternative
