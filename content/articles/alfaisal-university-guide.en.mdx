Alfaisal University is different. Established by royal decree in 2002 under the **King Faisal Foundation** ([Alfaisal University، About](https://www.alfaisal.edu/about)), with teaching launched in 2008. Its location is not incidental: north Riyadh, adjacent to **King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre**، one of the largest tertiary referral hospitals in the Middle East. This means medical students receive clinical exposure in a world-class environment from their earliest years.

It is a **private, non-profit research university**, English-medium, coeducational from day one, and its medical curriculum is built in partnership with the **Cleveland Clinic**.

## Colleges and Programs

### 1. College of Medicine

- **MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine & Surgery)**، 6 years + 1 internship year
- Curriculum built in partnership with Cleveland Clinic (clinical phase)
- Clinical rotations at King Faisal Specialist Hospital, Al Hammadi, and others
- Graduate programs: MS Infectious Diseases, MS Genomic Medicine, MS Biomedical Ethics
- Accreditation: WFME (World Federation for Medical Education) + Saudi Commission for Health Specialties

### 2. College of Engineering

- Electrical Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Software Engineering
- Biomedical Engineering
- IT Engineering
- Accreditation: ABET (US-based accreditation for engineering programs)

### 3. College of Business

- BBA، with tracks in Finance, Marketing, Entrepreneurship, General Management
- **Executive MBA (EMBA)**، the most well-known program
- Master of Finance
- Master of Healthcare Administration
- Accreditation: AACSB (representing ≤6% of business schools globally)

### 4. College of Science and General Studies

- Life Sciences، strong pathway for pre-med or biomedical research
- Mathematics and Computer Science
- Physics, Chemistry

### 5. College of Pharmacy

- **Doctor of Pharmacy (PharmD)**، 6 years
- Clinical training in major Riyadh hospitals
- Accredited by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties

## Career Outcomes، Where Alumni Work

Alfaisal graduates typically target **the private sector and major hospitals**, not traditional government roles:

| Field | Typical employers |
|---|---|
| **Medicine** | King Faisal Specialist Hospital, Al Hammadi, Saudi German, National Guard hospitals; fellowships in US/Canada/UK |
| **Engineering** | Aramco, SABIC, Ma'aden, major contractors, regional and local tech companies |
| **Business** | Local banks (Al Rajhi, SNB, Riyad Bank), consulting firms (McKinsey, PwC, Deloitte), large Saudi family businesses, PIF and sovereign entities |
| **Pharmacy** | Private hospital pharmacies, global pharma (Pfizer, GSK, Merck), premium retail pharmacy chains |

**Notable point**: Many Alfaisal graduates continue graduate studies at US and European universities, particularly in medicine and engineering, benefiting from Alfaisal's international accreditations that make their transcripts readily recognized abroad.

## What Makes Alfaisal Different

1. **Located inside a referral hospital ecosystem**، the medical school is effectively embedded within King Faisal Specialist Hospital. No other Saudi university offers this.
2. **Cleveland Clinic partnership**، the medical curriculum is designed with a top-tier US medical institution.
3. **Triple international accreditation**، AACSB + ABET + WFME across different colleges. Rare among Saudi universities.
4. **Full coeducation since founding**، mixed lectures, classroom environment closer to Western universities.
5. **Small size relative to national universities**، better faculty-to-student ratios and more classroom interaction.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**Alfaisal suits you if:**
- Your English is excellent (90%+ of study, exams, and research is in English)
- You want a coeducational environment similar to Western universities
- Your academic/professional ambition is international, and you plan graduate study abroad
- Your family can carry private-university tuition (Alfaisal is not free)
- You want a medical school with early clinical training in a world-class referral hospital

**It may not suit you if:**
- You prefer a traditional Saudi environment in Arabic with gender-separated classes
- Your career target is a government role that specifically requires a public-university diploma (rare, but exists in some tracks)
- Tuition is a genuine hardship (Saudi public universities are strong and free)

## Strong Alternatives

- **King Saud bin Abdulaziz University for Health Sciences**، public, health-sciences focus, similar environment
- **Prince Sultan University**، private, Riyadh, stronger in Business and Engineering
- **Prince Mohammad Bin Fahd University**، private, Al Khobar, Eastern Province option
- **KAUST**، graduate only (MS/PhD), fully research-oriented
