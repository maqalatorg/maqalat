Tamheer is one of the [Hadaf fund's](/en/hrdf-hadaf-guide) most important programmes for recent Saudi graduates. Launched in 2016, it combines three rarely-together benefits: **a good monthly stipend + practical experience inside an establishment + certified completion**. Official URL: [tamheer.hrdf.org.sa](https://tamheer.hrdf.org.sa/).

## Why Tamheer differs from Hafiz

Many graduates struggle to choose: **Hafiz or Tamheer?** The difference is large:

| Criterion | Hafiz | Tamheer |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Cash support without work obligation | Training inside an establishment |
| Duration | 12 months | 6 months |
| Practical experience | No | Yes — core |
| Stipend | Fixed, lower | Fixed, higher |
| Post-programme employment chance | Medium | High |
| Acceptance | Automatic on eligibility | Requires establishment approval |
| Combining both | Not combinable |

**Practical rule**: **If you're a fresh graduate, Tamheer is significantly better** — you get an equivalent or better stipend, build a CV, and raise post-programme employment odds.

## Eligibility conditions

To register in Tamheer:

1. **Saudi nationality**.
2. **Recent graduate**: bachelor's or diploma from an accredited institution. Graduation within the last 6 years.
3. **Currently unemployed**: neither public nor private sector.
4. **Less than one year of prior experience** in the same specialty.
5. **Has not benefited from Tamheer before**.
6. **Age below 40**.
7. **Serious job seeker**: registered on Taqat.

## Tamheer tracks

There are two main tracks:

### Private-sector Tamheer

- The establishment posts trainee vacancies in specific specialties.
- The trainee applies directly or is nominated by Hadaf.
- The stipend is fully paid by Hadaf (the establishment doesn't pay the trainee).
- The establishment provides the work environment, supervisor, and training plan.

### Public-sector Tamheer

- Government entities open seasonal openings.
- Higher competition (Sharia, law, accounting, IT graduates).
- Jobs available in ministries like: Justice, Islamic Affairs, Municipalities, Education.
- After Tamheer, possibility of official employment via a job competition.

## Steps to register in Tamheer

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [tamheer.hrdf.org.sa](https://tamheer.hrdf.org.sa/) or [taqat.sa](https://www.taqat.sa/) |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher |
| 3 | Choose "Tamheer" → "Training Seeker" |
| 4 | Review your academic data (pulled from Muwathaq) |
| 5 | Complete your CV carefully — it's the foundation |
| 6 | Browse listed opportunities, or await nomination |
| 7 | Apply to suitable openings |
| 8 | Prepare for the establishment interview |
| 9 | On acceptance, sign the Tamheer contract electronically |

## Programme flow

**Day one**:
- Welcome from the establishment.
- Presentation of the 6-month training plan.
- Assignment of the direct supervisor.
- Signing the evaluation plan.

**Over 6 months**:
- **Full-time** (40-45 hours/week).
- **Direct supervisor** evaluates you weekly.
- **Gradually increasing tasks**: start simple, grow in complexity.
- **Monthly reports** to the establishment and Hadaf.
- **Complementary courses** sometimes (technical or behavioural).

**Sixth month**:
- Final evaluation.
- **Certificate**: issued by Hadaf with official seal.
- **Employment offer**: often extended by the same establishment.
- **If no offer**: you have a strong CV to apply elsewhere.

## Monthly stipend

- **Paid by Hadaf** directly to your bank account — not by the establishment.
- **Paid at the end of each training month**.
- **Attendance is a condition**: excessive unexcused absence is deducted from the stipend.
- **No tax on it** — full amount is yours.

## Trainee obligations

- **Daily attendance**: like a regular employee.
- **Commit to establishment rules**: dress, hours, conduct.
- **Periodic reports to Hadaf**: on the portal.
- **Do not resign without cause**: may bar you from other Hadaf programmes.
- **Disclose any income**: cannot combine with another job.

## When is the certificate better than the job offer?

**If offered employment at excellent terms**: accept. Job security beats market gamble.

**If offered at weak terms (very low salary, short contract, arbitrary conditions)**: take the certificate and apply elsewhere — your CV is now much stronger than before.

**Rule**: don't sign an employment contract before Tamheer ends. The establishment knows you have limited options on the last day — negotiate earlier.

## Trainee mistakes to avoid

- **Accepting the first Tamheer offer without thought**: wrong specialty = 6 wasted months.
- **Treating the programme as a break**: some think Tamheer is easy — they lose the recommendation.
- **Not engaging with the team**: professional relationships after Tamheer are valuable.
- **Refusing "small" tasks**: every task is a learning opportunity.
- **Resigning before programme end**: forfeits stipend for remaining months + negative flag at Hadaf.

## Tips to maximise value

- **Choose a large establishment if possible**: the brand on CV beats a small one.
- **Request real tasks**: don't spend 6 months copying files.
- **Build a network**: LinkedIn, engage with colleagues.
- **Keep examples of your work**: reports, projects, presentations — all CV material.
- **Read your contract carefully**: some establishments require post-Tamheer no-hire — reject this term.
