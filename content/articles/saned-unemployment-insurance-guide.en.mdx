SANED is Saudi Arabia's **unemployment insurance** for private-sector Saudis. Launched in 2014, managed by the [General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI)](https://www.gosi.gov.sa/). The concept is simple: **a Saudi private-sector employee has a monthly SANED contribution deducted from salary, in exchange for a financial safety net if they lose their job involuntarily**.

## SANED is not Hafiz — a common confusion

Many think SANED and Hafiz are the same. The difference is fundamental:

| Criterion | [SANED](#) | [Hafiz](/en/hafiz-job-seeker-support-guide) |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Insurance | Social support |
| Who qualifies | Those who worked and contributed before | Those who never worked, or lost work without prior contribution |
| Funding source | Employee + employer + state contribution | Hadaf government budget |
| Benefit value | Percentage of your previous salary | Fixed amount for everyone |
| Payment duration | 12 months lifetime | 12 months per instance |
| Managing body | GOSI | Hadaf fund |

**Practically**: **SANED is better for those who had a higher salary**, since the benefit is a percentage of it. **Hafiz is better for those who never worked**.

## How do you enrol in SANED?

**Not optional**. Every Saudi working in the private sector (company or establishment) is **auto-enrolled** by law. The contribution is deducted monthly from your salary and appears on your GOSI statement.

**Contributions**:
- **Employee**: a small percentage of salary.
- **Employer**: matching or higher percentage.
- **The state**: supports the scheme via additional contribution.

You can check your SANED contribution via:
- The **Taminaty** app — the official GOSI app.
- GOSI portal at [gosi.gov.sa](https://www.gosi.gov.sa/).

## Eligibility conditions

To receive a SANED benefit after unemployment:

1. **Saudi nationality**.
2. **You were employed in the private sector**.
3. **You contributed to SANED for at least 12 months** in the last 36 months before unemployment (may be intermittent).
4. **Unemployment was involuntary**: dismissed, made redundant, business closure, fixed-term contract ended. **Resignation does not qualify** (limited exceptions).
5. **Capable of working**: no incapacitating disability.
6. **Actively seeking work**: registered on Taqat, attending interviews, not refusing reasonable offers.
7. **No other steady income**: no pension, no active business, no other job.
8. **Age below statutory retirement**.

## Benefit calculation

The benefit is computed as a **percentage of your average contributory salary over your last working months** (typically 24 months):

- **First months**: higher percentage (up to 60% of average salary).
- **Later months**: tapers gradually (may drop to 45% in the final months).

Simple example (approximate for illustration — see gosi.gov.sa for current rates):

- Your average salary is 8,000 SAR.
- First months: 4,800 SAR monthly.
- Later months: 3,600 SAR monthly.

**Benefit cap**: subject to an official upper ceiling that no benefit exceeds regardless of salary.

## Steps to claim the SANED benefit

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | After unemployment, open [gosi.gov.sa](https://www.gosi.gov.sa/) or the Taminaty app |
| 2 | Sign in with Absher or National Access |
| 3 | Choose "SANED" → "Unemployment Benefit Request" |
| 4 | Upload the end-of-service letter from your former employer |
| 5 | Upload last salary details |
| 6 | Register on Taqat as a job seeker — mandatory |
| 7 | Upload bank account details |
| 8 | Review and submit |

Eligibility decision typically issued within **15-20 working days**, monthly payment begins immediately after.

## Beneficiary obligations during payment

- **Stay registered on Taqat**: updated CV, active search.
- **Attend interviews you're nominated for**.
- **Attend training courses**.
- **Do not refuse reasonable job offers**: refusing 3 times = suspension.
- **Immediately disclose any new income or job**.
- **Disclose travel outside the Kingdom**.

**Breaching any of these** suspends payment and may trigger clawback.

## When does the benefit end?

- **Getting a new job**.
- **Statutory period expiry**: 12 months over lifetime (divisible).
- **Starting a productive business**.
- **Reaching statutory retirement age**.
- **Obligation breach**.
- **Emigrating permanently**.

## Can you use SANED more than once?

Yes, but the total does not exceed **12 months over your entire working life**. If you claimed 6 months then found work then lost it again, you have 6 months remaining — but you must meet the contribution condition (12 months within the last 36) before each new claim.

## Combining SANED with other programmes

- **Hafiz**: not combinable with SANED. Choose one by your situation.
- **Citizen Account**: combinable (no conflict).
- **Social Security**: partially combinable (based on family status).
- **Tamheer**: not combinable with SANED.

## Benefit for those who resigned voluntarily?

**Generally no**. Resignation excludes you from SANED. **Rare exceptions**:
- Resignation due to documented persecution by employer.
- Resignation due to arbitrary transfer to a remote location.
- Resignation for documented health reasons.

In these cases, the request is reviewed exceptionally.
