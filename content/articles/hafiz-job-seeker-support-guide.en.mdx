Hafiz is one of Saudi Arabia's most well-known job-seeker support programmes, launched in 2011 and marking a major shift in how the state views unemployment: **from passive assistance to seriousness-conditional support**. Run by the [Human Resources Development Fund (Hadaf)](/en/hrdf-hadaf-guide) via [hrdf.org.sa](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/) and the [Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/) platform.

## The Hafiz philosophy — why conditional?

Before Hafiz, support was paid on mere proof of unemployment. Hafiz changed the rule: **support in exchange for actual search**. The beneficiary must:

- Upload an updated CV to Taqat.
- Attend interviews they are nominated for.
- Not refuse a job offer matching their skills at a reasonable salary.
- Attend training courses they are assigned.

The idea: **support is a bridge to a job**, not a substitute. Refusing 3 offers in a row without a convincing reason results in removal from the programme.

## Hafiz tracks

Over the years, Hafiz evolved into several tracks:

| Track | Beneficiary |
|---|---|
| **Hafiz Seeker** | Saudi looking for their first serious job |
| **Hafiz Difficulty** | Those facing special circumstances (disability, sole breadwinner, remote region) |
| **Hafiz Deserving (formerly "Motalak")** | Those whose Hafiz Seeker term ended without finding work despite documented effort |
| **Hafiz Nationalisation** | Paid to the establishment that hired the Saudi (not to the Saudi directly) |
| **Hafiz Employment** | Additional support upon signing the first employment contract |

## Eligibility conditions

For Hafiz Seeker (the most common):

1. **Saudi nationality**.
2. **Age**: 20+, under 60.
3. **No current job**: neither public nor private sector.
4. **No other steady income**: no pension, no social security, no productive business.
5. **Not a full-time student**.
6. **Independence or family responsibility**: independent single or head of household.
7. **Serious Taqat registration**: non-empty CV.
8. **No conflicting programme**: Hafiz is independent of Citizen Account (combinable), of Developed Social Security (partially combinable), but conflicts with some other employment programmes.

## Registration steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [hrdf.org.sa](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/) or [taqat.sa](https://www.taqat.sa/) |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher |
| 3 | Choose "Register for Hafiz" |
| 4 | Review data pulled from Absher |
| 5 | Complete: education, experience (if any), languages |
| 6 | Upload your CV to Taqat (mandatory) |
| 7 | Set desired job specialty and city |
| 8 | Upload bank details (personal account) |
| 9 | Review the summary and submit |

Eligibility decision typically issued within **15-30 working days**, notified by SMS.

## After approval — how support works

- **Support is paid monthly** to your registered bank account.
- **Duration**: 12 months maximum.
- **Continuation is conditional on**:
  - **Attending interviews**: refusing without a convincing reason = negative flag.
  - **Disclosing any income**: any temporary work or project must be immediately disclosed.
  - **Attending assigned courses**: two consecutive absences = suspension.
  - **Updating CV and monthly profile**.

## When does the support end?

- **Getting a job**: ends immediately, not combined with salary.
- **12-month term expiry**: with the option of "Hafiz Deserving" in specific cases.
- **Breach of obligations**: refusing 3 offers, repeated no-shows at courses, false declaration.
- **Reaching statutory retirement age**.
- **Emigrating from the Kingdom** permanently.

## Mistakes that stop your support immediately

- **Refusing "below your level" job offers**: three refusals = suspension. The system considers any employment better than unemployment.
- **Concealing temporary or commercial work**: uncovered via bank and GOSI linkages.
- **Outdated CV**: fewer matches, weaker ranking.
- **Missing a course without acceptable excuse**.
- **False declaration of a non-existent qualification**.

## Tips to maximise benefit

- **Use the 12 months strategically**: take Doroob courses, accept temporary training, build a practical CV.
- **Accepting Tamheer training beats refusing work**: [Tamheer](/en/tamheer-programme-guide) combines support with work experience.
- **Enable Taqat alerts**: every matching job comes to you.
- **Prepare your professional profile**: LinkedIn, certificates [officially verified](/en/muwathaq-document-verification-guide) (Mu'ahhal for university, Nataeji for general education).
- **Specialise in a demand field**: some fields (sales, customer service, programming) recruit much faster.

## Hafiz vs other support programmes

| Programme | With Hafiz? |
|---|---|
| [Citizen Account](/en/citizen-account-guide) | Combinable (no conflict) |
| [Developed Social Security](/en/saned-social-security-guide) | Partially combinable based on family status |
| [Reef](/en/reef-agricultural-support-guide) | Combinable (no conflict if Reef is a secondary activity) |
| [Tamheer](/en/tamheer-programme-guide) | Not combinable — choose one |
| Saned (unemployment insurance) | Not simultaneous — switching allowed |

## Hafiz in the Saudi Job-Seeker Support Ecosystem

Hafiz isn't the only program for unemployed job seekers — it's part of an interconnected ecosystem run by the Ministry of Human Resources and Hadaf. Understand the difference before choosing:

| Program | Target audience | Type of support |
|---|---|---|
| **Hafiz** | Job seeker without prior employment | Temporary monthly stipend + training |
| **[Tamheer](/en/tamheer-programme-guide)** | Recent university graduate | 6-month training inside an establishment + stipend |
| **[Saned](/en/saned-unemployment-insurance-guide)** | Recently unemployed (private sector) | Insurance compensation for 3-12 months |
| **[Social Security](/en/saned-social-security-guide)** | Lowest-income families | Permanent pension with conditions |

Practical distinction:
- **Hafiz** for those who **never worked before** or were unemployed for a long stretch.
- **Saned** for those who **lost their job due to the establishment** (involuntary unemployment).
- **Tamheer** for those who **graduated recently and haven't found work**.

## When Hafiz Ends — Transitioning to Actual Employment

Hafiz is temporary — not a permanent income source. Transitioning from it to an actual job is the goal. Transition tools:

1. **[Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/)**: the official portal for applying to private-sector jobs.
2. **[Doroob](/en/doroob-free-courses-guide)**: free training in in-demand skills (programming, marketing, languages).
3. **Job fairs**: organized by universities and Hadaf periodically.
4. **Social networks**: Saudi LinkedIn is very active — build a professional profile.
5. **Direct application to employers**: for establishments in higher Hadaf brands.

**Critical advice**: don't rely on Hafiz for more than 6-9 consecutive months. The program is designed as a bridge, not an endpoint.

## Hafiz Eligibility — Frequently Missed Conditions

Beyond the basic eligibility requirements, some conditions often trip up applicants:

- **Age restriction**: typically 20-35 years old at time of application; outside this range requires exceptional justification.
- **Residence requirement**: must be physically resident in Saudi Arabia for the full period of support, with defined limits on travel outside the Kingdom.
- **Active job search**: you must apply to a minimum number of positions through Taqat monthly, or complete a minimum number of training hours through Doroob.
- **No dual benefits**: cannot be simultaneously receiving Hafiz plus employer subsidy plus Social Security — the system detects overlaps automatically through GOSI and Absher linkage.
- **Reporting requirement**: any change in your situation (marriage, employment offer, relocation) must be reported within 30 days or benefits are recovered retroactively.

Failing any of these silently disqualifies you and may create a negative record affecting future support applications.
