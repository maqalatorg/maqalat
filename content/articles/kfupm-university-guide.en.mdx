In June 2025, King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals became **the first Arab university to enter the QS World Rankings top 100** at #67. The following year (2027 edition) it climbed further to **#63** ([Official KFUPM Rankings](https://www.kfupm.edu.sa/about-us/our-story/facts-and-rankings)). This guide unpacks what sits behind the number: actual programs, subject-by-subject performance, and the labor-market pathways for graduates in the Saudi economy.

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Dhahran, Eastern Province |
| **Founded** | 1963 (as College of Petroleum & Minerals) → 1975 (university status) |
| **Character** | Research-intensive university focused on engineering, sciences, and business |
| **Language** | English (except Islamic and Arabic Studies) |
| **Type** | Public — free for Saudis, with a monthly stipend |
| **Gender** | Historically male; progressive coeducation since 2020 |
| **Campus** | Fully self-contained (housing + facilities) in Dhahran |

The second notable figure: **#2 globally in patents granted by the US Patent Office** — a hard-to-fake measure of applied research output.

## Where Graduates Work

KFUPM graduates don't job-hunt through public portals. **The university's relationship with major employers is institutional and direct**. Realistic labor-market map by discipline:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Strong secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Petroleum Engineering** | Saudi Aramco | Oilfield services (Schlumberger, Halliburton, Baker Hughes) |
| **Geosciences** | Aramco + Ma'aden | Saudi Geological Survey, exploration companies |
| **Chemical Engineering** | SABIC + Aramco (refining) | Ma'aden, petrochemicals cluster in Jubail & Yanbu |
| **Mechanical Engineering** | Aramco | Saudi Electricity, SABIC plants, GACA, SAMI |
| **Electrical Engineering** | Saudi Electricity | Aramco, STC, Mobily, Zain, contractors |
| **Computer / Data Science** | STC, Google Cloud KSA, AWS KSA | Banks, giga-projects, startups |
| **Industrial Engineering** | Aramco, SABIC, Ma'aden | Management consulting (McKinsey, BCG), logistics |
| **Aerospace Engineering** | SAMI (Saudi Arabian Military Industries) | Saudia Airlines, GACA |
| **Mining Engineering** | Ma'aden (largest future employer by a wide margin) | Aramco (minerals division) |
| **Architecture & Planning** | NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global | Ministry of Municipal Affairs, consultancies |
| **Accounting & Finance** | Saudi banks | Aramco (finance), Capital Market Authority, listed companies |
| **Management & Marketing** | Listed companies, Aramco (planning) | Consulting, government strategy roles |

**Worth knowing**: Aramco runs the **College Preparatory Program (CPP)** for top KFUPM students — full scholarship with guaranteed employment upon graduation. A significant share of each cohort enters CPP before finishing their degree.

## What Makes KFUPM Different

Five points separating it from most Saudi universities:

1. **English as the primary language of instruction** — graduates leave able to write technical, present, and negotiate in English. A concrete advantage in international markets.
2. **Institutional industry partnership** — no need to wait for career fairs. Major employers (Aramco, SABIC, Ma'aden) maintain offices inside campus.
3. **#2 globally in US patents** — the applied research output is real, not marketing.
4. **Self-contained campus** — high-focus environment, student housing, integrated facilities. Suits students who want academic immersion.
5. **Alumni network** — KFUPM graduates hold leadership positions across Aramco, SABIC, and government ministries — an internal employment pipeline.

## Before You Apply — Honest Read

**KFUPM fits you if:**
- You're serious about engineering or science with an applied orientation
- You want a clear pipeline to Aramco / SABIC / Ma'aden
- Your English is strong, or you're prepared for a serious Prep-Year
- You prefer a focused isolated environment over a busy city

**KFUPM may not fit you if:**
- You want a pure humanities or literary discipline (KFUPM covers STEM + Business only)
- You want an expansive social university life beyond the campus
- Medicine, dentistry, or pharmacy are your goals (KFUPM does not offer them)

## Strong Alternatives

- **King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST)** — graduate-only, English, research-intensive; but no undergraduate.
- **King Saud University** — broad engineering options in Riyadh with an urban environment.
- **King Abdulaziz University** — engineering, medicine, and business in Jeddah.
- **Prince Mohammad bin Fahd University** — private, geographically close to Dhahran, English-taught, but far lower global ranking.
- **Prince Sultan University** — private in Riyadh, English engineering and business programs.
