The Saudi insurance sector is one of the fastest-growing in the region and operates under a strict regulatory framework that protects customer rights. This guide gives you a full map: types, companies, what each policy covers, and how to choose.

**Core regulation**: every insurance company in Saudi Arabia is licensed by the [Insurance Authority](https://www.ia.gov.sa/) — the independent body established by a 2023 Council of Ministers decision, which took over this role from the Saudi Central Bank (SAMA). Health insurance specifically is additionally supervised by the [Council of Health Insurance](https://www.chi.gov.sa/). Any company without these licences = **fraud**.

## Insurance types in Saudi Arabia

### Motor insurance

The most common, mandatory for every registered vehicle:

| Type | What it covers | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| **Third-party** | Damage your car causes to others only (other vehicles, people, property) | Older cars, tight budget, mandatory minimum |
| **Comprehensive** | Third-party + damage to your own car (accidents, theft, fire, flood) | Newer, more valuable cars, bank-financed |
| **Comprehensive + roadside assistance** | Comprehensive + tow, fast repair, replacement car | Heavy users or long-distance drivers |

**Rough pricing**: third-party ~500-1,500 SAR annually. Comprehensive ~2,000-6,000 SAR based on car value, driver, and age.

### Health insurance

Mandatory in Saudi Arabia for private-sector workers and family members:

| Type | Provided by | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| **Private-sector employee insurance** | Employer | Mandatory, minimum coverage set by the Unified Health Insurance Policy (details vary by job and salary) |
| **Family insurance** | Employer usually | Includes spouse / children under 25 |
| **Individual insurance** | Purchased by the individual | For unemployed residents and those wanting higher coverage |
| **Tourist visitor insurance** | Bought by the visitor to enter Saudi Arabia | Part of the tourist visa |
| **Government insurance** | Free for unemployed citizens | Via primary-care centres and Ministry of Health hospitals |

**Health coverage levels**: policies differ in payout cap and which hospital network they accept — from the mandatory minimum (limited network, basic payout cap) up to pricier policies covering higher-end hospitals and service levels. **We could not confirm an official "Tier A/B/C/VIP" naming scheme documented by the Council of Health Insurance** — don't assume this labelling when reviewing your own policy; check the actual coverage details in your contract or at [chi.gov.sa](https://www.chi.gov.sa/).

### Property insurance

| Type | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **Home structure** | Damage from fire, floods, explosion, lightning |
| **Home contents** | Furniture and appliances inside the home |
| **Comprehensive home** | Structure + contents + theft |
| **Office and retail** | Damage to commercial property + equipment |
| **Buildings under construction** | Accident risks during construction |

**Note**: many Saudi homes (especially owner-occupied) are uninsured, so kitchen fires and floods cause big personal losses yearly. A comprehensive home policy costs ~500-2,000 SAR annually — a small price for protection.

### Travel insurance

For Saudis travelling abroad:

| What it covers | Detail |
|---|---|
| Emergency medical | Sudden medical costs while abroad |
| Trip cancellation | Loss of ticket and hotel due to force majeure |
| Lost luggage | Compensation for missing luggage |
| Trip delay | Hotel and meal reimbursement |
| Personal liability | Damage you cause to others |

**Mandatory for a Schengen visa** (30,000 EUR minimum) and some countries. Fees ~100-300 SAR for a two-week trip, per destination and age.

### Life insurance (Takaful)

| Type | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Family Takaful** | Payout to family upon head of household's death |
| **Savings Takaful** | Savings + protection, participant receives amount at term end or family upon death |
| **Disability insurance** | Monthly stipend if you suffer a disability preventing work |

**Shariah note**: conventional life insurance is debated in Islamic jurisprudence. Saudi providers offer "Takaful" alternatives compliant with Shariah rules.

### Professional liability

For doctors, engineers, lawyers, and accountants — protects from malpractice suits. Legally required for some professions (doctors specifically).

## Major insurance companies in Saudi Arabia

The most well-known Insurance Authority-licensed companies. Full, current list at [ia.gov.sa](https://www.ia.gov.sa/):

### Tawuniya

- Largest and oldest (founded 1986).
- Largest shareholders are government bodies: the General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI).
- Offers all types, with focus on comprehensive motor and health.
- Leader in institutional health insurance.

### Bupa Arabia

- Especially known in health.
- Wide network of hospitals and clinics.
- Competes with Tawuniya for health market share.
- High coverage caps.

### MedGulf

- Health-focused too.
- Strong network in mid-tier health insurance.
- Competitive pricing.

### Salama

- Pure Takaful (Takaful company).
- Focus on motor and property.

### AlAhli Takaful

- Subsidiary of Saudi National Bank.
- All types, with focus on family Takaful (Islamic life insurance).

### Walaa Cooperative Insurance

- Diverse types.
- Innovative products like personal-accident insurance.

### AXA Cooperative Insurance

- AXA France's Saudi arm.
- All types, global standards.

### Malath

- Focus on motor and property.
- Strong network in Eastern regions.

### Others

- United Commercial Insurance (UCA).
- Al-Sagr Insurance.
- Wataniya Insurance.
- SABB Takaful.

## Comparing insurance companies

Every company gives you a different quote for the same coverage. Official comparison tools:

- **[Tameeni (tameeni.com)](https://www.tameeni.com/)**: licensed platform showing quotes from 6-10 motor insurers simultaneously.
- **[Naz (naz.sa)](https://www.naz.sa/)**: similar alternative.
- **Insurer websites directly**: each has a quote calculator.
- **Insurance broker**: a licensed office that compares for you — takes commission from the insurer, not you.

**Factors that set premium**:

| Factor | Impact |
|---|---|
| Policy type (comprehensive/third-party) | Largest |
| Vehicle value (for motor) | Higher value = higher premium |
| Driver age | Under 25 or over 60 = higher premium |
| Accident record | Prior accidents = higher premium |
| Use type | Commercial > personal |
| Licence type (private/commercial) | Commercial higher |
| Annual mileage | Higher = higher premium |
| Residence city | Riyadh/Jeddah > small cities |
| Nationality | Sometimes applied |

## Tips for choosing the right policy

- **Don't blindly pick the cheapest**: you may find at claim time it covers nothing.
- **Review "exclusions"**: every policy has cases it doesn't cover — read them.
- **Verify the network**: health? which hospitals? motor? which repair shops?
- **Check coverage cap**: what's the maximum payout?
- **Compare at least 3 quotes**: don't buy from the first offer.
- **Ask about claim speed**: some insurers pay in a week, others in 6 months.
