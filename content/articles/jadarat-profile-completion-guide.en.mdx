Jadarat ([jadarat.sa](https://jadarat.sa/)) is Saudi Arabia's official government-sector employment portal. It was launched to unify every government hiring process in one place — from ministries to authorities to universities to municipalities. **Completing your profile to 100% is not optional** if you're serious about landing a government job: the profile completion rate feeds directly into the shortlisting algorithm, and every missing field lowers your chance of being called.

## How Jadarat Works — The Core Idea

The model is simple and structured:

1. **A government entity** posts a job with specific requirements (qualification, experience, exams needed).
2. **The user** applies from their Jadarat profile — data is pulled automatically from your file.
3. **The system** compares each applicant's qualifications against the job's requirements and ranks them by match, descending.
4. **The entity** reviews the top-ranked, invites them to exams and interviews.
5. **The decision** is communicated through Jadarat and SMS.

**Key point**: the entity **doesn't manually view your file** first — the algorithm does. If your file is incomplete, it may never reach an HR officer's hands.

## Completion Percentage — Why It Matters

Jadarat shows your profile completion rate on your personal dashboard. The rate moves based on how many fields are filled:

| Completion rate | Effect |
|---|---|
| **30-50%** | Minimum to apply, shortlisting chances very weak |
| **50-70%** | Considered for basic jobs, but priority goes to others |
| **70-90%** | Good shortlisting, competitive with the highest rated |
| **90-100%** | Top priority, appears first in search results |

**Quantified impact**: per HRDF's periodic reports, users at 100% completion receive interview invitations at a rate **exceeding 3× that of peers** at 60%.

## Seven Components of a 100% Profile

### 1. Basic Personal Data

These are obvious but small mistakes derail your file:

- **Full quadrinomial name**: matching Absher exactly. Any difference (even a space) = mismatch.
- **National ID and civil status**: pulled automatically from Absher.
- **Hijri and Gregorian birth dates**: verify they are correct — some systems mix them.
- **Marital status**: single/married/divorced/widowed. Hidden from the public but used for some jobs.
- **National Address**: pulled from [National Address (SPL)](https://splonline.com.sa/). Ensure it's active.
- **Mobile and email**: more important than anything else — that's how invitations reach you.

**⚠️ Most common error**: an old email you don't open. Update it immediately.

### 2. Educational Qualification

The most heavily-weighted section in filtering:

- **University degree**: bachelor's, diploma, master's, or PhD.
- **University**: matching its official name (King Saud University, not KSU).
- **Specialization**: full official name.
- **Graduation year and GPA**: exact numbers.
- **Automatic verification**: Jadarat checks your certificate via [Mowaad](/en/muwathaq-document-verification-guide) — if not in the system, ask your university to add it first.
- **Certificate from outside Saudi**: needs **equivalency** from [ETEC](https://etec.gov.sa/). Without equivalency, it doesn't count.

**Tip**: add all your certificates, even lower ones (e.g., if you have both bachelor's and PhD, add both).

### 3. Previous Experience

Many ignore this section — a critical mistake:

- **Every previous job**: start and end dates, employer, job title.
- **Job description**: 3-5 points explaining your actual responsibilities.
- **Achievements**: specific numbers (e.g., "sales rose 30% in 12 months").
- **Experience verification**: Jadarat is linked to [GOSI](/en/gosi-pension-guide) and [Qiwa](/en/qiwa-platform-guide) — your private-sector employment periods are auto-verified.

**⚠️ Legal note**: don't lie in experience. Security screening catches it, and you face multi-year application bans.

### 4. Professional Certifications

This section can flip your ranking:

**Recognized professional certifications**:

| Field | Accepted certifications |
|---|---|
| **Project management** | PMP, PRINCE2, Agile/Scrum |
| **Accounting & finance** | CPA, CFA, ACCA, CMA, [SOCPA](https://socpa.org.sa/) certificates for Saudi accountants |
| **Human resources** | SHRM, PHR, Institute of Public Administration certificates |
| **IT** | AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Cisco, Microsoft |
| **Cybersecurity** | CISSP, CEH, CompTIA Security+ |
| **Digital marketing** | Google Ads, Google Analytics, Meta Blueprint |
| **Quality** | ISO Lead Auditor, Six Sigma |

**⚠️ Decisive condition**: **the certificate must be current and globally accredited**. Jadarat verifies some automatically, some manually. **Forged certificates permanently freeze your file**.

### 5. Approved Training Courses

Different from professional certifications — training courses with completion certificates:

**Accepted courses**:
- **[Doroob](/en/doroob-free-courses-guide)**: every Doroob course is auto-accepted in Jadarat (same parent organization).
- **[Technical and Vocational Training Corporation (TVTC)](https://www.tvtc.gov.sa/)**: officially accredited professional preparation courses.
- **Institute of Public Administration**: specialized administrative courses.
- **Saudi universities**: accredited enrichment courses (continuing education).
- **International courses from Coursera, edX, Udemy** — accepted only if accompanied by a verifiable certificate from a known university or company.

**⚠️ Warning**: free YouTube courses without certificates **don't count** — don't add them. Even paid Udemy courses only count if their certificate is electronically verifiable.

### 6. Professional License

For specializations that require them:

- **[Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS)](https://www.scfhs.org.sa/)**: for doctors, pharmacists, nurses, medical technicians.
- **[Saudi Organization for Chartered and Professional Accountants (SOCPA)](https://socpa.org.sa/)**: for accountants.
- **[Saudi Council of Engineers](https://www.saudieng.sa/)**: for engineers.
- **[Saudi Bar Association](https://www.sba.gov.sa/)**: for lawyers.
- **[Capital Market Authority (CMA)](https://cma.org.sa/)**: for those working in investment.
- **Islamic preaching license**: for du'ah.

**⚠️ If the job requires a professional license**: **without the license, you won't be shortlisted at all**. Renew your license before applying.

### 7. English Language Test

For jobs requiring it (most technical and international positions):

**Accepted tests**:
- **[STEP](https://www.etec.gov.sa/)**: Saudi Test of English Proficiency from [Qiyas (ETEC)](https://etec.gov.sa/). Most widely accepted for government jobs.
- **[IELTS](https://www.ielts.org/)**: British, 6.0+ score for advanced positions.
- **[TOEFL iBT](https://www.ets.org/toefl.html)**: American, 79+ score.
- **[Duolingo English Test](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/)**: accepted for some modern positions.

**Verification**: Jadarat verifies STEP automatically. For foreign tests, upload the certificate itself for manual review.

## Effect of a Complete Profile — By the Numbers

Per [HRDF's annual reports](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/) and available studies on government employment patterns:

- **100% profile** = **3× higher** chance of getting an interview versus a 50% profile.
- **Adding an accredited professional certificate** = raising technical entity interest by **40-60%**.
- **Adding a certified English test** = unlocking international and technical jobs (35% of government jobs require it).
- **Complete experience with achievement numbers** = distinguishes you from those who never worked in filtering.
- **Valid professional license** = non-negotiable requirement for medical, engineering, and legal jobs.

## Mistakes Many Make

1. **Relying on the initial profile without updates**: your file must be updated whenever you get a certificate or promotion.
2. **Ignoring the experience section for those without prior jobs**: add university training, internships, volunteer work — all count.
3. **Uploading unaccredited certificates**: pauses your file for manual review, and may register in your negative record.
4. **Neglecting the STEP test**: many rely on IELTS, but STEP is cheaper and faster for government jobs.
5. **Relying on intermediaries**: **no official intermediaries** for Jadarat. Anyone offering to "raise your completion rate" is a scam.

## Strategic Tips for Applicants

- **Update your profile monthly**: even with small items (short course, completion certificate).
- **Prepare for STEP early**: without an English test, 30-40% of jobs are closed to you.
- **Attend government job fairs**: you get direct notification of jobs before public posting.
- **Watch competition opening times**: each entity opens its competitions in seasons — follow ministry accounts on social platforms.
- **Don't apply to everything randomly**: applying for jobs that don't match your qualifications lowers your algorithmic ranking.
