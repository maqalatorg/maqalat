Doroob is the free e-training arm of the [Hadaf fund](/en/hrdf-hadaf-guide). Launched to provide certified training paths for Saudis at no cost, with completion certificates recognised on CVs. URL: [doroob.sa](https://www.doroob.sa/). Because it's government-funded, there are no ads and no limits on how many courses you can take.

## Why Doroob differs from Coursera or Udemy

| Criterion | Doroob | Coursera / Udemy |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Zero for Saudis | Paid (subscription or per course) |
| Content | Designed for the Saudi market | Globally generic |
| Language | Mostly Arabic | Mostly English |
| Certificate | Certified by Hadaf | From the platform or university |
| Employer recognition | Accepted on Saudi CVs | Depends on platform reputation |
| Instructor | Saudi trainers or Arabised | International |

## Registration and use steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Go to [doroob.sa](https://www.doroob.sa/) and click "Sign In" |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher |
| 3 | Review your data (pulled from Absher) and select your specialty |
| 4 | Search the course catalog or use the "Fields" filter |
| 5 | Enrol in the course — no prior admission required |
| 6 | Watch videos, solve exercises, pass the final quiz |
| 7 | Download the PDF certificate with the Doroob seal |

## Tips to maximise value

- **Pick 2-3 tracks at a time**: one complete track (like "Freelancing") beats 10 scattered courses.
- **Solve the exercises, don't skip**: understanding lives in doing, not watching.
- **Study Saudi examples**: many courses include local market case studies.
- **Set a fixed time**: an hour daily beats 7 hours on the weekend.
- **Add the certificate to your CV**: it's recognised on Taqat as a documented skill.

## Doroob certificates vs international certificates

**Doroob certificate**: proves you completed a certified training course, and can be listed on your CV. Saudi employers recognise it.

**International certificate** (PMP, CFA, Google Ads, AWS…): issued by independent exam. Doroob may offer **preparation** only; you still need to register separately with the issuing body and pay exam fees. Hadaf subsidises part of the fee under its "Professional Certifications" programme for specific fields.

## Common mistakes

- **Enrolling in 20 courses then dropping**: lowers your Hadaf account rating.
- **Skipping the final quiz**: no certificate without it.
- **Relying on Doroob alone for a practical skill**: add real projects to have impact.
- **Not registering on Taqat**: Doroob + Taqat together open a stronger job pipeline.

## Doroob in the HRDF Ecosystem

Doroob doesn't operate in isolation. [HRDF (Hadaf)](/en/hrdf-hadaf-guide) runs four interconnected arms that work together to support Saudi employment:

| Tool | Role | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **[Doroob](https://www.doroob.sa/)** | Free e-learning | After graduation, during job search, to sharpen skills |
| **[Taqat](https://www.taqat.sa/)** | Official job-market portal | Post CV, apply for jobs, track career path |
| **[HRDF Subsidy Fund](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/)** | Financial support for employers hiring Saudis | Employer benefits directly, not the employee |
| **[Specialised Localisation Programmes](https://www.hrdf.org.sa/programs/)** | Targeted tracks (women, disabled, tech grads) | Depends on your category |

Your single Hadaf account covers Doroob and Taqat — no separate registration needed. Each tool has its own logic: Doroob for learning, Taqat for applying. Always link them together on your CV.

## Real Use Case: The Freelance Track

Because Saudi Arabia's freelance market has grown visibly since the [official Freelance Portal](https://freelance.sa/) launched in 2019 with the Saudi Freelance License, Doroob built a comprehensive track for this specialty. The track (approximate scope): 20-30 hours spread across three weeks, covering:

1. **Officially registering the Freelance License** (free procedure via the Freelance Portal)
2. **Building a professional profile** on platforms like [Mostaql](https://mostaql.com/), [Khamsat](https://khamsat.com/), Fiverr, and Upwork
3. **Client management**: proposals, contracting, non-payment risks, dispute resolution
4. **Tax governance**: when to register with ZATCA? What's the VAT exemption for individual clients? When to issue a tax invoice?

This track graduates a freelancer who can legally sell services in Saudi Arabia, without surprises with [ZATCA](https://zatca.gov.sa/) or social security. The course parallels reality, not just theory.

## Doroob vs International Learning Platforms

Doroob competes with global platforms on specific dimensions. Compare before committing to a paid course elsewhere:

| Criterion | Doroob | Coursera / Udemy |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | Zero for Saudis | Paid (subscription or per-course) |
| Content depth | Designed for Saudi labor market | Global, general |
| Language | Arabic primarily | English primarily |
| Certificate recognition | Recognized by HRDF/Taqat/Saudi employers | Recognized by international employers |
| Time commitment | 2-100 hours per track | Similar range |
| Employer verification | Instant via Taqat linkage | Requires manual verification |

For entry into the Saudi labor market: **Doroob first**, always. For senior international careers: **combine Doroob + a global certificate** to signal both local relevance and global standard.
