The Developed Social Security programme provides a monthly pension for Saudi households whose income falls below a minimum-living threshold. It was issued by **Royal Decree (M/32) dated 2/4/1442 AH**, and applications opened on **20 November 2021** as a comprehensive reform of the old Social Security scheme. It is managed by the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD) via [sbis.hrsd.gov.sa](https://sbis.hrsd.gov.sa/). Source: [Saudi Press Agency launch announcement](https://www.spa.gov.sa/2305712).

## Old vs. developed Social Security

| Criterion | Old scheme | Developed scheme |
|---|---|---|
| Calculation basis | Fixed lump per case | Gap between income and minimum-living threshold |
| Assessment | Static periodic | Dynamic by household size and income |
| Data sources | Mostly manual declaration | Automated links to Absher, banks, records |
| Review | Every few years | Monthly if income changes |
| Priority groups | Predefined lists | Computed mathematically by need |

## Who qualifies?

Eligibility is assessed on four joint criteria:

1. **Nationality**: Saudi citizen or in the same category.
2. **Residency**: Inside the Kingdom (absence not exceeding the allowed limit, typically 90 days per year).
3. **Independence**: Independent housing via rental contract or title deed under the head of household's name, or documented independence within an extended-family home.
4. **Income and assets**: Total household income below the minimum-living threshold, and assets (property, vehicles, deposits) within the cap.

**Priority categories** (given special treatment in review and disbursement): orphans, divorced women, widows, elderly (60+), people with disabilities, prisoners' families, and accident victims unable to work.

## How the monthly benefit is calculated

The formula:

> **Benefit = minimum-living threshold (by household size) − actual household income**

The **minimum-living threshold** is set annually by MHRSD and varies with household size:

| Household size | Monthly threshold (illustrative) |
|---|---|
| 1 (head only) | Base |
| 2 | Base + adult dependent |
| 3 | Base + 2 dependents (adult + child) |
| 4+ | Additional amount per extra dependent |

**Note**: Actual figures update annually via ministerial decree. We don't publish outdated numbers because they mislead. Use the official calculator on [sbis.hrsd.gov.sa](https://sbis.hrsd.gov.sa/) for current amounts.

## Registration steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [sbis.hrsd.gov.sa](https://sbis.hrsd.gov.sa/) and choose "New Registration" |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher account |
| 3 | Complete head-of-household and dependent details (data pulls from Absher) |
| 4 | Specify marital status accurately (married/divorced/widowed/independent single) |
| 5 | Attach proof of housing (rental contract registered in Ejar, or title deed) |
| 6 | Attach income sources: salary/GOSI/commercial/other |
| 7 | Attach bank account details under head of household's name |
| 8 | Review the summary and submit |

Applicants are notified of the eligibility result by SMS and portal alert. Results are issued periodically within the Ministry's monthly cycles.

## Payment schedule

Payment is officially made on the **1st of each Gregorian month**. Weekend rule:

- If the 1st falls on **Friday** → paid on **Thursday** (day before).
- If the 1st falls on **Saturday** → paid on **Sunday** (day after).

Funds are deposited via the Madad system into the bank account registered under the head of household's name.

## Post-approval obligations

- **Disclose income changes within 30 days**: any new job, salary change, or new income source must be updated — otherwise it counts as concealment.
- **Continued residency in the Kingdom**: prolonged absence suspends payment.
- **Annual review**: beneficiaries must log in yearly to update their file.

## Appealing a rejection or benefit reduction

You have the right to appeal within **90 days** of the eligibility result, via the "Objection" service on the portal, with supporting documents. Appeal review takes up to **60 days** from submission. Only **one appeal** per eligibility cycle is allowed, and if it is rejected you cannot resubmit for 12 months.

## Social Security vs. Citizen Account

| Criterion | Developed Social Security | Citizen Account |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Guarantee minimum living standard | Offset fuel/energy price reform |
| Basis | Income gap vs. living threshold | Graduated design by income |
| Payment day | 1st of each month | 10th of each month |
| Managed by | MHRSD | MHRSD (separate arm) |
| Combining both | Allowed — neither requires the other |

## Priority Categories

The executive regulations grant **priority categories** special treatment in review and disbursement:

- **Orphans**: children who have lost one or both parents.
- **Widows and divorced women** and other independent heads of household.
- **Elderly citizens** aged 60+.
- **People with disabilities** officially registered in the National Disability Registry via the [Ministry of Human Resources](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/).
- **Families of prisoners** and equivalents.
- **People unable to work** due to accidents or chronic illness.

For the official regulatory text of priority categories and rights, see the [Executive Regulations of the Social Security System](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/node/841018) on the Ministry's site.

## When Your New Job Conflicts With Social Security

If you get a paid job after being approved for Social Security, support is not cancelled immediately. The system recalculates:

- Your new income is added to household income, and the gap between total income and the minimum-living threshold is recomputed.
- If income exceeds the threshold, benefit stops; if it stays below, benefit continues at a reduced amount.
- **Disclosure is mandatory**: failing to report income changes leads to retroactive fund recovery per the executive regulations.
