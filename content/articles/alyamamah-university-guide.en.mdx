Alyamamah University has **deep educational roots**. Its origin traces to Alyamamah Institutes, founded by Sheikh Abdullah bin Suleiman Al Rajhi more than 50 years ago ([Alyamamah University، About](https://www.yu.edu.sa/about)). The institution became **Alyamamah Private College** in 2001 and a university in 2004.

It is a non-profit private university in Riyadh, English-medium in its modern programs, and typically offers **lower tuition** than the larger private peers (Alfaisal, PSU), with nationally accredited academic quality and active international accreditation pathways.

**Note**: Alyamamah is smaller than PSU and Alfaisal and its international ranking trails them, but its national accreditation is complete and its programs are recognized across the Saudi private and government sectors.

## Colleges and Programs

### 1. College of Business Administration

- BBA، tracks in Finance, Marketing, Human Resources, Supply Chain
- BS Accounting
- **MBA**، with executive tracks
- Accreditation: AACSB pathway (Eligibility stage)

### 2. College of Engineering & Architecture

- **Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)**، among the strongest in Riyadh's private sector
- Industrial Engineering
- Mechanical Engineering
- Accreditation: Active ABET pathway

### 3. College of Computing & Information Systems

- Computer Science
- Information Systems
- Cybersecurity
- Networking and IT

### 4. College of Law

- LLB، from the first generation of private law programs in the Kingdom
- Master of Commercial Law (LLM)
- Focus on commercial law, labor law, and arbitration

## Career Outcomes، Where Alumni Work

Alyamamah graduates operate **effectively in the Riyadh market fabric**:

| Field | Typical employers |
|---|---|
| **Business & Accounting** | Saudi banks (admin and operations divisions), mid-tier accounting and audit firms, family businesses, finance departments in large corporates |
| **Architecture & Engineering** | Engineering consultancies in Riyadh, real-estate developers, Ministry of Municipal Affairs, NEOM/The Line/Qiddiya projects (moderate opportunities) |
| **Computing & Cybersecurity** | STC, mid-tier tech firms, corporate IT departments, government-digital transformation firms |
| **Law** | Mid-tier law firms, corporate legal departments, Ministry of Justice, regulatory authorities |

**Notable point**: Alyamamah graduates **compete effectively in the Riyadh market**, but their placement rate at top global consulting firms (McKinsey, BCG) is lower than that of PSU and Alfaisal graduates. The gap is not in ability but in institutional brand recognition.

## What Makes Alyamamah Different

1. **Lower tuition**، Alyamamah's tuition is typically lower than Alfaisal's and PSU's for comparable programs, making it the "mid-market" private option.
2. **Deep educational heritage**، Alyamamah Institutes trained a full generation of educators and professionals before the university existed.
3. **Early Law College**، one of the first private law programs in Saudi Arabia, with a large alumni network in law and the judiciary.
4. **Small-medium size**، an academic environment close to students with strong faculty interaction.
5. **Riyadh location**، internships in corporate Riyadh without relocation.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**Alyamamah suits you if:**
- You want a Riyadh-based private university with tuition below Alfaisal/PSU
- Your major is in Business, Law, or Architecture
- You want a personal academic environment of medium size
- Your English is solid or you can complete the foundation year
- Your family wants a private university with recognized local heritage

**It may not suit you if:**
- You want the highest possible international ranking (PSU and Alfaisal are ahead internationally)
- Your major is Medicine or Pharmacy (not offered at Alyamamah)
- You want full international accreditation (AACSB/ABET) now, not in-pathway (Alyamamah is currently on the pathway)
- You want a strong alumni network at top global consulting firms

## Strong Alternatives

- **Prince Sultan University (PSU)**، private, Riyadh, full AACSB/ABET accreditation
- **Alfaisal University**، private, Riyadh, strongest in Medicine, coeducational
- **King Saud University**، public, Riyadh, tuition-free, wider range of majors
- **Imam Muhammad bin Saud University**، public, Riyadh, tuition-free, strong in Law and Sharia
