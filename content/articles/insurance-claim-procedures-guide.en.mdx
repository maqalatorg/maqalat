An insurance claim is the moment that tests what you paid premiums for. Insurers are legally required to pay, but **your procedures** determine how fast and how much. This guide walks four common cases: car accidents, home fires/damage, theft, and health insurance.

**General rule**: everything documented with photos and paperwork is processed fast. Everything relying on "your word" is delayed or rejected.

## Car accident insurance claim

### Steps at the scene

| # | Step | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Don't move the cars unless there's a safety need | Najm needs the scene as it happened |
| 2 | Call Najm (920005145) | Early reporting saves time |
| 3 | Photograph: both cars, scene, plates, damage on both sides, any signage, the road | Visual evidence that cannot be manipulated |
| 4 | Request the Najm report — save the reference | The report is the basis of the claim |
| 5 | Exchange details with the other party | Plate number, phone, insurer, policy |
| 6 | Do not sign any admission of fault at the scene | Fault determination is Najm's job, not the parties |

### Filing the claim with the insurer

Within 24 hours of the accident:

1. **Call your insurer**: the number is on your policy. Report the accident and Najm report number.
2. **Download their app or open their site**: open a new claim.
3. **Upload documents**:
   - Najm report.
   - Photos of the car and accident.
   - Copy of the valid licence.
   - Copy of the valid Istimara.
   - Copy of the insurance policy.
   - Filled claim form (they provide it).
4. **Insurer assigns an approved workshop**: bring the car there.
5. **Damage assessment**: an insurer's representative inspects and issues a cost report.
6. **Repair** or **cash settlement** per your policy.

### Official timelines

- The insurer must respond within **15 working days** of receiving all documents.
- Workshop repair typically takes **2 weeks to 2 months** depending on damage type.
- **Cash settlement if chosen**: 7-21 days after value agreement.

### Mistakes that hurt your claim

- **Leaving the scene before Najm**: counts as fleeing, raises your fault %.
- **Repairing the car before insurer approval**: unapproved repair costs won't be reimbursed.
- **Settling privately with the other party**: you have no authority to determine fault.
- **Delaying document submission by a week**: gives grounds for delaying the response.

## Home insurance claim — fire and flood

Electrical fires, floods, and water leaks are leading causes of home damage. If your home is insured:

### Steps immediately after the incident

1. **Stop the danger first**: electricity, gas, water — before photographing.
2. **Call Civil Defence** (998) for fires. **Safety first**.
3. **Photograph damage before any repair**: every room, every damaged item, every corner.
4. **Save purchase receipts if possible**: for appliances and expensive furniture.
5. **Don't discard damaged items**: the insurance assessor needs to inspect them.

### Filing the claim

- **Within 48 hours** of the incident, contact the insurer.
- **Civil Defence report**: print from their site or obtain on the spot.
- **Detailed photos of the scene**.
- **Damage list**: prepare a table of every damaged item and estimated value.
- **Insurer sends an assessor**: inspects and sets the payout value.
- **Objecting to the assessment**: allowed — request a second assessment by an independent expert.

**Important note**: many home policies exclude floods, or damages from negligence (like non-approved electrical connections). **Read your policy before relying on it**.

## Theft claim

For cars, homes, and contents:

### Steps

1. **Immediate police report** (999): the official theft report is the basis of any claim.
2. **Detailed list of stolen items** with purchase receipts or older photos.
3. **Police investigation report** roughly a week later.
4. **File the claim with the insurer** with all documents.
5. **Insurer investigation**: may send a representative for inspection and questioning.
6. **Payout decision**: typically 30-60 days.

**Warning**: comprehensive insurance usually covers theft. Third-party doesn't. Home insurance "comprehensive with contents" covers it partially or fully.

## Health insurance claim

### Two main paths

**1) Standard case — direct via card**:
- Go to an in-network hospital/clinic.
- Show insurance card + ID.
- Service delivered without payment (except co-pay if any).
- Insurer pays the hospital directly.

**2) Exception case — pay then reimbursement**:
- Out-of-network clinic or a foreign country.
- You pay first and keep receipts.
- Submit to insurer: receipts, medical report, prescriptions, bank account details.
- Insurer pays you part or all per the policy.

### Pre-approval requests

Some procedures need insurer approval before execution: elective surgeries, expensive imaging (MRI, CT), pregnancy and delivery, special medications. If you pay without approval, the insurer may deny reimbursement.

### Appealing denied coverage

- Insurer sends a written rejection with reason.
- You may appeal to the insurer's medical committee with additional medical justification.
- If appeal denied, escalate to the **Council of Health Insurance (CCHI)** via [cchi.gov.sa](https://www.cchi.gov.sa/) → Complaints.

## When to file a complaint against the insurer

**A SAMA complaint succeeds if**:
- The insurer delayed more than 15 working days without justification.
- Rejected the claim without a written reason.
- Requested impossible or unreasonable documents.
- Valued damage below its actual worth.
- Pressured you to accept a partial payout.

**How to file a SAMA complaint**:
1. Open [sama.gov.sa](https://www.sama.gov.sa/) → "Customer Complaints."
2. Sign in with Absher.
3. State the dispute details + documents + policy number.
4. SAMA requires the insurer to respond within 10 working days.

**Committee for Insurance Disputes at the Ministry of Justice**: last resort, for major disputes. Litigation takes months, but the judgment is binding.

## Tips for dealing better with the insurer

- **Keep a formal tone**: no emotion — every contact is documented.
- **Correspond by email when possible**: leaves a documented trail.
- **Save reference numbers**: every number you receive, save it.
- **Don't describe the accident in words implying your fault**: fault determination is Najm's/police's job.
- **Read your contract annually**: coverage sometimes changes on renewal.
