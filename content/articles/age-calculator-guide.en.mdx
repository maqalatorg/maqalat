## How to Calculate Age Precisely

Age calculation is not simply **current year − birth year**. To get a precise age you need to:

1. **Compute the year gap**: `current year − birth year`
2. **Adjust for months**: if the birth month hasn't passed yet this year, subtract one year.
3. **Adjust for days**: if the birth day hasn't passed yet this month, subtract one month.

**Example**: born March 15, 2000, today is January 1, 2026.
- Initial gap: 2026 − 2000 = 26
- March hasn't arrived yet → actual age is 25 years, 9 months, and 17 days.

The tool above runs this calculation automatically in both Gregorian and Hijri at the same time.
