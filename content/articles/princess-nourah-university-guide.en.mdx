Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University is not just a large university، it is **the largest women's university in the world**. The eastern Riyadh campus stretches across **13 million square meters (13 km²)** with **600 smart buildings**, a library holding **over 2 million books**, and an automated internal transport system، infrastructure unmatched by any women's university globally ([Wikipedia، Princess Nourah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Nourah_Bint_Abdul_Rahman_University)).

The university serves **33,825 students** today across **14 colleges** covering the full academic spectrum from medicine to design.

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Eastern Riyadh (under King Salman Bridge، Prince Mohammed bin Salman Road) |
| **Founded** | 1970 (women's teacher training college) → 2008 (independent university) → 2011 (current campus opened) |
| **Character** | Comprehensive women's university، largest in the world by this classification |
| **Student body** | 33,825 female students |
| **Campus size** | 13 km² (13 million m²)، **largest for any women's university globally** |
| **Infrastructure** | 600 smart buildings, 2M+ book library, automated internal transport |
| **Type** | Public، free for Saudi women with monthly stipend |
| **Historical milestone** | First women's driving school in the kingdom (2018، six months before the general lift on driving restrictions) |

**Direct read**: The global QS ranking is modest, but **the improvement trajectory is rapid**، the young university (17 years as an independent institution) competes with older universities. The QS Most Improved Arab region award confirms the direction.

## Colleges and Programs

Fourteen colleges across four sectors:

### Health Sector
- **Medicine**
- **Dentistry**
- **Pharmacy**
- **Nursing**
- **Health and Rehabilitation Sciences**، physical therapy, respiratory therapy, rehabilitation, medical laboratories, radiology, clinical nutrition

### Science and Technology Sector
- **Sciences**، physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, statistics
- **Computer and Information Sciences**، computer science, software engineering, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence
- **Business Administration**، accounting, finance, management, marketing, management information systems

### Humanities and Design Sector
- **Arts**
- **Education**
- **Design and Arts**، interior design, graphic design, fashion, fine arts
- **Home Economics**
- **Social Work**
- **Languages and Translation**، English, French, Chinese, Turkish, Japanese, Spanish

### Continuing Education
- **Community College**، computer science and management diploma tracks
- **Arabic Language Institute for Non-Native Speakers**
- **Preparatory Year Program**، study skills, English, communication, computing

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's + PhD for most programs.

## Where Graduates Work

PNU graduates enter every sector of the Saudi market:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Secondary paths |

| **Dentistry** | Private sector | Public hospitals, academic teaching |

**Strategic note**: **Vision 2030 raised Saudi women's labor market participation from 17% to 36% by 2025**، and PNU graduates represent the largest academic cohort in this transformation, especially in health, financial, and technology specialties.

## What Makes PNU Different

1. **Infrastructure without rival**، 13 km², 600 buildings, internal transport, 2M+ book library. Facilities better than many major public universities.
2. **Complete women's focus**، an environment designed for Saudi female students without academic compromise.
3. **The teaching hospital (King Abdullah)**، inside the campus, providing complete clinical training for medical students.
4. **Specialised programs rare in Saudi Arabia**، advanced home economics, women-focused design, deep social work.
5. **International recognition**، partnerships with global universities (UK, US, Australia), student exchange programs.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**PNU fits you if:**
- You are looking for a women's university with world-class infrastructure
- You are from Riyadh or willing to live in campus housing (available inside the campus)
- Your specialty is medicine, health, business, or technology
- You prefer a fully women's environment over coeducational universities

**PNU may not fit you if:**
- You want an advanced engineering specialty (KFUPM now admits women in select graduate programs)
- Global ranking is your priority (KSU/KAU/KFUPM rank higher)
- You are outside Riyadh and cannot relocate

## Strong Alternatives

- **King Saud University**، has an independent women's campus with similar disciplines, higher ranking
- **King Abdulaziz University**، for Jeddah and the west, women's campus
- **Princess Nourah is a model for deep women-specific programs**، the closest alternative is a women's campus within a comprehensive university like KSU/KAU
- **US and European universities**، through scholarship programs
