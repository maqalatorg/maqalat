Admission to the Saudi Armed Forces (Ministry of Defence, MOD) happens through a **unified portal** launched by the ministry to consolidate applications across all branches (Land, Naval, Air, Air Defence, Strategic Missile, Medical Services). Exclusive official URL: [tajnid.mod.gov.sa](https://tajnid.mod.gov.sa/). Any other link in search results should not be trusted.

## Career tracks in MOD

| Track | Qualification | Preparation time | Nature of work |
|---|---|---|---|
| University officer | Recognised university degree | 9-12 months | Command, specialisation |
| High school officer | High school with high GPA + Military College | 3-4 years | Command |
| New soldier | High school or equivalent | 6-9 months basic training | Combat, guard, support |
| Technician | Technical diploma (electrical, electronics, mechanical) | Varies | Maintenance, technical support |
| Military medic | Health degree (medicine, nursing, pharmacy) | Varies | Health services |

## Core admission requirements

These are standard requirements, subject to annual revision — check the enlistment portal before relying on them:

1. **Nationality**: Saudi by origin, with Saudi parents.
2. **Age**: 17-25 for soldiers, varies by track.
3. **Marital status**: usually single for combat tracks.
4. **Clean record**: no criminal history, no drug or hudud offences.
5. **Physical and medical fitness**: height, weight, vision (by track), hearing, general health.
6. **Mental aptitude**: pass a capacity test and personal interview.
7. **No other military affiliation**: cannot apply to two military bodies simultaneously.

## Registration steps

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Follow the official announcement on [tajnid.mod.gov.sa](https://tajnid.mod.gov.sa/) and its X (Twitter) account |
| 2 | Access the portal during the announced application window (closed outside it) |
| 3 | Sign in with Absher — core data pulled automatically |
| 4 | Select rank and track (officer / soldier / technician…) |
| 5 | Upload certificates, ID copy, photo per official specs |
| 6 | Upload General Aptitude and Achievement test results (for students) |
| 7 | Review the summary and submit — save your reference number |

## Post-application stages

After submitting, the applicant passes several filters:

1. **Initial screening**: automated check of requirements. Takes 1-2 weeks.
2. **Medical exam**: at a designated military health centre in your region. Full round including vision, hearing, chest X-ray, blood tests, general assessment.
3. **Physical fitness test**: running, endurance, muscle strength. Standards vary by track.
4. **Personal interview**: before a military committee. Assesses character, awareness, merit.
5. **Security screening**: verification of criminal record and reputation.
6. **Final admission decision**: appears in the portal, and by SMS.

Total time from application to admission: **2-4 months** typically.

## Admission of women to MOD

Since 2021, the ministry opened admission to women in specific ranks:

- **Soldier / corporal rank**: internal security services, administrative, medical.
- **Officer rank**: for holders of university degrees in in-demand specialties (medicine, nursing, engineering, Sharia).
- **Available specialties**: vary by seasonal ministry announcement.
- **Benefits**: salary, housing, medical insurance, military pension.
- **Requirements**: similar to men with fitness-test differences.

## Application tips

- **Prepare certificates before applications open**: updates in government systems (Qiyas, Ministry of Education) take time.
- **Photo per official specs**: white background, no glasses, no smile, specific dimensions.
- **Don't apply to multiple concurrent tracks**: your file may be cancelled.
- **Follow official announcements only**: unofficial job sites sometimes publish misleading info.
- **Prepare fitness months in advance**: most candidates are eliminated here.

## Common mistakes

- **Uploading uncertified documents**: certificates not authenticated by the issuing authority.
- **Showing up for medical exam not in top shape**: sleep well, no caffeine before the test.
- **Lying in the interview**: uncovered by security screening and your file is permanently cancelled.
- **Relying on middlemen**: no official middlemen exist; they are all scams.

## The Five Branches of the Saudi Armed Forces

The Ministry of Defence umbrellas five main branches, each with a sub-admission portal and different career tracks:

| Branch | Practical focus | Notable preparatory academy |
|---|---|---|
| **Royal Saudi Land Forces** | Ground operations, infantry, armour, artillery | [King Abdulaziz Military Academy](https://mod.gov.sa/) in Riyadh |
| **Royal Saudi Air Force** | Combat aviation, transport, drones | [King Faisal Air Academy](https://rsaf.mod.gov.sa/) in Riyadh |
| **Royal Saudi Navy** | Ships, submarines, coastal defence | [King Fahd Naval Academy](https://rsnf.mod.gov.sa/) in Jeddah |
| **Air Defence Forces** | Surface-to-air missiles, radar | Training centre in Riyadh |
| **Strategic Rocket Forces** | Strategic missiles | Details classified for security |

Choice among branches depends on your academic qualification and personal preference. For instance: someone with an aviation-engineering degree is routed to the Air Force; a medical-degree holder to Military Medical Services; a Sharia-degree holder to Military Judiciary.

## Benefits and Rights in the Saudi Military

Before applying, understand what you'll actually receive (source: [Officers Service Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/) and [Enlisted Service Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/)):

- **Salary**: by rank and grade, slightly higher than the civilian equivalent, with steady annual increments.
- **Allowances**: posting allowance, hardship allowance, flight/diving allowances for specialty roles.
- **Housing**: government housing or housing allowance.
- **Health insurance**: full coverage for the serviceperson and family at military hospitals and their network.
- **Early retirement**: after 20 years (enlisted) or 30 years (officers), with full pension entitlement.
- **Scholarship abroad**: graduate study opportunities domestically and internationally at government expense.
- **End-of-service gratuity**: calculated under the military retirement system (different from [GOSI](/en/gosi-pension-guide)).

## MOD vs Other Saudi Security Agencies

Many confuse the Ministry of Defence with the Kingdom's other security bodies. Key distinctions:

| Agency | Responsibility | Application portal |
|---|---|---|
| **Ministry of Defence (MOD)** | Regular armed forces | [tajnid.mod.gov.sa](https://tajnid.mod.gov.sa/) |
| **[Ministry of Interior (MOI)](/en/saudi-moi-security-registration-guide)** | Police, Passports, Civil Defence, Border Guard | mabmob.moi.gov.sa |
| **National Guard** | Internal security, rapid response | sang.gov.sa |
| **Presidency of State Security** | Counter-terrorism and intelligence | gis.gov.sa |
| **Royal Guard** | Leadership protection | Special internal track |

Do not apply to more than one agency in the same period — it counts as a violation that may disqualify you from all.
