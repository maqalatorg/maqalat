Nusuk is the Saudi Ministry of Hajj and Umrah's unified portal for everything related to performing the rites: Haramain entry permits, Hajj, Umrah, services in Madinah, and overseas-pilgrim booking. Official URL: [nusuk.sa](https://www.nusuk.sa/).

Before 2018 these services were scattered across multiple platforms (Eatmarna, Tasreeh, Smart Hajj). In 2022 the ministry unified everything under "Nusuk," and the **digital permit became a requirement** to enter the Grand Mosque courtyards during peak seasons (Ramadan, Hajj) — not only for performing Umrah.

## Who needs a Nusuk permit?

| User | Permit type |
|---|---|
| Saudi or resident performing Umrah | Umrah permit |
| Saudi or resident praying at the Grand Mosque (e.g., Ramadan) | Prayer / visit permit |
| Saudi or resident performing Hajj | Hajj permit (after the lottery) |
| Pilgrim from outside the Kingdom | Umrah visa + digital permit |
| Visitor on a tourist visa wanting Umrah | Umrah permit on the tourist visa |

## Umrah permit for citizens and residents

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Open [nusuk.sa](https://www.nusuk.sa/) or download the Nusuk app |
| 2 | Sign in with National Single Sign-On or Absher |
| 3 | Choose "Umrah" → "Book Permit" |
| 4 | Set the date and time (typically 2-hour windows) |
| 5 | Add companions (must be linked in Absher or registered with their IDs) |
| 6 | Review and confirm the booking |
| 7 | Show the QR code from the app at the mosque gate |

**Key rules**:
- The permit is time-bound; being late invalidates it.
- Missing the appointment twice in a row temporarily suspends the account.
- The permit is per person; not transferable.

## Hajj registration for Saudis and residents

Hajj requires a separate permit on a different path:

- **Registration window**: announced annually about two months before Hajj (typically Shawwal-Dhu al-Qi'dah).
- **Selection**: by lottery or priority (first-time pilgrims, older pilgrims, etc.).
- **Packages**: 6 tiers from economy to premium — chosen before the lottery.
- **Payment**: after acceptance, to the chosen Hajj service company.
- **Priority**: those who have never performed Hajj, then those who performed it more than 5 years ago.

## Booking for overseas pilgrims

The fastest path to perform Umrah from abroad — and the most significant recent development:

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Visit [nusuk.sa/en](https://www.nusuk.sa/) and pick your language |
| 2 | Register with your passport (Saudi / non-Saudi / other-country resident) |
| 3 | Browse packages — all from accredited Saudi companies |
| 4 | Each package includes: Umrah visa, flight (optional), hotel, transport, meals, guide |
| 5 | Pay by card or bank transfer |
| 6 | Receive the e-visa by email in days |

**Warning**: some overseas Umrah agencies advertise fake packages or issue visas in your name without your knowledge. **Use only Nusuk-registered companies or agencies certified by your country's Ministry of Hajj and Umrah**.

## Umrah visa vs tourist visa

Since 2019, Saudi Arabia has opened a tourist visitor visa to a wide list of countries (49 countries e-visa initially, then more). For a Muslim, this visa **allows performing Umrah** without needing a specific Umrah visa.

| Criterion | Umrah visa | Tourist visa |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | 90 days | 90 days (multi-entry annually) |
| Allowed areas | Makkah, Madinah, Jeddah (transit) | All Kingdom (Makkah and Madinah for Muslims only) |
| For Hajj | No | No |
| For Umrah | Yes (its original purpose) | Yes for Muslims |
| Fees | Vary by package | ~100-130 USD |
| Applicant | Accredited Umrah company | Directly from government |

## Common mistakes

- **Peak-time booking without confirming companions**: some families book, then it's auto-cancelled because someone has a conflicting permit.
- **Overstaying the visa**: risk of deportation and 5-year ban.
- **Buying from unofficial sites**: many sites impersonate Nusuk — verify SSL and the official URL.
- **Missing your permit slot**: 30 minutes late invalidates it.
- **Donating your permit to another person**: the system uses face recognition (cameras) — both parties are penalised.

## Tips especially for Saudis

- **Book early in Ramadan**: permits sell out in minutes for the last ten nights.
- **Last ten nights are the hardest season**: you may need a permit even for regular prayer.
- **Grand Mosque is sometimes closed to non-permit holders**: don't enter without one, especially during peak tawaf hours.
- **Download the QR offline before entry**: the mosque network is congested.
