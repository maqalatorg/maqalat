Nafath is Saudi Arabia's official single sign-on system for government services — connecting to platforms like Qiwa, Sakani, Citizen Account, Social Security, Sehhaty, Najiz, Nusuk, and 530+ other government and private platforms. It's operated by the **National Information Center (NIC)** under [SDAIA](https://sdaia.gov.sa/) in cooperation with the Ministry of Interior. URL: [nafath.sa](https://www.nafath.sa/) or the "Nafath" app.

## Why Nafath exists

Before Nafath, each government platform had its own account:
- One account in Qiwa, one in Sakani, one in GOSI, one in Education…
- A separate password for each, sometimes with different data.
- Forgetting one meant complex re-registration.

Nafath solved this by making **Absher data** (tied to your national ID, biometrics, and photo) the single **source of truth**. Any platform that needs to confirm you are "you" asks via Nafath, which asks Absher and confirms or denies.

## How Nafath actually works

Take signing in to Qiwa as an example:

1. You open qiwa.sa and click "Sign In."
2. Nafath screen appears.
3. You enter your ID number.
4. **A notification appears in the Nafath app on your phone** with a random number (e.g., "47").
5. **The same number is shown on the Qiwa screen**.
6. If they match, you press "Approve" in the app.
7. Qiwa opens — you're in.

The random number prevents fraud — no one can trick you into signing in to a fake site because the number won't match.

## First-time app activation

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Download "Nafath" from App Store or Google Play |
| 2 | Open the app and choose "Activate Account" |
| 3 | Enter your ID number |
| 4 | Enter the OTP sent to your Absher-registered mobile |
| 5 | Set a PIN for additional protection (4 digits) |
| 6 | Enable face or fingerprint for quick sign-in |
| 7 | App is ready — home screen shows "Activated" |

## Signing in to any service via Nafath

After activation, signing in to any government platform is easier than getting coffee:

1. Go to the platform's site (Qiwa, Sakani, Sehhaty…) and choose "Sign in with Nafath."
2. Enter your ID number.
3. A number appears on the screen — look at your phone.
4. Notification in Nafath app with a matching number.
5. Press "Approve" + face or PIN.
6. The platform opens — no password per platform needed.

## Alternatives to the app

What if you don't want the Nafath app?

- **SMS**: the system sends a verification code from a short number. Enter it in the platform.
- **Automated call**: the system calls your mobile and states the verification code.

Both alternatives work, but SMS is less secure (can be intercepted in rare cases). The app remains the best.

## Common sign-in issues

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Alert didn't arrive" | Absher mobile differs from app mobile | Update Absher mobile to match the app mobile |
| "Number doesn't match" | You're trying to sign in to a fake site | Close the page immediately, go to the official platform site |
| "Request expired" | You delayed pressing "Approve" | Response window is short — restart the sign-in |
| "Account temporarily locked" | Consecutive failed attempts | Auto-unlocks after a short cooldown, or re-activate from nafath.sa |
| App opens and shows nothing | Needs update | Update from the app store |

## Nafath for residents

Residents (iqama holders) use Nafath the same way, provided their data is correct in Absher (iqama number, birthdate, photo, mobile). Residents can sign in to: Qiwa, Musaned, Sehhaty, Najiz, etc. Some services are Saudi-only (Sakani, Citizen Account) regardless of Nafath.

## Nafath Business (for establishments)

A separate version for establishments, letting a company's authorised representative sign in to business services: Qiwa (as an employer), Absher Business, ZATCA tax platform, and more. Activation requires linking the commercial registration to the representative's ID.

## Nafath security

- **Never share your PIN** with anyone.
- **Never approve a sign-in you didn't initiate**: a common scam. If a notification arrives and you weren't signing in, reject it.
- **Keep the app updated**: updates patch vulnerabilities.
- **If you lose your phone**: from the Nafath website via browser, deactivate, then activate on a new phone.

## Nafath in the Saudi Digital Identity Stack

Nafath doesn't hang alone in space — it's three interconnected layers forming your complete digital identity in the Kingdom:

| Layer | Role | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| **[Absher](https://www.absher.sa/)** | Source of original data | Identity, fingerprint, photo, registered mobile |
| **[Nafath](https://www.nafath.sa/)** | Unified sign-on bridge | Verifies you own the identity via your app |
| **[Tawakkalna](/en/tawakkalna-complete-guide)** | Visible digital identity | Digital version of the national ID legally accepted |

The practical difference: **Absher** stores your data, **Nafath** uses it for verification, **Tawakkalna** displays it to the other party (police, airport, bank). You don't choose "the best" — you need all three together, but Nafath is the most-used daily because it's the gateway to any digital platform.

## Nafath and the Official Electronic Signature

Saudi electronic transactions run under the **Electronic Transactions Law** issued by Royal Decree **(M/18) dated 8/3/1428 AH**, which grants the electronic signature legal validity equivalent to a handwritten signature when identity-verification requirements are met. Nafath provides this verification layer in practice when signing official documents on government platforms such as Najiz, Qiwa, and ZATCA.

Reference: [Electronic Transactions Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/6f509360-2c39-4358-ae2a-a9a700f2ed16/1) on the Bureau of Experts portal.
