## Salary Payment Rules in Saudi Arabia

Each government body follows a fixed date on a specific calendar (Hijri or Gregorian), with a single unified rule for handling the weekend.

### 1. Government Employee Salary

| Criterion | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 27th of every Hijri month |
| **Reference calendar** | Umm Al-Qura (official Hijri) |
| **Paying entity** | Ministry of Finance, via bank transfer |
| **Weekend rule** | If the 27th falls on Friday or Saturday, the deposit moves to Thursday |
| **Notification channel** | SMS from your bank on deposit |

**Source of the rule**: the Civil Service Law and the Ministry of Finance circular.

### 2. Pension Salaries (Public Pension Agency)

Same date as the government employee salary — **27th Hijri** — because the Public Pension Agency runs on the same disbursement cycle. This covers:

- Civilian retirees
- Military retirees
- Eligible heirs of retirees

### 3. Citizen Account

| Criterion | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 10th of every Gregorian month |
| **Reference calendar** | Gregorian |
| **Paying entity** | Ministry of Human Resources via the Citizen Account Program |
| **Weekend rule** | If the 10th falls on Friday or Saturday, the deposit moves to Thursday |
| **Official link** | [ca.gov.sa](https://www.ca.gov.sa) |

### 4. Developed Social Security

| Criterion | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 1st of every Gregorian month |
| **Reference calendar** | Gregorian |
| **Paying entity** | Ministry of Human Resources — Developed Social Security system |
| **Weekend rule** | If the 1st falls on Friday or Saturday, the deposit moves to Thursday |
| **Key condition** | Meeting the monthly eligibility criteria |

### 5. Legacy Social Security Pension

Paid on the **8th of every Hijri month** for beneficiaries who stayed on the legacy system before voluntarily migrating to Developed Social Security.

### 6. Sanad (unemployment insurance)

| Criterion | Details |
|---|---|
| **Date** | 27th of every Hijri month (same cycle as government salaries) |
| **Reference calendar** | Hijri |
| **Paying entity** | General Organization for Social Insurance (GOSI) |
| **Weekend rule** | If the 27th falls on Friday or Saturday, the deposit moves to Thursday |
| **Key condition** | Job loss plus a sufficient prior GOSI contribution history |
| **Official link** | [gosi.gov.sa](https://www.gosi.gov.sa) |

**Note**: Sanad compensation runs for a maximum of 12 months and decreases gradually over time. To check your eligibility and calculated amount, log in to the GOSI portal.

**Important note**: Hijri-based salaries "advance" against the Gregorian calendar by about 11 days per year, because the Hijri year is roughly 11 days shorter than the Gregorian one. Gregorian-based support, on the other hand, stays fixed on the same day of every Gregorian month.

## When Are Salaries Actually Deposited?

Deposits usually start **after midnight** on the scheduled day, but when they show up in your account depends on:

1. **The bank**: some banks reflect transfers instantly, others take hours.
2. **Batch size**: banks process deposits in batches; people at the tail of the list receive their funds hours later.
3. **Receiving channel**: a direct bank transfer is faster than alternative routes.

**Practical tip**: turn on SMS notifications from your bank so you're alerted the moment the deposit lands, instead of refreshing the app.

## Additional FAQ

**Can you request your salary early?**
No. The government follows a unified schedule that only shifts due to the official weekend. For urgent needs, some banks offer a "salary advance" under certain terms.

**What if my salary is late?**
Check your bank account and app first. If 24 hours pass, contact your employer, HR, or your bank's customer service.

**Does the weekend early-deposit rule apply to every salary?**
Yes — for government salaries, the Citizen Account, and Developed Social Security. The private sector follows each company's own rules.

**How do I know my salary date if I work in the private sector?**
Check your contract. Some companies pay at the end of every Gregorian month, others on the 7th or 10th. There is no unified rule for the private sector.
