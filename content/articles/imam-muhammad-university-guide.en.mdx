Imam Muhammad ibn Saud Islamic University is **Saudi Arabia's most established academic authority in Sharia and Islamic studies**. It began as a religious institute in 1950, was elevated to a university in 1974, and today serves 60,000+ students across **14 colleges**، including Medicine, Engineering, and Computer Science alongside its historical Islamic core ([Wikipedia، IMSIU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Muhammad_ibn_Saud_Islamic_University)).

## Quick Profile

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Location** | Riyadh (main campus) + affiliated institutes |
| **Founded** | 1950 (institute) → 1974 (university) |
| **Character** | Comprehensive Islamic university، Sharia origins, expanded into medicine, engineering, computing |
| **Student body** | 60,000+ |
| **Type** | Public، free for Saudis with monthly stipend |
| **International presence** | 70+ institutes in Saudi Arabia + institutes in Indonesia and Djibouti |

## Global Ranking

| Ranking | Position | Year |
|---|---|---|
| QS World University Rankings | 791–800 | 2027 |

**Direct read**: International ranking scales heavily emphasise English-language scientific publication output. Universities specialised in Sharia, Islamic, and Arabic studies naturally rank lower on these measures، but IMSIU's **Arab-Islamic scholarly authority** does not map to QS metrics.

## Colleges and Programs

Fourteen colleges across three sectors:

### Sharia and Humanities Sector (Historical Core)
- **Sharia**، fiqh, usul al-fiqh, Sharia policy, judiciary
- **Usul al-Din (Fundamentals of Religion)**، creed, hadith, tafsir, contemporary religions
- **Arabic Language**، grammar, morphology, literature, rhetoric, linguistics
- **Da'wah and Media** (Communication)
- **Social Sciences**، sociology, social work, psychology
- **Languages and Translation**
- **Education**
- **Sharia and Islamic Studies (Al-Ahsa branch)**

### STEM Sector
- **Medicine**، relatively new, with a teaching hospital
- **Engineering**، civil, electrical, mechanical, computer
- **Computer and Information Sciences**
- **Sciences**، physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics

### Business Sector
- **Economics and Administrative Sciences**، with a distinct focus on **Islamic Economics** and Sharia-compliant banking

### Continuing Education
- **Continuing Education and Community Service**

**Notable research institutes**:
- **Institute of Translation and Arabization**، leading Arab center for knowledge transfer into Arabic
- **Institute of Arabic Language for Non-Native Speakers**، attracts Arabic learners globally
- **Prince Sultan Library** (founded 1952)، a **World Digital Library partner**

**Degrees available**: Bachelor's + Master's + PhD for most programs, under the Deanship of Graduate Studies.

## Where Graduates Work

IMSIU graduates follow **substantially different career paths** by discipline:

| Discipline | Primary destination | Secondary paths |
|---|---|---|
| **Sharia** | Sharia judiciary (Ministry of Justice), Public Prosecution | Notary offices, Awqaf, fatwa councils, Board of Grievances |
| **Usul al-Din** | Preaching (Ministry of Islamic Affairs), Senior Scholars Council (academic path) | Religious education, Sharia research, charities |
| **Fiqh** | Fatwa Committees (Permanent Committee, Senior Scholars Council) | Sharia courts, Islamic banking Sharia advisory |
| **Islamic Economics** | Islamic banks (Al Rajhi, Al Bilad, Al Inma) | Sharia supervisory boards, takaful insurance, Sharia-compliant funds |
| **Arabic Language and Literature** | Public education, Ministry of Culture, media | Academic research, translation, publishing |
| **Da'wah and Media** | Religious TV channels, Qur'an radio stations | Media offices at the Ministry of Islamic Affairs, digital Islamic content |
| **Medicine** | Ministry of Health hospitals | Private sector, university hospitals |
| **Engineering and Computer Science** | Standard Saudi employers (Aramco, STC, banks, contractors)، no differentiation from other universities |

**Strategic note**: IMSIU graduates in **Islamic Economics** or **Sharia** hold a **strong competitive advantage** in the Saudi Islamic banking market، a growing sector where licensing requirements favor Sharia credentials from accredited institutions, with IMSIU at the front.

## What Makes IMSIU Different

1. **Arab-Islamic scholarly authority**، unmatched within Saudi Arabia except by Umm Al-Qura.
2. **Islamic Economics**، integrated academic ecosystem (college + institute + research center)، the strongest locally.
3. **Arabic Language for Non-Native Speakers Institute**، draws international students from Indonesia, Malaysia, and Africa.
4. **International presence**، institutes in Indonesia and Djibouti = an international employment network for preachers and scholars.
5. **Modern STEM expansion**، Medicine, Engineering, and Computing were added to offer comprehensive alternatives within one university.

## Before You Apply، Honest Read

**IMSIU fits you if:**
- You want a Sharia academic path with regional and Islamic-world authority
- You aim for the judiciary, fatwa, or Islamic economics
- You want to combine a modern specialty (medicine/engineering/computing) with a conservative environment
- You prefer the Riyadh environment

**IMSIU may not fit you if:**
- Global ranking is your priority (KFUPM/KSU/KAU rank higher)
- Your primary discipline is pure research science or engineering (other universities go deeper)
- You want a coeducational environment (IMSIU is traditional in this respect)

## Strong Alternatives

- **Umm Al-Qura University**، older Sharia authority in Makkah with expanded scientific programs
- **King Saud University**، general disciplines with higher rankings in Riyadh
- **Princess Nourah bint Abdulrahman University**، for women in Riyadh, wide range of disciplines
- **Madinah International University**، private, Sharia programs by distance learning
