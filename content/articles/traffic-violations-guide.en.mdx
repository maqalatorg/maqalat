Traffic violations are the most-used Absher service among Saudi drivers, and the rules have changed several times in recent years (reduced-fees system, point-based penalties, electronic adjudication). This guide covers the current 2026 framework as actually applied.

## Traffic Monitoring Systems in Saudi Arabia

Monitoring runs through three main systems:

| System | Function | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Saher** | Automated electronic capture via fixed and mobile cameras | Speeding, red-light, distraction, wrong-way driving |
| **Traffic patrols** | Field enforcement by traffic officers | Document violations, behavioral, in-progress offenses |
| **Smart-lane radar** | Average-speed capture over full lane sections | Highway average-speed enforcement |

Every violation is logged in the central Traffic system and pushed to Absher within hours; the owner is notified by SMS with the violation number and location.

---

## Reduced-Fees System, 50% Discount

The policy in force since 1444 AH allows a 50% discount on early payment:

- **Within 30 days of the violation date**: only 50% is paid
- **After 30 days**: the full amount is paid
- **After 60 days**: the fine may be doubled depending on the violation type

Serious violations (running a red light with a resulting accident, for example) are not necessarily covered by the discount and follow a separate penalty schedule.

---

## Available Payment Methods

The SADAD electronic system accepts payment via:

- **Bank apps**: enter the violation SADAD bill number directly
- **ATMs**: SADAD menu → traffic violations
- **Internet banking**: "Government Payments" service
- **Personal branch banking** for all Saudi banks

The **SADAD bill number** appears in Absher next to each violation and is copied into the bank app. Within minutes of payment, the violation status updates to "Paid" in Absher.

## How to Appeal a Violation, Step by Step

**Time window**: 30 days from the violation issuance date (not the date you learned of it).

**Appeal process**:

1. Absher → My Services → Traffic → "Appeal a Violation"
2. Select the violation to appeal
3. Choose the appeal reason from the list (capture error, not the driver, emergency, duplicate payment, and others)
4. Attach supporting documents (photos, video, sale-of-vehicle proof, hospital report)
5. The complaint is reviewed by the appeals committee, and the outcome arrives by SMS within 15–21 days

**Scenarios where appeals usually succeed**:

- The vehicle was sold before the violation date with ownership-transfer proof
- The vehicle was in a repair shop (shop certificate)
- Duplicate registration of the same violation (two identical violation numbers)
- Location impossibility (the vehicle was in another city at capture time)

**Scenarios usually rejected**:

- "I didn't see the sign" without evidence
- "I was in a hurry"
- "My speedometer was broken" without a repair certificate

## The Traffic-Points System

Point-based penalties run alongside the monetary fine and are counted against the **driver**:

| Accumulated points | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1–5 points | Warning only |
| 6 points | Official notice |
| 8 points | Mandatory awareness program |
| 12 points | 3-month license suspension |
| 24 points | 6-month suspension |
| 36 points | Permanent license withdrawal + retest from scratch |

Points expire **one full year** after the violation date. There is no way to pay a monetary amount to drop points, but attending the awareness program reduces them.

---

## Common Causes of Duplicate Violations

- **Multi-capture in one instant**: a Saher camera records more than one violation (speed + distraction). Each is issued as a separate bill.
- **True duplication**: rare system error — use the electronic appeal with screenshots of both entries.
- **Owner vs actual driver mismatch**: if you drove a friend's vehicle, the violation lands on the vehicle, then the owner routes it to "Transfer Violation to Actual Driver" on Absher.

## Additional Questions

**Can I pay a large violation in installments?**
There is no official installment plan for traffic fines. Some resort to personal loans or credit-card installment programs, but that is an agreement with the bank, not the Traffic Directorate.

**Do accumulated violations block vehicle registration renewal?**
Yes. Vehicle registration (Istimara) renewal is blocked until all violations recorded against the vehicle are paid, regardless of whether you were the actual driver.

**Can I transfer the violation to the actual driver?**
Yes. Via Absher → Traffic → "Transfer Violation to Actual Driver", within 15 days of issuance. It requires the actual driver's acceptance through Absher as well.
