The Saudi Ministry of Interior (MOI) runs the Kingdom's internal-security system, with 8 main sectors open to Saudi applicants. Each sector is its own track with different requirements, salary scale, and ranks — but the **portal is unified** through Absher, which makes the process much simpler than before 2020.

## Main sectors within MOI

| Sector | Core mission | Entry ranks |
|---|---|---|
| **Public Security (Police)** | Maintain public order, fight crime | Private, Lance Corporal, Corporal |
| **General Directorate of Passports** | Border control, document issuance | Private, Corporal, Sergeant |
| **General Traffic Directorate** | Traffic control, accidents, awareness | Private, Corporal |
| **Civil Defence** | Fires, emergencies, rescue | Private, Civil Defence Technician |
| **Border Guard** | Land and maritime border protection | Naval / Land Private |
| **Diplomatic Security** | VIP and embassy protection | Diplomatic Security Private |
| **Prisons Directorate** | Correctional facilities management | Private, Guard |
| **Facilities Security Forces** | Protection of vital installations | Facilities Security Private |

## Common admission requirements

Standard requirements across all sectors; each sector may add its own:

1. **Nationality**: Saudi by origin with Saudi parents.
2. **Age**: 17-25 typically for new privates, higher for officers.
3. **Qualification**: minimum primary school for privates, high school for corporals, university degree for officers.
4. **Height and weight**: at least 160 cm typically, proportional weight.
5. **Medical fitness**: free from chronic diseases, sound vision or within permitted range, sound hearing.
6. **Clean record**: no criminal history.
7. **Not married to a foreigner** (specific condition for some high-security sectors only).
8. **Pass fitness test and personal interview**.

## Application steps via the unified portal

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| 1 | Follow the ministry's official announcement (on [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa/) and MOI social channels) |
| 2 | Access the portal during the application window — closed outside it |
| 3 | Sign in with Absher (data pulled automatically) |
| 4 | Select the sector and desired rank |
| 5 | Upload certificates, ID copy, photo per specs |
| 6 | Upload General Aptitude or Achievement test result if required |
| 7 | Choose 3 preferred work regions (not guaranteed) |
| 8 | Review the summary and submit — save your reference number |

## Post-application stages

The typical path across all sectors:

1. **Initial screening**: automated eligibility check (1-2 weeks).
2. **Medical exam**: accredited health centre. Includes vision (reading test), hearing, X-ray, blood tests, general exam, initial psych screening.
3. **Fitness test**: 2 km run, endurance, pull-ups. Minimum thresholds announced in advance.
4. **Personal interview**: before the sector committee. Focus on: personality, security awareness, presence, self-confidence.
5. **Security screening**: criminal record and family record check.
6. **Final admission decision**: appears in the portal + SMS.

Total time: **3-6 months** typically.

## Employment benefits

- Base salary by rank + allowances (transport, operational, hazard for some sectors).
- Government housing or housing allowance.
- Military medical insurance.
- Early retirement after 20 years of service (per regulation).
- Regular promotions based on performance and courses.
- External scholarships for some specialties (Passports, Diplomatic Security).

## Application tips

- **Pick one best-fit sector**: applying to multiple sectors dilutes your effort.
- **Prepare fitness months before the announcement**: 2 km run under 12 minutes is a baseline for most sectors.
- **Practice interviews**: many candidates are eliminated by nerves.
- **Photo per official specs**: white background, no glasses, no long beard (by sector).
- **Avoid middlemen**: every unofficial channel is a scam.

## Mistakes that hurt your file

- **Missing documents by deadline**: your chance is cancelled.
- **Missing a scheduled test**: usually not rescheduled for you.
- **Lying in the interview or form**: uncovered by security screening.
- **Name doesn't match Absher**: simple spelling errors stop the application.

## Women's Admission to the Ministry of Interior

Following the Ministry's landmark 2019 announcement, women joined specific tracks that have expanded since:

| Sector | Ranks open to women | Job scope |
|---|---|---|
| **Traffic Police** | Soldier, Corporal, Sergeant | Traffic organisation, women's incidents, awareness |
| **Passports** | Soldier, Sergeant | Women's border checkpoints, document issuance |
| **Public Security** | Soldier, Sergeant | Women's searches, women-focused investigations |
| **Diplomatic Security** | Soldier (specific specialties) | Embassy protection, escorting women VIPs |
| **Women's Prisons** | Soldier, Guard, Officer | Managing women's correctional facilities |

Core requirements match men's, with fitness-test differences (shorter run distance, adapted endurance tests) and interviews tailored to the role. Official source: [Women's sector portal in Absher](https://www.absher.sa/).

## The MOI Rank Ladder

Promotion within the Ministry follows a clear rank ladder, each with a minimum tenure before promotion, based on performance and courses:

| Rank | Minimum promotion tenure | General level |
|---|---|---|
| Soldier | 4 years | Entry |
| Corporal | 4 years | Entry advanced |
| Sergeant | 4 years | NCO |
| Staff Sergeant | 5 years | Mid |
| First Sergeant | 5 years | Mid advanced |
| Master Sergeant | 6 years | Supervisor |
| Lieutenant | Track fork (academy) | Officer |
| First Lieutenant → Captain → Major → Colonel → Brigadier General → Major General → Lieutenant General | 4-6 years per rank | Officer |

Promotion from Master Sergeant to Lieutenant requires completing an **Officer Qualification Course** and entering the officer track after leave of absence. Sources: [Enlisted Military Service Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/) and [Officers Service Law](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/).

## MOI vs MOD — Practical Comparison

The choice between the two depends on your professional preferences, not on "which is better":

| Factor | Ministry of Interior | Ministry of Defence |
|---|---|---|
| Your mission | Internal security, daily citizen interaction | Border protection, military readiness |
| Work location | Saudi cities | Military bases, sometimes remote |
| Lifestyle | Closer to civilian life pattern | Stricter military regime |
| Training type | Primarily security (3-6 months) | Military tactical (6-12 months) |
| Most common women's track | Diverse (traffic, passports, security) | Limited (medical, administrative, women's-security) |
