Iqama renewal is a recurring procedure for every resident in Saudi Arabia and it triggers immediate penalties on delay. This guide walks through the current process via Absher and Muqeem, explains the independent fee components, and clarifies the deadlines you must observe.

## Where Iqama Is Renewed Officially

There are three issuing channels:

| Channel | Nature | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Absher Individuals** | Online self-service | Employer renews their worker's iqama |
| **Muqeem platform** | Corporate portal | Establishments managing many workers |
| **Jawazat offices** | In-person | Edge cases the online systems don't handle |

The default route is online through [absher.sa](https://www.absher.sa) or [muqeem.sa](https://www.muqeem.sa). Office visits are no longer required for most cases since Passport Directorate services digitized.

---

## Absher Steps (for the Employer)

Steps as of the 2026 Absher interface:

1. Log in to **Absher Individuals** with the employer's account.
2. Go to **My Services → Government Services → Passports**.
3. Select **"Iqama Renewal"** and enter the dependent's iqama number.
4. Choose the renewal duration (a full year; some cases permit six months).
5. Review the fee line — the screen shows the total due.
6. Pay via **SADAD** using the displayed bill number.
7. Within 24 working hours of payment, the system issues the new digital iqama automatically.

**Note**: the annual Labor Office fee and monthly dependents' fees must already be settled before you start; otherwise the renewal option will not appear.

## Fee Components — Three Independent Amounts

Many sources conflate these three, leaving workers to believe there is a single fee. The system distinguishes:

### 1. Annual Iqama Renewal Fee

Paid once a year for every adult resident. Covers issuing the new iqama for the following year. The exact figure is displayed on the Absher payment screen because it is set by executive Passport Directorate rules that occasionally update.

### 2. Dependents' Fee (Expat Levy)

Paid **monthly** for each family member on the worker's sponsorship (spouse, children) — not on the employer. It can be paid cumulatively before renewal or on a monthly basis via Absher. Unpaid balances block the renewal and disable many services.

### 3. Annual Labor Office Fee (Foreign Workforce Fee)

Paid by the **employer** annually for each non-Saudi worker (calculated against the establishment's Saudization rate). Rates differ between small and large establishments. It cannot be charged to the worker under Article 40 of the Labor Law.

**For current exact values**, open [Absher → Passports → Iqama Renewal](https://www.absher.sa) — the system shows the actual amount due when you select the iqama in question.

## Official Penalty Schedule for Delays

These fines are listed in the **Jawazat approved penalty schedule**:

| Offense | First fine | Second fine | Third case |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late iqama renewal | 500 SAR | 1,000 SAR + deportation | Deportation + re-entry ban |
| Not carrying iqama in public | 1,000 SAR | 2,000 SAR | — |
| Employing a worker with expired iqama | 5,000 SAR per worker | Doubled + closure | — |

The fine begins the day after expiry — neither the worker nor the employer gets any grace period.

## Most Common Errors and Fixes

- **"Payment made but renewal not showing"**: the system waits for the interbank settlement cycle (up to 24 working hours). After that, call Absher support on 19091.
- **"No bill number"**: usually because the dependents' fee or Labor Office fee is not fully settled. Pay them first and retry.
- **"Renewal won't proceed at all"**: check the iqama isn't already expired — once it is, the electronic path closes and you must visit a Jawazat office.
- **"Passport expires before the iqama year ends"**: the system requires passport validity to exceed the iqama duration. Renew the passport first at your country's consulate.

## When to Visit a Jawazat Office Directly

Statutory exceptions requiring an in-person visit:

- The iqama is fully expired and the online system no longer displays it
- Data corrections (profession, name, birth date)
- Reissuing a lost or damaged iqama
- First iqama issuance after arrival to the Kingdom

Office locations in major cities are published on the [Jawazat website](https://www.gdp.gov.sa). Business hours are Sunday–Thursday, 7:30 AM to 2:30 PM in most branches.

## Additional Questions

**Can I renew if I have unpaid traffic fines?**
Traffic fines don't block iqama renewal, but an escape ("hurub") report or a general government hold does. Check "Iqama Status" in Absher before applying.

**Does yearly renewal require a new medical test?**
No. The medical exam is required only for the first iqama, not for annual renewal.

**What if the worker's profession changes during the year?**
A profession change requires a separate application via Muqeem/Absher with Ministry approval and its own fee, but does not trigger a fresh iqama renewal if the current one is still valid.
