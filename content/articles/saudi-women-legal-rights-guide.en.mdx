This is a regulatory reference to the legal rights of Saudi women under current laws in force. **It relies on legal and procedural texts**, and does not address debates beyond them. For each right, the legal source, competent authority, and practical procedure are cited.

## Identity and personal documents

### National ID card

- **Right**: every Saudi adult woman (15+) obtains her own ID card.
- **Source**: Civil Affairs Regulations.
- **Procedure**: via Absher → Civil Affairs → "New ID Request" or visit a branch.
- **No third-party consent** required above 15.

### Passport

- **Right**: a Saudi woman over 21 obtains and renews her passport directly.
- **Procedure**: via Absher → Passports → "New Passport Request."
- **Under 21**: with guardian's consent.
- **Travel abroad**: no consent required above 21.

### Family register

- **Right**: divorced, widowed, and independent women obtain an independent family register.
- **Procedure**: via Absher or Civil Affairs.
- **Adding children**: per custody ruling.

## Independence and housing

### Independent national address

- **Right**: an adult woman obtains an independent national address without consent.
- **Procedure**: via [Saudi Post (SPL Online)](https://splonline.com.sa/).

### Renting and ownership

- **Right**: documenting a rental contract in [Ejar](/en/ejar-rental-contract-guide) in her name.
- **Right**: purchasing property and documenting the deed in her name via [Najiz](/en/najiz-services-guide).
- **Right**: registering with [Sakani](/en/sakani-housing-guide) as an independent head of household (divorced, widowed, independent).

### Utilities (electricity, water, telecom)

- **Right**: service contract in her personal name without a sponsor.
- **Procedure**: directly via provider platforms.

## Work and employment

### Right to work

- **Source**: Saudi Labour Law.
- **Right**: work in public and private sectors.
- **Right to choose**: type of work, location, and conditions without guardian consent.

### Equal pay

- **Source**: Labour Law, Article 3.
- **Right**: equal pay to men for the same work.
- **Enforcement**: complaint via [Qiwa](/en/qiwa-platform-guide) → "Labour Complaint."

### Maternity leave

- **Right**: 10 weeks (4 before birth + 6 after).
- **Pay**: full if she has one year of service, half if less.
- **Protection**: dismissal during pregnancy or maternity leave is prohibited.
- **If dismissed**: complaint on Qiwa, plus at least 60 days' extra wages as compensation.

### Nursing hour

- **Right**: one hour daily for a full year post-birth at full pay.
- **Use**: start of shift, end, or split.

### Workplace harassment

- **Source**: Anti-Harassment Law (1442 AH).
- **Right**: immediate report.
- **Procedure**: via Public Prosecution (990) or police or a Qiwa complaint.
- **Penalty for perpetrator**: imprisonment up to 5 years and a fine up to 300,000 SAR.

## Personal status

### Marriage

- **Right**: contract requires the woman's explicit consent — without it the contract is void.
- **Minimum age**: legally 18; below requires justified court authorisation.
- **Electronic contract**: via [Najiz](/en/najiz-services-guide) → Marriage Documentation.
- **Right to conditions**: adding contract conditions (completing studies, work, independent residence).

### Dowry (Mahr)

- **Right**: setting and receiving the dowry.
- **Protection**: non-payment of the dowry is a contract defect, and she may seek annulment.

### Financial rights during marriage

- **Right**: full nafaqah (housing, food, clothing, medical care).
- **Right**: no interference with her private funds.
- **Right**: to work and keep her income.

### Divorce

- **Right**: petition for divorce in court via [Najiz](/en/najiz-lawsuit-hearing-guide).
- **Accepted grounds**: harm, prolonged absence, non-support, and others.
- **Khul'**: right to end the marriage by relinquishing part or all of the dowry.
- **Electronic notification**: both parties notified upon divorce documentation.

### Custody

- **Default**: mother has custody until 15.
- **After 15**: the child chooses between parents.
- **Right**: mother retains custody even upon remarriage to a mahram for the child.
- **Disputes**: resolved in Personal Status Court in the child's best interest.

### Alimony after divorce

- **Iddah alimony**: for the divorced woman during the legal Iddah period.
- **Child alimony**: on the father until adulthood (daughter until marriage).
- **Non-payment**: complaint in Execution Court via Najiz, with the right to travel bans and bank freezes for the non-payer.

### Inheritance

- **Right**: the share prescribed by Shariah in relatives' estates.
- **Procedure**: via [Najiz](/en/najiz-services-guide) → Inheritance Determination.
- **Protection**: cannot be denied her share, and denial is a criminal offence litigated in court.

## Litigation and judicial protection

### Direct litigation

- **Right**: filing a lawsuit personally without consent or guardian.
- **Procedure**: via [Najiz](/en/najiz-lawsuit-hearing-guide).

### Legal counsel

- **Right**: engaging a lawyer of her choice.
- **Women in law**: legally licensed to represent clients in courts.

### Protection from harm

- **Source**: Protection from Harm Law (1434 AH).
- **Right**: filing a harm complaint via 1919 or protection shelters.
- **Procedures**: urgent protection orders, prosecution of perpetrators.
- **Social care role**: shelter and protection.

## Financial and commercial rights

### Commercial registration and business

- **Right**: opening a commercial registration in her name and starting a business.
- **Procedure**: via [Monshaat](https://www.monshaat.gov.sa/) or the Ministry of Commerce.
- **No consent**: since 2019, no third-party consent needed.

### Bank accounts

- **Right**: opening a bank account in her personal name at any bank.
- **Full control**: without a spouse or guardian's consent.
- **Credit cards**: per her financial eligibility.

### Legal signature

- **Right**: signing civil and commercial contracts personally.
- **Legal recognition**: her signature is binding as any adult person.

## Health rights

### Independent treatment

- **Right**: receiving medical care without any party's consent.
- **Exception**: major surgery for a child needs the guardian's consent.

### Health decision-making

- **Right**: accepting or refusing treatment herself.
- **Right**: accessing her health file via [Sehhaty](/en/sehhaty-app-complete-guide).

## Educational rights

### Education at all levels

- **Right**: enrolling in all educational levels without consent after adulthood.
- **Scholarships**: external scholarship tracks available with equal conditions.
- **Choice of specialty**: unrestricted.

## Political and administrative rights

### Voting in municipal elections

- **Right**: since 2015 in municipal elections.

### Assuming senior positions

- **Right**: holding ministries, embassies, agency leadership, and the judiciary (per current judicial authority regulations).
- **Shura Council**: seats designated for women.

## Steps when a right is violated

**Order of recourse when a legal right is violated**:

1. **Amicable resolution** first if possible.
2. **Sector regulator** (Ministry of Labour for work, Ministry of Justice for personal status…).
3. **Complaint on the government "Tawasul" platform**.
4. **Filing a lawsuit** in the competent court via [Najiz](/en/najiz-lawsuit-hearing-guide).
5. **[Royal Court petition](/en/royal-court-petition-guide)** as a last resort for humanitarian cases.
