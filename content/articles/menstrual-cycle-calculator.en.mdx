## How the calculator works

The math rests on three established medical rules:

### 1. Predicting the next period

**Next period = date of last period + cycle length (in days)**

A normal cycle runs 21 to 35 days. The most common length is 28 days. Track your cycle across 3 months to find your personal average.

### 2. Calculating ovulation day

**Ovulation day = start of next period − 14 days**

This rule is more accurate than "14 days after the current period starts" because the post-ovulation phase is fixed at 14 days, while the pre-ovulation phase varies.

**Examples**:
- 28-day cycle → ovulation on day 14
- 30-day cycle → ovulation on day 16
- 25-day cycle → ovulation on day 11

### 3. The fertility window

**Fertility window = 5 days before ovulation + ovulation day**

The science: sperm survive inside the female reproductive tract for up to 5 days, while the egg lives only 24 hours after ovulation. Intercourse several days before ovulation can therefore result in pregnancy.

Source: [ACOG — Fertility Awareness](https://www.acog.org).

## The four phases of the menstrual cycle

| Phase | Days (28-day cycle) | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Menstrual** | 1–5 | Uterine lining sheds; hormone levels drop |
| **Follicular** | 1–13 | Egg matures inside the ovary; estrogen rises |
| **Ovulation** | 14 | Egg is released from the ovary into the fallopian tube |
| **Luteal** | 15–28 | Progesterone secreted; the uterus prepares for pregnancy or menstruation |

At the end of the luteal phase:
- **If pregnancy occurs**: progesterone stays elevated and menstruation is skipped.
- **If no pregnancy**: progesterone falls and the period begins.

## Natural signs of ovulation

You can spot ovulation through:

1. **Change in cervical mucus**: becomes clear and stretchy (like egg white).
2. **Rise in basal body temperature (BBT)**: increases roughly 0.5°C after ovulation.
3. **Mild pain on one side of the abdomen**: known as "Mittelschmerz".
4. **Increased libido**: a natural effect of the LH hormone.
5. **Slight breast tenderness**.
6. **Home ovulation test**: detects the LH surge 24–36 hours before ovulation.

## When is a cycle "abnormal"?

See a gynecologist if:

- **Missed periods for 3 consecutive months** (without pregnancy)
- **Cycle length varies by more than 7–9 days** month to month
- **Heavy bleeding** requiring a pad change every hour
- **Severe pain** unrelieved by standard pain relievers
- **Bleeding between periods** on a recurring basis
- **Cycles consistently longer than 35 days**
- **Cycles consistently shorter than 21 days**

These signs may point to polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), thyroid disorders, hormonal imbalances, or other conditions requiring diagnosis.

## Can pregnancy happen outside the fertility window?

Theoretically: very rarely. Practically: yes, in these situations:

- **Irregular cycles** — ovulation may occur on an unexpected day
- **Early or late ovulation** caused by stress or medications
- **Double ovulation** (two eggs released in one cycle)

**Bottom line**: the fertility window is a planning indicator, not a full guarantee.

## Planning for pregnancy

For those trying to conceive:
1. **Identify your fertility window** with the calculator above or a home LH test.
2. **Intercourse every other day** during this window raises the odds.
3. **Take folic acid** (400 micrograms daily) for 3 months before conception — it prevents neural tube defects.
4. **Consult your doctor** before planning to rule out any barriers.

Source: [CDC — Planning for Pregnancy](https://www.cdc.gov).

## To avoid pregnancy

**Do not rely on cycle tracking alone**. Documented safe methods:
- Hormonal pills, patches, or injections
- Intrauterine device (copper or hormonal)
- Male condom (protects against pregnancy and sexually transmitted infections)
- Vaginal ring

Each method has its own success rate and specific fit — see a gynecologist to choose what suits you best.
