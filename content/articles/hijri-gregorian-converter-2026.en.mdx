## The core difference between the two calendars

| Criterion | Hijri calendar | Gregorian calendar |
|---|---|---|
| Basis | Moon's cycle around Earth | Earth's orbit around the sun |
| Days per year | ~354 days | 365 (or 366 in a leap year) |
| Start of the year | 1 Muharram | 1 January |
| Epoch | Migration of the Prophet ﷺ (622 CE) | Birth of Christ |
| Start of the day | At sunset | At midnight |
| Fixed seasonal months | No، months rotate through the year | Yes، each month stays in its season |

## Quick conversion using the approximate formula

To convert between years only (without day and month):

- Hijri → Gregorian: `G ≈ H × 0.97 + 622`
- Gregorian → Hijri: `H ≈ (G − 622) × 1.03`

**Example**: 1447 AH ≈ 1447 × 0.97 + 622 ≈ **2025/2026 CE**

> **Note**: This formula is approximate and only for the year. For an exact date (day and month), use the tool above.

## Common Mistakes

### 1. Relying on unofficial websites

Some websites use a different calendar than Umm al-Qura, producing a gap of one or two days. For official use، documents, contracts, government appointments، rely only on Umm al-Qura.

### 2. Confusing when the day begins in each calendar

In the Hijri calendar, the day starts at **sunset** (not midnight). That is why "1 Ramadan" effectively appears at maghrib on the preceding Gregorian day in astronomical tables.

### 3. Hijri months are not a fixed length

Each Hijri month is either 29 or 30 days, set by moon sighting in religious practice (and by astronomical calculation in Umm al-Qura). Converting a mid-month date can therefore differ by a day between sources.

## When does precise conversion matter?

- Official contracts: The date is written in the calendar adopted by the issuing authority.
- National ID: The Hijri date of birth is fixed for life، verify its conversion carefully.
- Medical appointments: Some hospitals schedule appointments in Hijri; verify before booking.
- Exam dates (Qudurat / Tahsili): The Education & Training Evaluation Commission publishes dates in both calendars.
